Vocations Monstrance (Blessed by Pope
Leo) in the Diocese of Raphoe from 21st to
4th July
The “Vocations Monstrance” is part of the
Church’s ongoing commitment to promote
and foster vocations to the priesthood and
to religious life. The monstrance will be in
various churches in the Diocese to
encourage prayer for vocations and to
deepen young p eople’s awareness of God’s
call in their lives. Through prayer before the
Blessed Sacrament, we ask the Lord to raise
up generous and joyful servants for His
Church. Vocations are born in prayer,
sustained by prayer, and strengthened
through prayer. Any pa rish wishing to have
the “Vocations Monstrance” during these 2
weeks, please contact Fr Rory Brady
at vocations@raphoediocese.ie .

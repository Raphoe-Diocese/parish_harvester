Mass at Croaghlin Mass Rock
We gathered on Friday evening 14th August in damp
conditions for our annual celebration of the Feast of
the Assumption of Mary. The Mass was very well
attended, so many people involved in the preparations
and celebrations. Our sincere thanks to everyone. We
were blessed with the presence of the Parish Choir and
also St Catherine ’s Accordion Band, just crowned
All-Ireland Champions for the third time in succession
at Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann in Belfast and celebrating
their 25th anniversary this month.
The Mass Rock site was in great shape thanks to PJ
and Dina next door, Gillen Mc Guinness, Cathal
Murrin & Peadar Gillespie. Our thanks to the Mass
Rock Committee and to James Gavigan, Thomas
Hegarty & Johnny Paul McGuinness who looked after
the flags and the chairs. Patrick Boyle had the holy
water font by the altar and the Mass Rock Plaque in
immaculate condition. And many brought flowers to
adorn the site in honour of Our Lady. Liam Conneely
organised the transport. Our sincere thanks to all
concerned. We ’re grateful also to all the Ministers of
the Eucharist, Readers, Gift Bearers & Collectors.
Special thanks to the Parish Choir & St Catherine ’s
Band. Ann in the Parish Office and Patricia our
Sacristan played a blinder as usual. The refreshments
afterwards were very much appreciated, including the
home baking. Thanks to Ann Marie Leslie & John for
organising Meehan ’s Mobile Kitchen and to Annie,
Dina, Norietta & all the helpers. Thanks to Plunkett
Smith for recording the event. We are grateful to Conal
McGuinness for facilitating us at this idyllic location.
And grateful to Patricia O ’Boyle for looking after
Adoration in the Church.

Thought for the Week
When Jesus asks, “Who do you
say I am?” , he is asking more
than a question of Peter — he
asks it of each one of us. Faith is
not simply knowing about
Jesus; it is knowing him,
trusting him, and allowing him
to shape the way we live.
Perhaps the most important
answer we give to Jesus is not
with our lips, but with our
lives. If you would like to donate

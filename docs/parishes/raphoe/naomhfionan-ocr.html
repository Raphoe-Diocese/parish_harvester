Reflection
In today ’s Gospel we experience Peter firmly identifying Jesus as the “Christ, the Son of the living
God. ” God does not always choose the holiest to govern and to serve in His Church. Our Lord
chose Peter as Chief of the Apostles even though he was subject to many imperfections. Peter,
filled with much zeal, was apt to be impetuous. While he followed our Savior with his whole heart,
he stumbled many times after his initial calling. He boasted that he would never abandon Our Lord.
Yet, he found himself cursing Him and saying that he never knew Him. That pierced our Lord ’s
heart!
Yet, Our Lord did not reject Peter, since He was sure that St. Peter had a strong and constant
determination to correct himself. Peter ’s natural disposition to cater to his feelings and desires
was in part the cause of his lapses. When we experience certain lapses in our on -going conversion, we must not abandon
our quest for holiness. Like Peter, let us have a strong and constant determination to take the measures needed to correct
ourselves. Then we too will receive special favours and blessings on earth and in heaven.
We must not listen to the voice that tells us that our faults are unpardonable. We must say boldly that our God died for all.
No matter how ungodly one is, he or she will find redemption in our Saviour. Let us consider with what patience our divine
Saviour awaits those who reject Him. Then like Peter, we may say, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God, ”
our Redeemer.

The Greatness of Eucharistic Adoration
“Those who stand before the Lord in Eucharistic Adoration are fulfilling an eminent service.
They are presenting to Christ all those who do not know him or are far f rom him; they keep
watch in his presence on their behalf," Pope St. John Paul II
The above quotation from Pope John Paul II speaks of the service we render to others by
presenting them to the Lord during our time of Eucharistic Adoration. The Holy Father
intended by these words to remind people of the immense good their prayers before the
Blessed Sacrament could do for those who right now have little or no knowledge of or
time for God and the things of God. In other words, we can and do win graces for others
who have need of them; graces of conversion, graces that will set them and confirm them
upon their path to God.
During our Holy Hour we sit or kneel in the presence of the Lord Jesus Christ truly present
in the Most Holy Eucharist. We gaze upon that little white host and by faith we declare
that behind the appearance of bread we encounter the face of the Lord of life. And we
bring to him ourselves and those we hold dear – living and deceased.
Only in heaven will we understand the value of our Holy Hour and the good it does our
souls and the souls of so many others. And so, we would encourage you to try some time
of Eucharistic Adoration for yourself. From the middle of September, across our 2
Parishes and 3 Churches we will have Adoration 3 days a week ( Wednesday in Newtown
11am to 8pm ; Thursday in St. Johnston 1pm to 6pm ; & Friday in Killea 2pm to 6pm ).
There is, therefore, ample opportunity to find time to be with Jesus’ face to face in the
Blessed Sacrament. However, these hours are contingent on there being at least 2 people
covering each hour. If you have already taken an hour but would like to change your hour,
or if you would like to be assigned a particular hour then please fill out and return the
leaflets you will find in the porch by next weekend. All hours are available to be taken
but we have particular need of adorers from 3pm to 6pm in St. Johnston, and from 7pm –
8pm in Newtown. New adorers would also be needed for the Killea hours on Friday
afternoon. Entire family groups might consider taking a particular hour – and then at least
two of the group would be present for the hour they have signed up for. Fr Philip Kemmy, PP Fr. Damien Nejad, CC

The 16th Sunday in Ordinary Time focuses on God ’s patience, mercy, and the
growth of His kingdom. The readings show how God gives people time to
change and how His kingdom grows in unexpected ways. Let us pray the
following prayer this week:
Lord Jesus, plant your Word in our hearts and make it fruitful in our lives. Keep
us free from sin and make us a fertile place where your love can grow. Let the
Word which you have planted in our hearts not return to you void, but may it
provide a rich harvest, a hundred, or fifty, or thirty -fold. Amen

the sixth World Day for Grandparents and the Elderly. For many Grandparents and elderly people, this
pilgrimage is one of the most important and joyful days of the year – a day when they feel honoured,
remembered, loved and welcomed.

has been carried out uninterrupted for over 1500 years. The Sacrament of Reconciliation will be available on the

advisories directly on the Westport Parish website or check updates via the Irish Catholic Bishops' Conference.

Prayer for the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary
Almighty and everlasting God,
You have taken up body and soul
into the heavenly glory the Immaculate Virgin Mary,
Mother of Your Son: Grant, we beseech You,
that, ever intent upon heavenly things,
we may be worthy to be partakers of her glory.
Through Jesus Christ Your Son, our Lord,
who lives and reigns with You and the Holy Spirit,
One God, forever and ever. Amen.

DONEGAL GROUP 173 RISE AND SHINE TRUST (formerly Irish Handicapped Children's
Pilgrimage Trust and The Irish Pilgrimage Trust) are celebrating forty years of taking
young people with special needs to Lourdes each Easter. To mark this milestone, Mass
will be celebrated on Saturday 26th September in the Church of the Irish Martyrs, at 12
noon. The Mass will be concelebrated by Bishop Niall Coll and Bishop Donal Mc Keowan,
Bishop of Derry and many of the priests who travelled to Lourdes with us over the years.
We invite all the guests, their families and all the helpers who travelled with us over th e
years to our Mass to celebrate the work of the Donegal Group over these forty years.

World Mission Sunday, 18th October . What is World Mission Sunday?

World Mission Sunday is the Holy Father's annual appeal in support of overseas mission.

It takes place in very parish worldwide where the Church is present.

Who organises World Mission Sunday? Missio Ireland [the Irish branch of Pontifical Mission Societies] coordinates World Mission Sunday.

It is part of a global network, serving the Church in over 1,130 dioceses world wide.

How are funds received on World Mission Sunday dispersed?

All projects go through the local bishops and nuncios in the countries where funds are requested.

They are vetted and checked by the Pontifical Mission Societies in Rome.

Projects are allocated to Ireland to match the amount of funds that are available, once the audit is complete.

This year, €2.6m has been sent out to projects from Missio Ireland.

Why is World Mission Sunday so important?

All donations received on Mission Sunday become part of the Holy Father's Universal Solidarity Fund.

This Fund is essential, not only for missionaries, but for the future of the Church.

It supports missionary activities in over 1,130 mission dioceses, mostly found in Africa, Asia and Latin America.

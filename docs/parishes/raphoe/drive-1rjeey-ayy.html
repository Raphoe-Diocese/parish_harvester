ST. PIUS X ADORATION CHAPEL
Eucharistic Adoration is a devotion whereby members of a parish unite in
making continuous adoration before the exposed Blessed Sacrament, for as
many hours as may be possible, based on the number of people who pledge to
do a weekly holy hour. Eucharistic Adoration is silent and contemplative in
nature.
We in this parish are very blessed that St. Pius X Adoration
Chapel (located in the grounds of Loreto Secondary School) is
open from Sunday 1 pm – Friday 8 pm and that we have
enough people willing to commit an hour each week. Many
people, who are committed to weekly adoration, experience
extra graces and blessings. People who spend time in weekly
adoration are powerful witnesses in their community to the real Presence of
Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament. Are you willing to be that witness?

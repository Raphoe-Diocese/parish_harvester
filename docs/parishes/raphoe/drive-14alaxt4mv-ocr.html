Patrick MacGill Festival, Glenties 23-25 October. Dedicated to revitalising and honouring his life as a writer and to ensure his work remains accessible to new generations, the Patrick MacGill Festival will feature a rich and varied weekend of events, Friday evening (23 October) will feature a screening at the Highlands Hotel of Child of The Dead End (tickets €5/€3), a 2009 drama documentary about MacGill, starring Stephen Rea which will be introduced by the director, Desmond Bell. Saturday (24 October) will begin with a guided walk through the historic landscape of Glenties, followed by two talks at the Highlands Hotel exploring Patrick MacGill’s extraordinary life and times by MacGill scholar José Manuel Pulido and Donegal writer Frank Galligan (tickets €5/€3). On Saturday evening, a gala concert at the Highlands Hotel, featuring a host of local musicians and performers, will provide an unforgettable night of music, drama and poetry, inspired by MacGill's poems and novels (tickets €10/7.50). The festival will conclude with a prize giving ceremony on Sunday (25 October) for winners of the poetry and short story competitions for schools in the area. Tickets for the screening, lectures and Saturday evening’s gala concert can be purchased at Ticket Source: http://ticketsource.com/the-patrick-macgill-festival or on the door.

DIOCESE OF RAPHOE Save the Date 8th September 2026 Dear Choir Director, Singer, Organist, Musician As choirs resume after the Summer break we hope it's a good time to offer some new music to enh ance the Sunday liturgy in our parishes. The Diocesan Church Music Group will hold a seminar for all those involved in music ministry in parishes throughout the diocese on Saturday 10th October in the Mount Errigal Hotel, Letterkenny beginning with registration at 10.00 am. The purpose of this seminar is to offer a new Mass setting which might interest you and provide some general music, with an emphasis this year on Responsorial Psalms. An additional theme of the seminar this year is Music for Weddings and Funerals. We will introduce or reintroduce appropriate music for these important liturgical occasions in the liturgical life of every parish. Whilst this is a specific topic sometimes outside of the remit of parish choirs, all the music we will be offering will be suitable foruseon Sundays and other occasions through the year. The seminar is open to choir directors, all choir members, organists and church musicians. We would warmly welcome also those who sing or play an instrument at weddings and funerals butwhomaynotbe directly associated with music ministry on a regular basis. To that end, anew directory for music appropriate foruseat weddings and funerals will be launched by Bishop Niall Coll at the seminar. It will provide suggestions for music for weddings and funerals as well as giving musicians and singers references for listening to and obtaining music. This is a great opportunity to meet with others who, like yourselves, are working to provide the best possible music in churches throughout the diocese. Your dedication and commitment is deeply appreciated. Yours faithfully Raphoe Diocesan Church Music Group. Any musicians, choir members, funeral or wedding singer etc who are interested in taking part please contact Fr. Boyle at inverparish@gmail.com by Monday 28th of September.

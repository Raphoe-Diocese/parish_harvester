Aged 65 or over?
The free flu vaccine will be available in Glenties
Primary Care Centre on Friday 25th September
from 10am – 12:30pm for those aged 65 year or
over ONLY. No appointment is necessary for this
clinic however you must be registered with
Dr Evelyn McManus or Dr Cooke to avail.
If you are registered with another GP Practice,
please contact them directly. The Flu vaccine is
available to all aged 65 and older. If you are
aged between 18-64yrs, please note that we will
be running a separate clinic in the coming weeks
for you as stock of vaccines are made available
to us. Those aged between 18-64 years are
eligible for the free flu vaccine if one of the
following applies: You have a chronic heart
condition, liver disease, chronic kidney disease,
Diabetes, chronic respiratory disease including
Asthma, COPD, Multiple Sclerosis, Down
Syndrome or, if you are a carer to somebody
with a condition listed above. Any queries,
please ring 074 9551330.

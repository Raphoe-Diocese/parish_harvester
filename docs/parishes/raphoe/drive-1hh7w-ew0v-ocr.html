SPEEDING THROUGH LETTERMACAWARD VILLAGE – A
demonstartion/ protest which is been organised by community
groups will place on Wednesday 2nd September 2026 at 3pm
along the main road on the cycle track/footpath from the
Dirlaught crossroads to the Gweebarra Bridge. The speed limit
through the village is 60km per hour and this is not been
adhered to. Lettermacaward is not prepared to wait for a
fatality before action is taken .You are asked to come out and
support this demonstration.
MARKET HALL We are delighted to announce the first Market
Hall event in Lettermacaward being held in St Bridget’s Hall on
Saturday 5th September from 1pm to 5pm to celebrate and
promote local producers and artists. A huge range of locally
produced products await you. There will be more than twenty
stallholders all delighted to sell their goods and wares,
everything from daily use items such as homebaking, jams and
fresh vegetables to creative arts such as paintings and
handcrafts. When you are all shopped out please stay a while
with a cuppa and a wee treat. So, come along and bring your
friends and family and help support our local community.
Parking is available next to the hall. Eircode is F94 TY81. Hope
to see you all there!
COME & SEE: Have you heard about the Encounter School of
Ministry Course but aren’t sure what it’s about or if it’s for you?
We invite you to ‘Come & see’ what it's about before you make
your decision. Class will take place on Thursdays from 6.30pm
(first half hour is prayer and worship) to 9.00pm beginning
on 3rd September at All Saints Church, Newtowncunningham.
Participants can attend up to 3 classes without committing to
pay for the course. If a participant decides to enrol before the
application deadline of October 1st, tuition fees will then be
applied. If a participant decides not to continue, there is no
obligation. More info or register here:
https://encounterschool.org/donegal/ or
email donegal@encounterschool.org for more information.
DONEGAL GROUP 173 RISE AND SHINE TRUST (formerly The
Irish Handicapped Children's Pilgrimage Trust and The Irish
Pilgrimage Trust) are celebrating forty years of taking young
people with special needs to Lourdes each Easter.
We are celebrating with a special Mass on Saturday 26th
September in the Church of the Irish Martyrs, Letterkenny at 12
noon. The Mass will be concelebrated by Bishop Niall Coll and
Bishop Donal Mc Keowan, Bishop of Derry and many of the
priests who travelled to Lourdes with us over the years.
We invite all the guests and their families and all the helpers
who travelled with us over the years to our Mass to celebrate
the work of the Donegal Group over these forty years.

Nominations requested for Tawnawilly Parish Pastoral Council (PPC) The Parish Pastoral Council (PPC), which has been in place in our parish since 2023, is now renewing its membership and looking for new people to become involved.

Some existing members will step down, while those remaining will continue in their role, offering experience and continuity.

New members will be welcomed and will bring new ideas and perspectives.

This process of renewal helps to keep the council representative of the parish community and responsive to the current needs of the parish.

The role of the PPC is to work alongside the parish priest Fr. James, in addressing all aspects of the pastoral life of the parish including liturgy, formation, social activities and outreach.

The PPC meet every four to six weeks from September to June each year.

The council helps to identify the current and future needs of the parish and encourages people to become actively involved in the parish to support and strengthen our community of faith.

If you wish to nominate someone who you feel would be suitable for this role, please put their name in the Parish Pastoral Council suggestion boxes at the back of St. Agatha’s Church or St. Patrick’s Church.

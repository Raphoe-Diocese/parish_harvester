Alpha
The Alpha is a 10-week series of sessions
exploring the basics of the Christian faith.
Alpha provides a safe space for people of all
ages to discuss life, meaning and faith in a
friendly and informal environment.
Each Alpha session start with a bit of social
time and a cup of tea or something to eat.
There is then a video on some aspects of the
faith which includes a discussion on the
topic with a small group. This time is an
opportunity for people to discuss aspects of
faith in a very friendly and informative
manner. Alpha is open to everyone who
wants to explore the Christian faith,
particularly those who know little or nothing
about it at present and would want to find
out more.
If anyone is interested in finding out a bit
more about Alpha or being part of the next
course in the parish starting in September,
please contact the Parish Office with your
details or speak with Fr. James or any
member of the Parish Council. For further
information on Alpha, please visit:

Eucharistic Adoration – St. Patrick’s
Following on from the recent meeting of
those involved in Eucharistic Adoration, it
was agreed to change the hours of
adoration in St. Patrick. This will begin this
Thursday 10th September. The new hours
of adoration in St. Patricks will now take
place between:
2: 00 pm and 8: 00pm each week.
St. John Paul II said of adoration that,
‘Jesus himself is calling you…’
All of us are encouraged to take part in
Eucharistic Adoration to help us grow and
strengthen our relationship and our
closeness to God, the Father, Son and Holy
Spirit. It’s not a time that’s wasted, but a
time that nourishes us in our faith and a
place where we gain a better awareness of
the presence of God with us and his love
for us. Spending time with God in
adoration connects us with God, but also
with one another, as we pray not only for
our own needs but for others as well. It can
also benefit us personally as it offers us
peace, clarity and strength to help us
through all we deal with each day in our
lives. These are just some of the many
blessings and gifts given through adoration.
So please consider giving some time to
Jesus in the Eucharist. “Deepen your prayer
life as well. Spend time praying before the
Eucharist in Eucharistic adoration. These
practises help you to hear God’s voice
clearly.” Pope Leo XIV

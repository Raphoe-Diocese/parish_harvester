Like last year, we again are asking families of parishioners who have died during the past year to bring in a picture of their

recently deceased

family member to either, St. Agatha’s or St. Patrick’s Church.

These pictures will then be put on display during the Novena where we can remember and pray for them in a special way.

As St Paul wrote to the Romans: “Nothing can separate us from the love of Christ”. The pi ctures of those who have died, are pictures of people who are still our family members, parishioners, neighbours, brothers and sisters in the faith and part of all of us.

Having their pictures displayed during the Novena will give us all, once again, an opportunity to pray in a special way for them and remember them.

It is also a way for us as a parish to acknowledge the pain and hurt that their bereaved families have endured and to assure them of the consolation our prayer and our sympathy.

Therefore, if you are a family member of a parishioner who has died recently, (since 1st November 2024) we are asking you, to bring in a framed photo of your loved one.

We would need them by

Sunday 1st November at the latest, so as to prepare the display in time for All Souls Day, Nov. 2nd.

You can leave them in the Parish Office, or the sacristy of either church, with your contact details.

The pictures will be returned to you after the Novena.

Nominations requested for Tawnawilly Parish Pastoral Council (PPC) The Parish Pastoral Council (PPC), which has been in place in our parish since 2023, is now renewing its membership and looking for new people to become involved.

Some existing members will step down, while those remaining will continue in their role, offering experience and continuity.

New members will be welcomed and will bring new ideas and perspectives.

This process of renewal helps to keep the council representative of the parish community and responsive to the current needs of the parish.

The role of the PPC is to work alongside the parish priest Fr. James, in addressing all aspects of the pastoral life of the parish including liturgy, formation, social activities and outreach.

The PPC meet every four to six weeks from September to June each year.

The council helps to identify the current and future needs of the parish and encourages people to become actively involved in the parish to support and strengthen our community of faith.

If you wish to nominate someone who you feel would be suitable for this role, please put their name in the Parish Pastoral Council suggestion boxes at the back of St. Agatha’s Church or St. Patrick’s Church.

Thursday adoration includes the Divine Mercy 5:30pm and concludes with Rosary 8:30pm All Masses are available live on: www.naomhfionan.com & Parish Radio 106.5 FM Reflection Reading today’s gospel challenges us to ask ourselves how well we are responding to the Lord’s multiple invitations to us.

Are we so tied up in all kinds of concerns and anxieties a bout things which do not really matter that we fail to recognise or simply ignore the invitation to God’s banquet?

And, even if we may not persecute God’s prophets and messengers, how seriously do we take their words of wisdom?

Following the refusal of the invited guests to accept the invitation, the King sends his servants out again – this time not to the houses of the wealthy and respectable, but to the crossroads in the town.

These were places near the gates and markets of a typical Middle East town where large crowds of ordinary people would gather.

Here you found the social and religious outcasts, tax collectors, prostitutes, all those in despised trades.

This time no exceptions are made. All ar invited, good and bad alike, until the wedding hall is filled.

What Jesus is saying is that everyone is invited to the Kingdom of God.

All kinds of people are called to be part of God’s new world.

It does not belong to an elite group of specially ch osen ones.

It encompasses saints and sinners. As the sacrament of the Kingdom, the Church must never forget that, in God’s eyes, there are no first and second-class members.

It must resist the temptation to create elite groups that are ‘different’ and, by implication, ‘better’ than the ordinary average members.

While it might seem wonderful to have a Christian community consisting only of totally dedicated members, it would not reflect the love of God reaching o ut to all.

It would not be a catholic church, a church open to all.

The only requirement is ‘the simple offering of your faith, green as a leaf’.

Cuimhneacháin Báis

Sr. Gráinne Coyle, Convent of Mercy, Dublin, and formerly Ballintemple, whose funeral took place in Dublin last weekend.

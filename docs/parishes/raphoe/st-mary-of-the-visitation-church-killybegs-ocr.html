St Josaphat Prayer for the People of Ukraine Feast 12th November: Patron Saint of Ukraine Saint Josaphat, You stood on the side of justice and right while showing kindness to friend and foe alike.

Your death was a saintly martyrdom. Your enemy in the shape of an angry mob beat you with sticks and weighed down your body with stones in deep water.

We ask you to intercede on behalf of your Ukrainian people who today face martyrdom and suffering.

Bombs, drones, bullets, and deprivation are the sticks and stones that inflict terror and death on innocent people.

We join our prayers to yours O Holy Martyr for your people.

May we from whose skies only rain and hail fall look with compassion and goodwill towards your people in Ukraine.

Inspire us to assist in whatever way is within our means.

We ask you with God’s help to make us doves of peace and not hawks of war or hatred in this conflict, and in our lives.

Saint Josaphat, intercede for Ukraine and bring peace, only peace.

Novena Prayer to the Holy Souls in Purgatory Dear Lord, we trust in Your infinite goodness.

We praise Your justice and implore Your mercy upon all the Holy Souls in Purgatory as they await their eternal Heavenly rest with You.

Many of us have grieved the death of our relatives.

You know the depths of our sufferings in grief, but You give us great hope in Your mercy.

Your justice demands that all souls be totally purified before coming into Your presence in the Beatific vision, and You mercifully allow us to participate in this mystery through our prayers for the dead.

We ask You to bring all the Holy Souls in Purgatory into eternal Heavenly rest with You, especially the souls of our deceased relatives.

Help us to grow in our trust in You each day.

Help us to surrender our lives fully to You, even in the midst of pain or suffering.

We ask this Prayer through Christ Our Lord.

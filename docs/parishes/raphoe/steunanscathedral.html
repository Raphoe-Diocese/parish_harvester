The Letterkenny Community Heritage Group is holding a DEDICATION
CEREMONY OF THE WORLD WAR 1 MEMORIAL at Cathedral Square,
on Wednesday 19th August at 7.30 pm. The memorial is in remembrance of all
from the Letterkenny area who went to fight in the First World War. A short talk will
be given on the idea and siting of the memorial in this location, followed by some
music and an ecumenical blessing of the site. The last post will then be played.
Refreshments will then be available in Conwal Parish Hall. The Community Heritage
Group would like to send an open invitation to the local community to attend this
event and help remember those who never returned.

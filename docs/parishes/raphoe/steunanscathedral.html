ST. PIUS X ADORATION CHAPEL
Eucharistic Adoration is a devotion whereby members of a parish unite in
making continuous adoration before the exposed Blessed Sacrament, for as
many hours as may be possible, based on the number of people who pledge to
do a weekly holy hour. Eucharistic Adoration is silent and contemplative in
We in this parish are very blessed that St. Pius X Adoration
Chapel (located in the grounds of Loreto Secondary School) is
open from Sunday 1 pm – Friday 8 pm and that we have
enough people willing to commit an hour each week. Many
people, who are committed to weekly adoration, experience
extra graces and blessings. People who spend time in weekly
adoration are powerful witnesses in their community to the real Presence of
Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament. Are you willing to be that witness?
THE FOLLOWING HOURS ARE IN NEED OF ADORERS :
Tuesday: 10 pm - 11 pm; Wednesday: 12 noon - 1 pm; 1 pm - 2 pm; 2 pm - 3 pm;
Thursday: 7 pm - 8 pm. If you are interested in committing to one of these hours or
an hour of your choice - please contact Mary on 086-0870446.

The Letterkenny Community Heritage Group is holding a DEDICATION
CEREMONY OF THE WORLD WAR 1 MEMORIAL at Cathedral Square,
on Wednesday 19th August at 7.30 pm. The memorial is in remembrance of all
from the Letterkenny area who went to fight in the First World War. A short talk will
be given on the idea and siting of the memorial in this location, followed by some
music and an ecumenical blessing of the site. The last post will then be played.
Refreshments will then be available in Conwal Parish Hall. The Community Heritage
Group would like to send an open invitation to the local community to attend this
event and help remember those who never returned.

MUSIC MINISTRY SEMINAR
The Diocesan Church Music Group will hold a Seminar for all those involved
in Music Ministry in Parishes throughout the Diocese on Saturday 10th
October in the Mount Errigal Hotel beginning with registration at 10 am.
The purpose of this seminar is to offer a new Mass setting which might
interest you and provide some general music, with an emphasis this year on
Responsorial Psalms. An additional theme of the Seminar this year, is Music
for Weddings and Funerals. We will introduce and reintroduce appropriate
music for these important liturgical occasions in the liturgical life of every
parish. Whilst this is a specific topic, sometimes outside of the remit of parish
choirs, all the music we will be offering will be suitable for use on Sundays
and other occasions throughout the year.
• The Seminar is open to Choir Directors, all Choir Members,
Organists and Church Musicians.
• We also welcome all who sing or play an instrument at weddings
and funerals but who may not be directly associated with music
ministry on a regular basis.
A new directory for music appropriate for use at weddings and funerals will be
launched by Bishop Niall Coll at the Seminar. It will provide suggestions for
music for weddings and funerals as well as giving musicians and singers
references for listening to and obtaining music.
• We would ask all Choir Members, if interested in attending this
Seminar, to please give your name, to your Choir Director.
• For individuals, who provide music on other occasions, we ask that you
contact the Parish Office, leaving your mobile number and email address
as soon as possible.
• The cost of this Seminar is €35 per person, which the Parish will be
covering for those who are involved in its music ministry . Cathedral https://www.steunanscathedral.ie/newsletter/

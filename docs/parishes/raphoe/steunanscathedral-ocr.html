We are now looking for new volunteers for this important Ministry in the Parish.

Perhaps you also know someone you could propose with their permission?

We are looking for both younger adults and recent retirees who would be suitable for this ministry.

How do you know if you might be suit ed and suitable?

Here are some questions you can ask yourself: Do I my faith [Sunday Mass every week]; Do I have a love for the Holy Eucharist?

Do I live in accordance with the Gospel as the Church teaches?

Do I desire to be of service to the parish?

Do I desire to grow in my faith? Many devout Catholics question whether they are worthy to distribute the Holy Eucharist. We all have failings, and the inner life of the soul is known only to God.

If you are indeed asked to do this service, then you must trust that you are suitable and able.

Training will be provided. Ministers are scheduled by the Coordinator, who will take account of other commitments you may have.

We would like to see candidates come forward who attend our daily Masses, especially 8 am and 10 am, especially younger attendees and recent retirees.

Wednesday 4th November. ST. PIUS X A DORATION C HAPEL Eucharistic Adoration is a devotion whereby members of a Parish unite in making continuous adoration before the exposed Blessed Sacrament.

Eucharistic Adoration is silent and contemplative in nature.

We in this parish are blessed that St. Pius X Adoration Chapel is open from

Sunday 1 pm -

Friday 8 pm and that we have enough people willing to commit to an hour each week.

People who spend time in weekly adoration are powerful witnesses in their community to the real Presence of Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament.

Are you willing to be t hat witness? Can you give one hour of your week to spend in quiet prayer and listen to the Lord?

We need more committed adorers … that is, someone who will be there faithfully every week (or someone in your place) at an hour suitable to you.

Gratitude invites us to notice God's loving presence in the ordinary moments of each day. Whether shared in a
kind word, a quiet sunrise, or the simple gift of time with those we love, these everyday blessings remind us that
grace is never far away. As we cultivate thankful hearts, we begin to see that even the smallest moments can
become encounters with God's generous love, drawing us closer to him and inspiring us to share that love with
others. A grateful heart sees God’s hand in everything, the laughter of a friend, the gift of a new day, even the
challenges that help us grow.

Cardinal Newman Prayer
May the Lord support us all day long,
till the shadows lengthen and the evening comes,
and the busy world is hushed
and the fever of life is over,
and our work is done.
Then in His great mercy
may He give us a safe lodging,
and a holy rest and peace at the last.
Amen Inver

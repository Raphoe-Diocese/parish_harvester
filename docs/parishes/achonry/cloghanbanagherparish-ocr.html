Clonony Castle: Sir Mathew De Renzy Versus Sir John MacCoghlan, a play by Eddie Alford, is a Historical Drama about life in West Offaly during the plantations of thes eventeenth century.

This is a fast moving tale o f ambition, reinvention, rivalry and resilience.

The story and text are inspired by the writings of Sir Mathew De Renzy, (1577-1635) who came to West Offaly in 1613 and took up residen ce in Clonony Castle.

Much of the information came from knowledge gleaned by the forensic study of Dr Renzy's manuscripts by Banagher historian Kieran Keenagh an who died earlier this year leaving a great treasure trove of essays on De Renzy's correspondence and land surve ys.

Please come and support The Harbour Theatre Company as we tour this new original play across West Offaly, Luker's Tues Nov 24th McIntyre's Wed Nov 25th Hough's Thurs Nov 26th and Birr Theatre Sat Nov 28th all at 8 p.m.

The veneration of the Archangels began in the Eastern Church. In the West, this day firs t marked the dedication of a Fifth century church of Saint Michael in Rome. Raphael and Gabriel were added to the Roman calendar in the twentieth century, and the three are now celebrated together. WED 30 : Sep 30 – St Jerome (340-420) priest and doctor of the Church. Baptised in Rome while studying the classics, became a hermit in Syria for a time and was ordained a priest. Began work onanew Latin translation of the Bible. Finally settling in Bethlehem where he founded monasteries, devoted hi mself to studying the Scriptures, writing, and teaching. Noted for his asceticism and scholarship and his incomparable service tothe word of God. THU 01 : St Thérèse of Lisieux (1873-97) Carmelite and Patroness of Missionaries, Doctor of the Church. Coming from a devout family, entered the Carmelites at fifteen, embraced the suffering of ill-health in service to the missionary spread of the gospel. Loved for her simplicity , humility and fidelity to ordinary duties as the path to sanctity, ‘The Little way’. FRI 02: The Holy Guardian Angels. Based on the scriptural belief that God assigns everyone an angel to guard body and soul – an expres sion of God’s personal care for each individual.

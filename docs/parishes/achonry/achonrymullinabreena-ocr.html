Court Abbey, – Conservation Works Notice Important conservation works funded under the Community Monuments Fund 2026 has commenced at Court Abbey.

The project involves the cutting back of vegetation from the historic monument to improve its condition & support its long-term conservation.

To facilitate these works, a crane & specialist contractors will be operating on site for approximately 2 weeks, subject to weather conditions.

As part of the works: A pedestrian gate pillar & a section of the graveyard boundary wall on the western side is temporarily removed.

A temporary crane platform is installed on adjoining private land, with the kind permission of the landowner.

All cut vegetation will be removed from the site for disposal.

The graveyard boundary will be reinstated temporarily following completion of the vegetation removal works, pending further conservation works proposed for 2027, subject to funding.

Visitors are asked to exercise caution in the vicinity of the works & to follow any temporary safety signage in place.

Sligo County Council would like to thank the local community, the adjoining landowner, & all those assisting with this important project to help safeguard Court Abbey for future generations.

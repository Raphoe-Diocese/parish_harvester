Norms for indulgences at the Commemoration of All Souls From 12 noon on 1 November until midnight on 2 November, the faithful may gain one plenary indulgence, applicable only to the souls of the departed, by: Devoutly visiting a church or oratory.

Reciting there one Our Father and the Apostles’ Creed.

Fulfilling the usual conditions for a plenary indulgence listed below.

Visit to a cemetery from 1 November to 8 November inclusive, the faithful may gain a plenary indulgence for the Holy Souls by: Devoutly visiting a cemetery & praying for the faithful departed, even if only mentally.

Fulfilling the usual conditions for a plenary indulgence.

This indulgence may be gained once on each day from 1 to 8 November, & is applicable only to the faithful departed.

The usual conditions for a plenary indulgence.

For either of the above indulgences, the faithful must: Go to Confession.

Receive the Eucharist. Prayer for the intentions of the Holy Father - one Our Father & one Hail Mary, or another suitable prayer.

Complete detachment from all sin, even venial sin, with a sincere disposition of heart.

Confession may be made within the week before or after 1 Nov., according to the Irish norms cited above.

Holy Communion and prayer for the Pope's intentions are ordinarily undertaken on the day the indulgenced work is performed.

Make us gentle in word, kind in spirit, strong in hope and guide us, ever gently along that road of peace that leads to your door; a door that will always open to the knock of faithful people.

Amen. TO SING IS TO PRAY TWICE …… It’s an old saying but a true one!

We all love to hear people sing as it is a sign that there is joy in the room and room in the heart to share that joy.

At parish level, we try as best we can to include music and song in our celebrations.

Thankfully, people join in that singing and we are lucky to have that.

We are also lucky to have choirs in both our churches.

Sincere and repeated thanks to all who are involved.

There is always room for new members and, with that in mind, if you have a song in you, a tune in you, would you consider volunteering to be a member of the choir?

We will talk about this in more detail but it would be wonderful to have some additional members to accompany those who lead us in music and song, especially in the Senior Choir in Tubbercurry.

We made an effort last year to develop a Youth Choir and, though we had some interest, it did not quite work out last year.

That is not to say, there isn’t this year!

Sing …. Pray twice! Tubbercurry-Cloonacool https://tubbercurrycloonacool.com

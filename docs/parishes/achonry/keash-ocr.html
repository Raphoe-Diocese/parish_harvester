Annual Special Remembrance Masses for the Dead.

Our Annual Special Remembrance Masses for the dead will take place on the weekend of the 28th/29th of November at both Masses.

A member of each family will be invited to come forward to light a candle for their loved ones who have died.

The names of those who have died during the past year will be read.

If you have relatives or old neighbours who would like to come, please let them know, especially those who have lost a loved one recently.

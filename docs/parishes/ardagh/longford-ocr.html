Thirty-Third

Sunday 15th November This Sunday’s first reading from The Book of Proverbs speaks about the perfect wife nurturing her household.

It is an image of what God has entrusted to us.

In the Gospel the talents refer to our share if the life of Grace– our share in the work of creation, our sharing in the building up of the kingdom of God, and our responsibility for nurturing all those around us.

St Thomas Aquinas writes: “ creation is none other than the relation of creature to the creator. ” 2a.

We depend on God for everything-life, faith the world, beauty and each other.

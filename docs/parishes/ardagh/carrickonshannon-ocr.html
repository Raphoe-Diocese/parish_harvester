for more information BE-PREPARED.- Have your oil in your Lamp: Our gospel reading today teaches us that there is a time when the door will be closed to those who are not prepared.

It speaks to us to make the kind of choices in life that will facilitate our admission into the kingdom of God.

The parable further calls us to abandon a life of carelessness, foolishness and presumption.

We are all call ed to be always conscious of our eternal purpose, to be vigilant, watchful, prayerful, atte ntive, and prudent while we live this life.

To escape the predicament of t he foolish virgins, we must cultivate the habit of regular meditations, constantly asking ourselves such critical questions, on the need that we should always be ready in lives.

For the master to meet us prepared, and our lamp of faith must be kept burning, we must constantly fill the lamp with the oil of faithfulness and obedience, humility, sacrificial love, compassion, chastity and purity.

To live otherwise, is to be like the foolish bridesmaids who were unprepared when the master arrived. our first reading sees wisdom as a sure way to the realizations of o ur purpose in life – to seek and find God and enjoy his company here and in the eter nity.

COCOON MEMOIRS

Local writer James Molloy is collecting the memories and experiences of people aged 70 and over who were asked to cocoon during the COVID-19 pandemic for a project called Cocoon Memoirs, a community oral-history project aiming to preserve these stories for future generations.

If you cocooned during the pandemic, or know a friend, relative, or neighbour who may be interested in sharing their story, James would love to hear from you.

Contributors can contact James at covidvoicesbook@gmail.com or by post to James Molloy, PO Box 13889, Birr, Co. Offaly. 10% of proceeds from each copy of the finished book will be donated to an Irish charity supporting older people.

29th Sunday of the Year

MISSION SUNDAY

"You will shine in the world like bright stars" Today we join with over one billion of our Catholic brothers and sisters around the world who are observing Mission Sunday.

It is a day when our attention is drawn to the Baptismal Font, the place where our faith journey began.

On that day we were called to be sharers in the mission of the Church.

Mission is at the heart of our Church because our Founder, Christ was the first missionary.

If you take all references to mission out of the Bible, you will have nothing left except the covers!

Consequently, the true greatness of our Church lies not in how many seats it fills but in how many it sends-out!

The Church must send or the Church will end!

We are sent then, as our ancestors down the ages were sent to make His voice heard and His face seen.

All we need to be successful in our missionary task is embedded in the gifts, talents, capabilities, dreams and visions we possess.

We just need to use them!

Feast of All Saints – 01st November & the Month of Remembrance: From my own experience of having loved ones die, as well as what others have shared with me, I have found that usually, a fter a time, we sense that our deceased loved ones no longer need us to pray for them.

Now they just want us to connect with them in remembra nce of Holy Mass, visits to the cemeteries, and for any Holy Soul that may need our prayerful remembrance in November or any time of the year.

Praying and remembering them gives us a lovely and comforting presence.

Today’s feast calls to mind women and men who are known to us pe rhaps, who really embody these ideals, and who are taught by the Spirit to learn from the wisdom of God.

Many of our brothers and sisters are been killed because of the faith as Christians, like last week all the Catholic Christians that were murde red in the church in Jordan.

These are saints who have witnessed to their belief and faith, and now it has been taken from them like so m any in the past. They too, give us here in our parishes hope a nd courage, to be ambassadors for Jesus, for truth, goodness, and love.

We often take f or granted in the western world our freedom of religion etc., but not so in other parts of our world.

God’s presence and response is always to fill us with his blessing, giving us a share in the very of the Blessed One, so that if we perserve in friendship with God, we might ourselves become Blessed, and join the company of the saints.

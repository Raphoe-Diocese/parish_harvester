Thirty-Third Sunday in Ordinary Time – World Day of the Poor: Today’s readings mark the beginning of the Week of Witness dedicated to praying for Christians persecuted for their faith, and speak of trials and endurance.

As we reflect on the readings, we focus on Christians persecuted for their faith, trusting in God’s promise of ultimate victory.

St . Paul today speaks directly to those who may have to pay a price for staying faithf ul.

He says that the day of the Lord will come like a thief.

The world around them talks of peace and security.

The c harity “Aid to the Church in Need ” tells us that some 413 million Christians live in countries where religious freedom is severely violated.

That’s 18% or about one in five Christians.

Let’s keep them in our prayers in a special way this weekend.

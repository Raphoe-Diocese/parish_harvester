The Priest must live only to become a saint
A book titled: ‘The Priests we need to save the Church’ is a crie-de-cour by the author
for real priests, holy men, to lead once again the Church of God - and to offer solace
and sure support to the flock of Christ that is lost and bleeding. The writer, Kevin
Wells, demands much from the priest who will ministe r today. He says “for far too
long too many wayward churchmen have profaned, insulted, and trampled upon a
sacred heritage that generations of hardworking, sincere Catholics priests built their
lives around. “We need faithful priests - not the lazy, repressed, social justice types
with inauthentic vocations who have packed the seminaries, dominated the
headlines, and sullied the reputation of the holy priesthood over the past several
decades. Until the parish priest lives only to become a saint, there will be no healing
in the Church. Until the parish priest embraces poverty and pours himself out without
counting the cost, the Church's sickness will worsen. Until the parish priest decides
to die for his flock, the Church will remain on life support. “We need real men today;
bold proclaimers of God's Word who are unafraid to preach the Catholic faith as the
blazing furnace of Truth that it is. We need priests who are willing to preach tough
homilies, hear daily confessions, commit to daily Holy Hours, and remain
magnanimous of heart through long, sometimes thankless 16-hour days. We need
priests to offer up their lives now so that the laity will gain a restored sense of
fatherhood. Such priests will become the story of our time. They will bring about
massive conversions and re store our Church and our culture.”

Artificial Intelligence and risk of dehumanization
The head of the Ukrainian Greek Catholic Church said in an interview that “it is
essential that artificial intelligence not replace human beings, nor be used against
human dignity.” Major Archbishop Sviatoslav Shevchuk warned that “it is imperative
that human beings not become slaves to the fruits of their own culture, to what they
themselves have created.” “What artificial intelligence will never be able to do is to
love,” he added. “We must still become ‘human’ with a capital ‘H.’ And this h appens
when we contemplate the face of God or speak with Him. For we are made in the
image of God.”

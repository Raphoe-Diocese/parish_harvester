Thinking about being a Priest in the Diocese of Cork and Ross?
If you are between the ages of 18 and 40 and feel there is a
possibility that God may be calling you to be a diocesan priest,
then our monthly Discernment Group. Meetings may be a good
way to help you to discern this call. It is a gentle non-co mmittal
first step in the process which takes the form of a monthly
meeting with other men discerning their call. The monthly
meetings offer an opportunity for prayer, conversation,
fellowship and accompaniment. An experienced spiritual
director will also be provided to help guide you along th e
path. The first meeting takes place on Saturday September
26th. To find more, have a chat, or to register your intere st,
please email Fr Marius at  vocations@corkandross.org

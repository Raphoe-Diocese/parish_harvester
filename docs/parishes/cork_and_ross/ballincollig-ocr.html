Mon - Wed. 9.00am - 12. 30pm The Society for the Propagation of the Faith was founded in 1822 by a French laywoman, Pauline Jaricot.

She wished to help not only French missions and missionaries but all missions and missionaries especially the poorest. In 1922 The Society for the Propagation of the Faith became the primary organisation of the Church for supporting the activity of the missionaries.

Pope Pius X1 instituted Mission Sunday in 1926 and ordered that it be observed in a ll churches and oratories throughout the world as a day of prayer and material support for missionary activity.

It was Pauline who drew up the basic plan of the Society.

It is one of prayer, offering up of suffering and making a financial contribution for the spread of the Faith.

This year the theme chosen by Pope Leo is "One in Christ, united in mission" “Holiness and mission are inescapable aspects of the vocation of every Baptised person.

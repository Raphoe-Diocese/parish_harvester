Attheendofthe fifteenth century Islam was the great threat to Christian Europe. (Thinking of September eleventh one might ask: "Does anything ever change?".) In 1498 the Turks occupied the Gree k city of Lepanto directly E astofthetoeof Italy . This Islamic presence just across a narrow sea from Southern Italy remained in place until 1571 when a combined Papal, Spanish, Venetian and Genoese fleet under Don John of Austria set out to defend Italy and the rest of Western E urope. On the first Sunday of October 1571, huge Rosary processions took place in Rome asking Our Lady's protection for Christianity. On that very day, October 7th., the Christian forces gained a spectacular victory over the Turkish fleet. Fifteen Turkish ships were sunk and 177 taken. 15,000 Christian rowers, slaves on the Turkish galleys were liberated. Itwasthe first great defeat of Islam at sea. Pope St. Pius Vordered that a commemoration of the Rosary should be made on that day and some 200 years later after another victory over the Turks in Hungary the Pope extended the Feast of the Holy Rosary to the universal Church. Pope Leo XIII added to the Litany of Loreto the invocation "Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, pray for us." There was nothing new about the Rosary onthedayofthe BaƩle of Lepanto. A century before that the Dominican Order had been organizing the Confraternity of the Holy Rosary all over Europe. Catholics both in England and Ireland made the Rosary their particular devotion always turning to the Mother of Godat times of special need. For those who love the Rosary in life it becomes a symbol of their Faith entwined in their dead fingers at theendof life.

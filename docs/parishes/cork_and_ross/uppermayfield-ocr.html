Gospel Reflection
A new way of working …
Have you ever been jealous of a sibling, a colleague
at work, a team-mate? Have you ever felt that they
were receiving better treatment than you, even
though you put in more effort, more time, or did a
better job than they did? If you are like me and
answer honestly – I am sure we can all, say YES to
these questions; and so, this parable is for each of
us!
Our Gospel today offers a radical way of looking at
life and work. Today Jesus upsets the expectations
and the commonly held views of the status seeking
and result-driven society. He makes it clear that the
economy of the Kingdom of God is not based on
individual merit, competition, success and
achievement; rather it is predicated on the common
good, justice, inclusion and equity for all people.
That is why he tells this parable where the vineyard
owner gives as much to the last hired worker that
day as he does to those hired first. It was hard to
accept for the workers that day, just as it is hard for
us to accept sometimes when we feel we are treated
unfairly in comparison to others. Today offers us an
invitation to reflect and to come to accept that the
common good of all people is way of the Gospel. Family of Parishes Clergy

Introducing Lay Funeral Ministers We still value having our funerals over several days, we appreciate the effort our neighbours make to come to the funeral home to sympathise, we look for a funeral Mass which acknowledges the personality of the deceased person an d their relationships, not just a generic church service to pray for them. To assist parishioners keep our funerals as we like to have them, our north-side parishes will introduce lay ministers as part of a funeral team with the clergy. Initially these lay funeral ministers will lead the bereaved in prayer thenight before the funeral Mass, usually in the funeral home, as priests used todo before Covid. Many of us have missed that moment of prayer with the priest and so we will re-introduce it with the lay ministers. In time, the lay funeral ministers will be available to families to begin prep aring the funeral Mass with the diocesan booklet of readings, prayers and hymns. Later, they may be present at the funeral Mass andbeof help there. We're not sure when priests will begin to be unavailable forallthe burial services inthe cemeteries, but it is worth preparing for that eventuality sooner rather than later. A number of parishioners have already been approached to consider taking on this rewarding ministry. They will be trained over several weeks by the diocese and then begin working alongside the clergy of our parishes. Weareall aware o f the lay funeral ministers already operating in Farranree, around The Lough and Togher. We know you will appreciate the ministry of lay funeral ministers alongside the ministry of the priests and deacons, representing the parish by leading mourn ers and their neighbours, family and friends, in prayer the night before the funeral Mass. While we are beginning with the three northside parishes which have approximately 166 funerals of parishioners each year - the cathedral, Blackpoo l with the Glen and Ballyvolane, Sunday's Well - in time, we will extend this valuable ministry to the other parishes in our family of parishes. Please k eep your fellow parishioners in prayer as they prepare to serve in our pari shes. The initial meeting for those already approached to consider this ministry will be on Wednesday 30th September at 7pm in the Cathedral Visitor Centre.

Ballinhassig Parish Newsletter for Ballygarvan, Ballyheada and Goggins Hill Vol. 35 No. 6 Sunday: October 18th, 2026 (World Mission Sunday) Every Christian is a Missionary Mary O’Hsubmits…. World Mission Sunday, which is celebrated on the second last Sunday of October each year, is the one Sunday of the year when the entire global Church comes together in support of mission.

It is the Holy Father’s annual appeal for spiritual and financial support so that the life giving work of overseas mission and missionaries can continue. ‘Every Christian is a Missionary’ and our Irish missionaries continue to bring the Good News to people throughout the world.

World Missions Ireland, the working title of the four Pontifical Mission Societies (Missio Ireland), organises the Mission Sunday collection in all Churches this week end Saturday/Sunday 17th/18th.

A Prayer for a Missionary Dear Lord, thank you for calling me to share your love and truth with others.

As I serve in places that may be unfamiliar and challenging, help me trust you each step of the way.

Give me courage when I feel afraid, strength when I grow weary, and wisdom as I minister to those you have placed before me.

Help me reflect your love through my words, actions, and daily life.

Protect me from harm, provide for my needs, and guide me wherever you lead.

When I miss home or feel discouraged, remind me that I am never alone and that your presence goes with me.

Open hearts to receive your message and help me remain faithful to the work you have called me to do.

May everything I do bring glory to your name.

Amen. Ballinhassig http://www.ballinhassigparish.com/

Readings October 6th Gn 2:18-24. Ps 127, 5. Heb 2:9-11. Mk 10:2-16. Team Hope Shoe Box Appeal 2026 Team Hope’s Christmas Shoebox Appeal is an annual campaign that delivers gifts straight into the hands of children affected by poverty.

Your Shoe Boxes will bring smiles laughter and hope to children who think the world has forgotten them.

A shoebox won’t change the world, but it might just change the world for one child, it can do far more than we can imagine, by bringing Christmas to children who have never experienced anything like it before.

The project simply works by asking people to fill an average sized shoe box or plastic container, wrap the box and lid separately with Christmas paper.

What to include in your box:- Write - Pencils, paper, copybooks, crayons, colouring books etc.

Wash - Soap, toothbrush, comb, facecloth etc.

Wear - Socks, hat, scarf, gloves, underwear, flip flops etc.

Wow - Doll, ball, cuddly toy, sweets, skipping rope, soft toyetc.

Do not include liquids (shampoo), food or chocolate, sharp or b reakable items. You choose whether you would like your box to go to a boy or girl and what 2-4; 5-9; 10-14. All boxes are opened by volunteers at the collection centres simply to check they are ok for contents and customs purposes. (Our boxes will go to a collection centre in Cork city or county for Eastern Europe & Africa) . The boxes are then sealed, sent by truck or container and will only be opened again by the receiving child.

Boxes are given to children based on need, regardless of religion, race or politics.

We are motivated to help others based on the beliefs of Christian tradition, which are compassion, social justice and dignity of all people.

If you would like to participate in this project contact Mary at the A. Centre (Parish OfÏce) at 021 4885 062 for further information and special leaflet which must accompany each box.

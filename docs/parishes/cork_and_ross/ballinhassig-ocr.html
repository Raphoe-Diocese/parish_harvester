Readings October 6th Gn 2:18-24. Ps 127, 5. Heb 2:9-11. Mk 10:2-16. Annual Mass for Bereaved November 20th Most of November we concentrate on remembering our dead.

The Holy Souls Novena, the visits to the graveyards are good but they make it impossible for people to avoid being revisited by the pain caused by the death of a loved one.

Here in Ballinhassig Parish we have had sad, sudden and untimely deaths this year and in the past few years.

Consequently we have many parishioners for whom November is a very sad and lonely month.

The thought of facing Christmas and beyond without the departed loved one always brings enormous sadness in need of some ray of hope to dispel the darkness of grief.

Conscious of this, the Annual Mass for the Bereaved will be held on Friday November 20th at 7.00pm in Ballyheada Church.

All those who suffered a bereavement in the past twelve months, family members, neighbours and friends are especially invited to attend this Special Mass.

It is hoped this Special Mass will bring a message of hope, comfort and consolation to all who live with the pain of loss, especially the death of a loved one.

As part of the ceremony everybody will be invited to bring a lighted candle, available at the door, to the altar on entering the church.

These individual candles will then symbolise a community of hope with all candles then lighting as one.

Everybody welcome on the night. We are also conscious that we may not be aware of all parishioners who suffered bereavements of family members who resided outside the parish.

The night, however, is not only for those bereaved in the past twelve months, it is a night for remembering and praying for all who are grieving.

There is a great tradition of support and understanding in our parish at times of bereavement.

Mass on Friday November 20th will be another opportunity to offer sympathy, and support through being present and praying with our bereaved families, neighbours and friends.

Remembrance Sunday World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims is celebrated on the third Sunday of November each year, this year it is November 15th . It is a day to remember those who died or suffered serious injuries in road traffic accidents and also remember their families.

On this day we also pay tribute to the dedicated emergency crews, gardaí and medical professionals who deal daily with the traumatic aftermat h of road collisions.

World Day of the Poor 2026 will be on this Sunday November 15th.

To close the Year of Mercy, Pope Francis sug- gested the Catholic Church set aside one day each year when communities can “reflect on how poverty is at the very heart of the Gospel.” It will be celebrated on the 33rd Sunday In Ordinary Time each year.

A Prayer For Families to say on World Day of the Poor Dear God, Our Father.

As we remember World Day of the Poor, We pray for our family and for all families who experience poverty around the world.

When we eat, we thank you for our meal, and we remember families who do not have enough food.

We do not waste what we have, and we share our food with others.

When we play, we thank you for the gift of free time and safety, and we pray especially for all the children around the world who are not able to play freely.

When we pray, we thank you for our family, and we pray for all families around the world who live in poverty.

May you be with them, and help our family to always remember to reach out to them.

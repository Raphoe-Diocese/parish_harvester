Ballinhassig Parish Newsletter for Ballygarvan, Ballyheada and Goggins Hill Vol. 35 No. 8 Sunday: November 1st, 2026 (All Saints) Remembrance and Thanksgiving for our Loved Ones Mary O’Hwrites…. Many people find November a difficult month, those who have been recently bereaved and those who carry the scars from a past painful bereavement.

Most of November we concentrate on remembering our deceased loved ones: - The Holy Souls Novena, the visits to the graveyard, while these are spiritually good for us they make it impossible for people to avoid being revisited by the pain and loss of a loved one.

We hold dear the memory of those who have been a truly significant part of each of us.

The gift of memory and its place within our hearts brings us consolation . Our hearts are warmed by the comfort of others and slowly over time we begin to heal and embrace life once again as our loved ones would wish us to live.

We ask God to heal our pain and sadness as the month of November is a time of Remembrance and Thanksgiving for the lives of our loved ones , no matter how long or short that earthly life was.

Reflection:- Holy Souls are special people Whom from us God did call We are left here broken hearted For we love them one and all.

All we have left is just our memories Of the times when they were near Longing for those precious moments Wishing they were here.

But death is not the end for us It is just an open door where some day we will be with them With more happiness that before.

Once again with all our loved ones All our sorrow it will end In God’s home we’ll be re united Parents, family and friends.

So in this special holy month Remember them and pray Those holy souls our own dear loved ones to God are on their way.

The sky is still as blue, the grass is still as green, the birds sing as sweetly, the flowers have not lost their fragrance, it may not seem so at the moment but slowly it will.

Plenary Indulgences in November By long tradition, plenary indulgences for the souls of t he departed may be gained in November as Follows: - On All Souls Day A Plenary indulgence for the holy souls may be gained once between noon on November 1st and midnight on November 2nd, by visiting a church or oratory and there reciting one Our Father and the Apostles’ Creed.

The person making this prayer must: Have recently received the Sacrament of Confession (any time within the week preceding or the week following November 1st) Have received Holy Comunion (any day from November 1st to 8th).

Have prayed for the Pope’s intentions :- one Our Father and one Hail Mary, or any other prayer of one’s choice.

On any day in November A plenary indulgence for the holy souls may be gained once each day between November 1st and 8th, by visiting a cemetery and praying for t he dead.

The same conditions as above (confession, communion and prayer f or the pope’s intentions) are also required.

All welcome. Prayer of St. Gertrude: Releasing Souls from Purga tory The Prayer of St. Gertrude is a traditional Catholic Prayer offered for the Souls in Purgatory.

According to Catholic tradition, Jesus promised St. Gertrude that reciting this prayer devoutly would release 1000 souls from Purgatory each time it is said.

Eternal Father, I offer You the most Precious Blood of Your Divine Son, Jesus, in union with the Masses said throughout the world today, for all the Holy Souls in Purgatory, for sinners everywhere, for sinners in the Universal Church, those in my home, and within my family.

Community in Action at St Oliv er’s Church Singing for the Brain Brings the Community Together It was wonderful to see St Oliver’s Church full of music, movement and conversation recently as members of the local community came together for a Singing for the Brain session. Facilitated by Ceara, the session involved much more than singing, with participants taking part in breathing exercises, movement and activities designed to engage both the body and mind. Most importantly, it provided an opportunity for people to come toge ther, enjoy each other’s company and take part in something positive within their local community. Founded by Fiona Flavin of the HSE, Singing for the Brain uses singing and related activities to support social connection and wellbeing, with sessions taking place in communities across Cork. It is great to see the parish rooms at St Oliver’s Church being used to support initiatives like this and to provide a welcoming space where people from the community can meet, learn, participate and connect with one another. The session is just one example of the positive community activity taking place within St Oliver’s and of how our parish rooms can continue to serve as spaces for connection and community. If you are interested in learning more please contact the Singing for the Brain Administrator on 089 6136342 or email singing4thebrain@gmail.com .

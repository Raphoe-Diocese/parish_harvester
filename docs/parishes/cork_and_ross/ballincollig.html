As the Church prepares to observe the Tenth World Day of the Poor on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Pope Le o XIV delivered a message focused on the theme: “The Lord is the refuge of the poor” (Psalm 14:6), and focuses on condemning social injustice, arrogant corruption, and the factors that deepen poverty.

In his message for the 10th World Day of the Poor, Pope Leo XIV contemplates God as “the refuge of the poor.” He encourages Christians to re- evaluate the importance of the poor in our lives and to reflect on our dedication to justice, solidarity, and human dignity.

The Pope warns that the cry of the poor is often silenced through “a multitude of increasingly subtle tactics,” while the digital world can worsen prejudice and reinforce indifference and that “the poor of our day are the forgoƩen and the marginalised: robbed not only of bread, but also of a voice and a face.” “The poor have no choice but to cry out to God,” he writes, adding that they entrust themselves to Him “certain that they will be heard because God is faithful and rich in mercy.” The Holy Father expresses hope that the Tenth World Day of the Poor will help Christians “rediscover the faces of so many brothers and sisters who seek refuge in God and long to feel at home in our communities.” talents in the service of our parish community.

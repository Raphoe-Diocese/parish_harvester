Thinking about being a Priest in the Diocese of Cork & Ross?
If you are between the ages of 18 and 40 and feel there is a possibility that
God may be calling you to be a diocesan priest, then our monthly
Discernment Group Meetings may be a good way to help you to discern this
call. It is a gentle non-committal first step in the process which takes the
form of a monthly meeting with other men discerning their call. The monthly
meetings offer an opportunity for prayer, conversation, fellowship and
accompaniment. An experienced spiritual director will also be provided to
help guide you along the path. The first meeting takes place on Saturday
September 26th. To find more, have a chat, or to register your interest,
please email Fr Marius at vocations@corkandross.org

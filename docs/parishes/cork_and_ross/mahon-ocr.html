Yet Jesus is speaking about something much deeper.

The talents represent all that God has entrusted to us: our faith, gifts, opportunities, time, and ability to love and serve others.

The servants who invest their talents are praised, not because they make a profit, but because they respond generously and courageously to their master's trust. The third servant allows fear to guide him.

Rather than risk failure, he buries his gift and leaves it unused.

As the Church year draws towards its close, this Gospel invites us to reflect on how we are using the blessings God has given us.

Faith is not meant to be hidden away or kept safely buried.

It is a gift to be lived, shared, and put into action.

The Lord continues to place His trust in us.

May we have the courage to use our gifts generously, so that our lives bear fruit for God's Kingdom and for others.

Christmas Triduum Mass Cards These cards are now available to buy from the Parish Office.

The cost is just €3 – a bargain! This is the same price as 2025, and they have proved to be very popular over the last number of years.

As in previous years, the proceeds from the sale of the cards will go to the local conference of the Society of St. Vincent de Paul and also to help with the missionary work of Bishop John Ryan in the diocese of Mzuzu, Malawi.

Society of St. Vincent de Paul The local conference helps people in need with a range of supports including food vouchers, fuel for heating, support with domestic bills, guidance, advice and encouragement to access support from the wider SVP network.

Volunteers are always needed, so if you feel that you could help with this important work, please contact 087 – 152 0360.

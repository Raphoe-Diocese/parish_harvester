Parish Envelope Box sets The Parish envelope box sets for 2027 will be prepared for distribution over the coming weeks.

If you are not presently using the envelope sets but would like to do so, please contact the parish office.

Use of the envelopes either weekly, monthly or periodically assists the parish in preparing a claim each year under the Charities Tax Refund Scheme.

With a donor’s consent, the parish can claim a tax refund when the annual contribution by a tax payer equals or exceeds €250.00. Thank you to all who continue to support the parish in any way.

Fifteen students begin priesthood training for Irish dioceses
Fifteen new students have begun their training for the priesthood for the academic year
2026 – 2027. In total, there are now 74 seminarians in formation training to minister for
Irish dioceses, and these are:
- 15 entering their Propaedeutic Year in various seminaries (Introductory Year)
- 51 are studying in the national seminary of Saint Patrick’s College, Maynooth, and at the
Redemptoris Mater Seminary in Dundalk; and 8 are based in seminaries on the continent.
A Special Collection for Priesthood Education will be taken up this weekend, 19th/20th
September, throughout the Diocese.
Please remember them in your prayers.

faithful servant of God and humble priest,
you bore the wounds of Christ with love

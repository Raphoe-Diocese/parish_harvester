First Reading: Isaiah 45:1.4-6 The liberation of Jerusalem's exiles is a sovereign act of God through a human instrument.

The prophet explicitly identifies Cyrus as the one to affect this liberation.

Cyrus is called God's anointed even if the true God was to him an unknown God.

Nevertheless, the Israelites saw the hand of God through Cyrus.

Second Reading: 1 Thess 1:1-15 For a young Church faced with persecution, the Thessalonians have given evidence of Christian living, which could be a model for every Christian Church: faith in action, expressed in charity, and enduring with hope.

These fundamental virtues express the continuity of past - present- future in Christian life.

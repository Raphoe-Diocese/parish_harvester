office@glanmireparish.ie Reflection You shall love the Lord, your God, with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.

This is the greatest and the first commandment.

The second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself.

By observing the second commandment you are actually showing that you love God with all your heart, soul and mind.

The two commandments cannot be separated.

Every month when I visit houses, I witness the incredible care which sons and daughters and grandchildren give their parents.

Frequently, this care is given by neighbours and relatives as well.

Invariably at fu- nerals, it is humbling to again witness this compassion from family membe rs, friends and neighbours.

Annually when I witness the second and sixth classes making their First Communion and Confirmation, the children show that they understand showing love to the family and grandparents in a unique way at the sign of peace and at the end of mass.

Parishioners incidentally tell you about how generous and compassion - ate other people are, by visiting people in hospital, working with the Vincent de Paul Society and visiting people in prison.

Yes all of these expressions of keeping t hese commandments happen in this family of parishes.

So today let us thank God, that so many people observe loving God, with all their hearts, souls, and mind and loving their neighbours.

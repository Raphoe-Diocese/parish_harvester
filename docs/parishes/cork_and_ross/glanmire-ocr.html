Today we celebrate the feast of Jesus Christ, King of the Universe.

Jesus is a very different King. He is the King of the Truth, the King of unconditional Love, the King of Life.

His teaching in the gospel every week sheds a new light on how we can live our lives better.

So when we listen to Jesus in the gospel we know we are hearing the truth.

Jesus always bears witness to the truth even when it would have been more advantageous in the short term not to as we have heard in today’s gospel.

Jesus’ completely different concept of kingship is only recognised by those who listen to him and realise that he is the truth and that by following him we will be saved, redeemed and will enter into the kingdom of God.

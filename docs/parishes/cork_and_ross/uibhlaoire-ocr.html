Envelopes and lists for the names of your beloved dead are still available in the three churches of the parish and in the Parish Office.

Parish Envelopes If you use the envelope system for the various church collections, your box of envelopes for 2027 is now available from the Parish Office.

A sincere word of thanks to all who support the parish financially whether it is through the envelope system, by placing cash in the collection baskets, and by periodic or annual contributions.

Thanks also to those who have signed up for the envelope system following the recent appeal at the weekend Masses.

For your information, the offertory collection at the weekends and on church holidays goes towards the financial upkeep of the parish on a day-to-day, week-to-week paying the bills, just like any household.

Youth Ministry Collection This annual diocesan collection will be taken up at all Masses next weekend, 14th/15th November.

The collection supports the work of Sheila Kelleher, our diocesan youth ministry coordinator, and the many volunteers dedicated to the nurturing of the faith among our youth.

It is important for us to encourage and support our young people in every way we can so that they can become involved in their local parish.

Station Masses Now that the Station Masses have finished for the Autumn, just to say a word of thanks to those households who generously hosted a Mass for their neighbours and friends.

Thanks for the hospitality, the welcome and also to those who provided refreshments after the Church Stations in Little Island, Knockraha and Glounthaune.

Thanks also to the many volunteers who so kindly distributed the Station Mass envelopes to the homes in the various parts of the parish.

If you wish like to host a Station Mass for your area of parish in Spring 2027, please give it some thought and contact the Parish Office if you would like to do so.

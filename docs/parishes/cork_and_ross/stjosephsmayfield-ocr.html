Gospel Reflection:
Be persistent… and patient…
Our Gospel today is about persistence… just
as the widow in the story pesters the judge
until he delivers a just decision we too are
called to be persistent.
Persistence in prayer demonstrates that we
are not afraid to show our dependence on God
even though all around us society says we
need be self-sufficient, to look after
ourselves. To keep praying is equally looking
after ourselves but also the needs of those
around us and the needs of our world. The
widow in the story is concerned about the
delay in getting a response, however we must
not only must persevere in prayer we must be
patient, awaiting God’s time for an answer
and accepting the answer even though it may
not be one we wanted! God will always give
the right answer to our prayers, even if in the
immediate moment it may not seem that way.
Take time to reflect on what have been the
fruits of your own persistent prayer? When
you have patiently waited for God’s time and

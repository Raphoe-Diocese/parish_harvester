Sunday 6th September 2026 – Twenty Third Sunday in Ordinary T ime – Year A
In this Sunday's Gospel, Jesus offers practical gui dance for dealing with conflict. Rather than allowi ng disagreements
to grow, he urges us to take the first step, speak honestly, and seek reconciliation. The goal is not to win an argument
but to restore a relationship: "If he listens to yo u, you have won back your brother." We know how com munities, families
and individuals can carry old hurts for many years. Recent public debates remind us that unresolved di sagreements
never simply disappear. Yet the Gospel challenges u s to move beyond division and to look for opportuni ties to heal
relationships. Jesus invites us to ask difficult qu estions of ourselves: Is there someone I need to fo rgive? Is there
someone from whom I need to seek forgiveness? Is th ere a conversation I have been avoiding? Where peop le make
the effort to build bridges, Christ is present. His way is always the way of reconciliation, understan ding and peace.

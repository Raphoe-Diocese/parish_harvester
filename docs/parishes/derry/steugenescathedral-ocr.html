A short reflection on today’s Gospel Matthew 1 5:21-28
Today’s Gospel contains an exchange between Jesus and a Canaanite woman in which Jesus
appears to exercise racism and elitism to a degree which shocks us to the core. But is this
what is really taking place! Is Jesus a victim of inherited prejudice and a n unquestioning
acceptance of Jewish supremacy? Or is he demonstrating effectively what happens when
we fail to reach out in love to all mankind: when we fail to realise that it is God’s will and
the will of Jesus, His beloved Son, for us to gro w in unive rsal love and care as opposed to
self-love, racism and the false notion of selected supremacy! What happens is that a child
remain s suffering in torment while a loving mother is forced to watch on in helpless anguish.
Jesus always see s into the truth which lives in the heart. He sees int o the heart of the
Canaanite woman. He sees her love for her tormented child. He sees her willingness to
suffer insult for the sake of her child. And he sees her unflinching faith in him as her child’s
only hope.
Jesus, may we grow and mature daily in universal love and care, away from ‘self -love and
elitism’ in the knowledge that it is your will that we do so.

A short reflection on today’s Gospel John 18:33-37 In today’s Gospel, Jesus stands before Pontius Pilate, who questions Him about being a king.

Jesus explains that His kingdom is not of this world.

He is not a king who rules through power, wealth or authority, but one who leads through love, truth, mercy and sacrifice.

As we celebrate the Solemnity of Christ the King, we are invited to reflect on who truly reigns in our hearts.

To recognise Jesus as our King means following His example: showing kindness, offering forgiveness, standing up for what is right and caring for those in need.

Jesus says, “Everyone who is on the side of truth listens to my voice.” In a world full of distractions, we are reminded to listen to His voice and allow His teachings to guide our daily lives.

As we bring the Church's liturgical year to a close, may we welcome Christ more fully into our hearts and help build His kingdom of peace, justice and love in our homes, our parish and our wider community.

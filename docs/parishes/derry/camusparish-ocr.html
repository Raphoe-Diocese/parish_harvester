clonleighparish@derrydiocese.org ; Website : www.clonleighparish.com Gospel Reflection (Matthew 22:34-40 ) In today’s gospel, Jesus teaches that the greatest co mmandment is to love God wholeheartedly and to love our neighbour as ourselves.

These two responsibilities are inseparable and form the foundation of Christian life.

Loving God involves more than religious practices suc h as prayer, worship, and Scripture reading.

It means trusting Him in difficult times, remaining faithful through challenges, choosing what is right, and allowing Him to guide every aspect of our lives.

Genuine love for God is shown not only in words but also in daily actions.

Our love for God becomes visible in how we treat others.

We cannot truly love God while harming, judging, or refusing to forgive those around us.

Instead, we are called to show compassion, kindness, patience, and mercy.

Even simple acts such as listening, encouraging, forgiving, and helping those in need can become powerful expressions of God's love.

Jesus also calls us to recognize the dignity and wort h of every person, including those who differ from us or disappoint us.

True love does not ignore wrongdoing, but it speaks the truth with respect and sets boundaries wit hout hatred.

The central message of this Gospel is that love must be at the heart of both our relationship with God and our relationship with othe rs.

Faith is not measured by religious knowledge or activities alone, but by wheth er it transforms us into more loving people.

When love guides our thoughts, words , and actions, our lives become a living witness to God's presence in the world.

May the Lord bless us with hearts open to love.

May us to see Christ in one another and to bring kindness, hope, and compassion wherever we go.

As I begin this morning, help me to remember that my worth is found in You and not in the opinions of others or in my own doubts.

Fill my heart with confidence, not pride, but the quiet assurance that I am created with purpose and loved without condition.

Help me to recognise the gifts You have placed within me and give me the courage to use them wisely.

When fear or insecurity tries to take hold, remind me that I do not walk alone.

Strengthen my mind, guide my words, and help me face each challenge with faith rather than worry.

Teach me to believe in the person You are shaping me to become.

May I carry myself with kindness, speak with confidence, and trust that Your plans for me are good.

Today, let me walk with peace, knowing that I am enough because I am Yours.

Amen.

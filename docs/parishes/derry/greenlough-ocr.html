SUNDAY 13 SEPTEMBER 2026
TW ENTY FOURTH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME
Next weekend, as we prepare to
welcome Bishop Michael Router
among us, we will pray God's
blessing on him in a particular
way at each of our weekend
Masses.
Once again we wish Bishop Dónal McKeown a very happy
and fruitful retirement. No doubt he will remain very active
as he offers his services to Bishop Router, and to the priests
and people of the diocese in whatever ways he can.
Our new bishop was a priest of the diocese of Kilmore and,
for the last seven years, has served as Auxiliary Bishop in
Armagh. He comes among us as a stranger, yes, but also as
a pastor. He asks us all to be patient as he gets to know us
and as we get to know him.
We pray that his time among us in this diocese of Derry will
see a great renewal of faith as we "walk together" (the
meaning of Synodality) in the footsteps of all the faithful
bishops, priests, religious, and people who have gone before
us.

Please pray for Bishop Michael Router as he prepares to

begin his pastoral ministry among us. The Mass of
Installation will take place in St Eugene's Cathedr al,
Derry, next Sunday, at 3.00pm.
Each parish is asked to send five members to the
ceremony. Invitations for parishioners from both
Bellaghy and Greenlough can be collected from the

parish office at Bellaghy

Anniversaries: Assumpta Henry, Robert

Mullan, Joe Henry, Annie Reid, Carrie
Bradley, Eileen Donnelly, Mary Graham,
Angela Boylan, Paddy Rankin
In today's gospel Jesus teaches a powerful
lesson on forgiveness. We pray for the men
called to priesthood that, as they celebrate
the Sacrament of Reconciliation with their
people, they will help them to know and
experience the Lord who ".is compassion and love, slow
to anger, and rich in mercy." (Responsorial Psalm)

EUCHARISTIC ADORATION: Anyone interested
in promoting Eucharistic Adoration in the parishes
of Bellaghy and Greenlough is invited to attend a
meeting in the Drummond Hotel, Ballykelly, BT49
9HP, on Wednesday 30 September, from 8-9pm
(Registration will take place from 7:30pm).
Both Bishop McKeown and Bishop Router are happy to
promote Eucharistic Adoration throughout the diocese as a means of bri nging
people to Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament, to foster vocations, and to
encourage prayer for vocations.
We are already blessed to have weekly Adoration in our p arishes - in
Greenlough on Thursdays, and in Bellaghy on Fridays. Now, p erhaps,
would be a good time to invite and encourage more people to pray before

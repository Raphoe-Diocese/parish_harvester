From 1 September until 4 October (The Feast of St
Francis of Assisi) we are in the Season of Creation, an
initiative of the late Pope Francis. The theme this year
is: "Living Water."
As well as using the gift of water carefully and sparingly,
we are invited to pray for the many billions of people in
today's world who cannot take fresh, running water for
granted.
Pope Leo asks us to think about the “deserts inside us, in
our hearts" and to pray that the Lord will renew our
hearts, moving us from "desert to garden, from division
to communion, from violence to peace."
We are invited to pray for all those who will be taking
part in World Youth Day in Seoul, South Korea, next
year, that they will take courage, and become streams of
Living Water, bringing faith, hope, love, and care for
creation into the life of the Church and the world.
Let us pray together.
Let us act together.
Let us make space for conversion.
Let us make Living Water flow.

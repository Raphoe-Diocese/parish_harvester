Wednesday 21 October at 7.30pm. A Warm Welcome to Fr Paul Fraser We are delighted to extend a very warm welcome to Fr Paul Fraser, who has been appointed Parish Priest of Drumragh.

A native of Derry City, Fr Paul was ordained to the priesthood in St Patrick’s Church, Pennyburn, on 1 June 1986. Following three years of ministry in Buncrana, Co Donegal, he undertook teacher training at St Mary’s, Strawberry Hill, London.

He subsequently served as a teacher and chaplain at St Columb’s College in Derry.

Over his forty years of priestly ministry, Fr Paul has gained a wealth of pastoral and educational experience, including appointments in the Archdiocese of Los Angeles and the Diocese of Arundel and Brighton, where, alongside his parish responsibilities, he also taught Psychology and ICT. In 2015, he returned to Derry as curate in St Mary’s Parish, Creggan, before moving to Tyrone in 2019 as Parish Priest of Ardstraw West, Castlederg and Aghyaran.

Three years ago, Drumquin (Langfield) was added to these parishes, forming the West Tyrone Pastoral Area.

Fr Paul now comes to Drumragh at an important time in the life of our Diocese, as we respond to changing circumstances and seek new ways of working together to build strong and vibrant parish communities.

He is looking forward to the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead for the Church in the Omagh area.

We wish Fr Paul every blessing as he begins his ministry among us.

Let us keep him in our prayers and give him our wholehearted support, not only through our words of welcome, but through our presence, our encouragement and our active participation in the life and mission of the parish.

It invites Catholics everywhere to participate in the Church’s global mission through prayer and financial support for mission dioceses most in need.

Every baptised person shares responsibility for proclaiming the Gospel and supporting the growth of the Church throughout the world.

The theme for World Mission Sunday is chosen by the Holy Father.

Pope Leo XIV served as a missionary priest and bishop in Peru, where he sawhow faith, prayer and generosity can transform communities.

So, who is the service for? Anyone who has either personally lost a baby at any stage of pregnancy, at birth, or in early years, or who has been affected by family members' or friends' loss.

Whether the loss was recent or 80 years ago, everyone is we lcome to attend.

We have also extended the services, and gladly welcome anyone who is grieving the fact that they haven't had children.

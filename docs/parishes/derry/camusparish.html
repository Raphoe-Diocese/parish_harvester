Prayer at Time of Exam Results
Dear Lord, in this moment of uncertainty, I seek Your peace and guidance.
Help me to accept the results of my efforts with grace, whether they meet
my hopes or not. Grant me the strength to learn and grow from this experience,
trusting in Your plan for my life. May I find comfort in Your love and wisdom.
May Your peace fill my heart as I walk forward in faith. Amen.

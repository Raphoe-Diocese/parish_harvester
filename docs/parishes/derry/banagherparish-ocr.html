Our Lady Untier of Knots Novena The Novena to Our Lady, Untier of Knots will begin after the 7.30pm Mass on Thursday 1st October in St. Eugene’s Cathedral. It will contin ue for the following eight Thursdays, concluding on 26th November. Do you have ‘knots’ in your life? Knots of discord in your family, unreso lved pain in relationships, or a lack of peace and joy in your home? Sometimes life can feel like one overwhelming knot, especially when we face health challenges or burdens beyond our con trol. This Novena reminds us that sin never entangled Our Lady, and that Christ gave us His Mother as Our Lady, Untier of Knots. Through this devotion we entrust our personal ‘knots’ to her loving care – learning toletgo and allowing her to gently untie what binds us. All welcome to join in prayer and reflection.

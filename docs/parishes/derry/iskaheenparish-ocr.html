Scoil Naomh Fionán and Scoil Naomh Brid will re-open shortly for the
New School Year. Welcome back to all the pupils and staff and a BIG
WELCOME to our Junior Infants, and all new members of staff.
Blessing of Bikers and Motorcycles will take place on Sunday 13
September in St Aengus’ Chapel, Burt, Co Donegal, at 1:30. All are welcome
to attend the brief service and blessing. Following the blessing all are welcome
to join the run to Ned’s Point RNLI Station, to present the donation to the RNLI.

Chapel, DUNGIVEN, at 11:30am for deceased bikers, especially those who
have died in the past year.

It is our aim over the next number of weeks to take the ‘mystery’ out of the Bible and to give us all a whole new
understanding and love of God’s Word . In St Patrick’s Hall, Castlederg , we are going to run an eight-week Bibe
Study course called “Unlocking the mysteries of the Bible .” It will take place over the course of eight
Wednesday evenings, commencing 9 September, from 7.30 – 9.00pm. This course is presented by renowned
Catholic speaker Jeff Cavins who, through engaging video presentations and his acclaimed Bible Timeline
approach, will literally unlock the mystery of the Bible for us.
There will be opportunity for small-group discussion, with the aid of a workbook, with w hich you will be provided.
There is a suggested donation of £15, which will include your workbook, a colour-coded Bi ble Timeline Card which
will be a useful tool as we move along the ‘timeline’ of the course. Refreshments will be provided.
You can book for the course in advance via Fr Paul / the Parish Office or just come along and enrol on Wednesday
9 September. Let us unlock the mystery of the Bible together. Everyone is welcome.

JESUS IS WITH US IN THE STORMS OF LIFE
Every Christian experiences storms. We naturally ask the Lord to remove our
difficulties, yet the Gospel reveals that Jesus often offers something even
greater: His presence. Rather than simply calming the storm around us, He
seeks to deepen our trust in Him within it.
The greatest miracle in this Gospel is not that Jesus walked on water, nor even
that Peter briefly did the same. The true miracle is that frightened, faltering
disciples became worshippers who recognised Jesus as the Son of God.
Perhaps our prayer should be less, “Lord, make my life easier, ” and more,
“Lord, whatever storm I must face, keep my eyes fixed on You. ” When we do
stumble, as Peter did, we can be confident that Christ ’s saving hand is already
reaching out to lift us up

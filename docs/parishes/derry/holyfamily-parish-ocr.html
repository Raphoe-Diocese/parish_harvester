FEAST OF CHRIST THE KING We have been fit for the kingdom by Jesus’s suffering death and resurrection.

Like Jesus, we are kingdom kinds of people.

And that means that we have the opportunity, through the power of Christ’s cross, to hold together the tensions that life presents to us There are plenty of them to be found.

Maybe we aren’t surrounded by rulers and soldiers and criminals; maybe it’s frustrating neighbours and family members with odious political views and classmates who come from different backgrounds.

But no matter what hostilities or differences are swirling around us, we, the power of the cross of Christ and not on our own power, can stand in the gap and make peace between parties.

Jesus, after all, was able to make peace with his murderers, asking God to forgive them.

And as we saw in today’s reading, Jesus promised someone facing death by execution that he would remember him in paradise.

In God’s kingdom, no one is forgotten. The cross is strong enough to absorb all the world’s contradictions and make peace between them.

We can bring all our inner contradictions to Christ on the Cross.

May we, who have been seduced by the mysteries of the cross, follow Jesus and become a sign of peace in our parish and in a world beset by hostilities, misunderstandings, and resentments.

1st Reading: Eccles (Sir) 27:30 - 28:7. The Book of
Ecclesiastes was composed about 190 BC by Ben Sirach,
a faithful Jew. He had devoted his life to the pursuit of
wisdom. “Resentment and anger, these are foul things,
and both are found with the sinner.” It is a non -
negotiable element of the covenant with the Most High.
To be forgiven ourselves we must in turn forgive others.
2nd Reading: Rom 14:7-9. In our education, entertain-
ment, media, elected government, laws, commerce, wor-
ship – our day-to-day concerns involve others. Let there-
fore the peace and joy brought to us by the Holy Spirit
always serve to help our brothers. A Christian neither
lives nor dies for himself. "Alive or dead we belong to
the Lord." “I give you a new commandment: love one
another just as I have loved you.”
Gospel: Mt 18:21-35. One ‘talent of silver’ would be
valued at about €500,000, an intentionally fantastic fig-
ure. It represents the debt of gratitude we owe the Lord
for re-opening to us the gates of paradise. In comparison
the debt of 100 denarii was a paltry sum. So, if we fail to
forgive human sized debts neither will our debt to God –
to whom we owe everything - be forgiven. LEGION OF MARY

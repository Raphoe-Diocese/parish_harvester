As the late Pope Fran- cis said, "Priests should have on them the smell of the sheep." “NOVEMBER REMEMBER ” PASTORAL INITIATIVE With the grim statistic of there having been 150 people killed on the country’s roads so far this year (12 more than the same period last year) – 45 of these people aged under 25 and 30 aged over 65 (of the 29 pedestri- ans killed, 10 were over 65) – we need to redouble our efforts as road users to do what we can to make our roads safer, especially as we enter the darker and colder months.

To this end, while November is a time when we re- member our dead, this year, in an attempt to acknowl- edge that the life and death of each of us has its influ- ence on others, we would like as many people as possi- ble to take on a pledge to use the roads with care in November.

The details of that pledge are to be found on posters and our social media pages.

There is also a prayer card available from the churches to be kept in your car and to be recited each day during the month.

Signage has been placed on the roads entering our two parishes to remind us of this.

Please do your best to participate in this and support it.

compassionate volunteer drivers to join our
pool of drivers. As a volunteer driver you
would be bringing patients undergoing che-

for their treatment. Gifting your time
means that cancer patients will have com-
fortable (one on one) transport to attend

ney for many during a challenging time but

needed most. For more info and to become
a volunteer driver, please contact Monica or
Emily at volunteer@irishcancer.ie

READINGS FOR 23RD SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME
1st Reading : Ez 33:7-9. Ezekiel (600 BC), was primar-
ily a visionary. As both prophet and priest, he lived
during the destruction of Jerusalem and the subsequent
deportations to Babylon (modern day Iraq). His prophe-
cies were an important preparation for Christ, The Sav-
iour is viewed as a shepherd rather than as a ‘king’,
whose gift will be a new heart and new spirit. Follow-
ing Ezekiel we must do our utmost to correct our
brother, both individually and collectively.
2nd Reading: Rm 13:8-10. We have to learn to help
and to heal each other. Loving our neighbour as our-
selves, the new law brought by Christ, is the one thing
that cannot hurt anyone.
Gospel: Mt. 18:15-20 . This teaching complements the
one about the shepherd (Jesus) going after his one lost
sheep. His communities will imitate the shepherd in
caring for everyone, providing fraternal correction
where necessary. Jesus will never abandon them. OUR LADY OF FATIMA appealed for the Holy

The EOHSJ Order is a Catholic Order under the protec-
tion of the Holy See, dedicated to supporting the Latin
Patriarchate of Jerusalem and the Christian communi-
ties of the Holy Land. The Holy Land is the land of the
Gospel, but it is also home to Christian families,
schools, parishes and communities who need prayer,
solidarity and practical support today. Help sustain the
living Christian presence in the Holy Land by becom-
ing a Friend of the Order. Friends of the Order offers
a simple way to help by providing support for Catholic
schools and teachers; parishes, churches, seminaries
and religious communities; healthcare, homes for the
elderly and humanitarian aid; Projects in Jerusalem,
Bethlehem, Gaza, Jordan, Galilee and the West Bank,
and more. Friends are not members of the Order but are
people of goodwill who wish to support this mission
through prayer, awareness and charitable giving.
100% of Friends’ donations go directly to approved
Holy Land needs, without administrative deduc-
tions.

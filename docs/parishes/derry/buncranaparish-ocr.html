BISHOP MICHAEL ROUTER AT FATHER HEGARTY’S ROCK Only three weeks after his installation as Bishop of Derry, The Most Reverend Michael Router paid a pas- toral visit last Sunday to the parishes of Burt, Inch & Fahan and Desertegney & Lower Fahan (Buncrana).

We were delighted to have him amongst us on what was an unseasonably warm and sunny day, for what was a spectacularly wonderful occasion.

He walked with us on a Synodal walk from the Stone Jug and cele- brated Mass on Father Hegarty’s Rock , before returning then to Cockhill Parish Hall for tea and refreshments.

The turnout was splendid, the welcome warm, the sup- port immense and the faith tangible.

In the midst of a sometimes stiff Atlantic breeze he joked about not be-ing blown into the water, seemingly perched as he was on the edge of the Lough.

But from there he com- manded a great view of the gathering and the Statue of Friar Seamus Hegarty OP – and he offered a most memorable Mass recalling the courage and hope which Father Hegarty had in abundance, and asking God to grant us the same gifts for the challenges in our own time.

The music and singing were outstanding and the joy and contentment of those gathered there, of all ages, was clear to be seen.

After visiting Father Walsh in his home, the bishop returned to Derry with a spring in his step and firm in the knowledge that he will always be welcome in these parts.

It was a truly wonderful occasion made possible only by the devotion and help of huge teams of people: our stewards and volunteers, choirs and musicians, farmers and bakers, schools and Parents’ Associations (especially at St Oran’s NS ), and to all who came to celebrate.

To them all and to God himself, we must always be thankful.

Sun 18th - 5pm THROWING OPEN THE DOORS TO CHRIST NOVENA This year’s Novena begins next

Sunday 25 October at 7pm in St Mary’s Church , Cockhill.

For the nine Sundays before Christmas we will gather for one hour and the ceremony will comprise of the Rosary, a talk given by an invited speaker, the Novena prayer and petitions (which can be placed in a box at the door of the church), a Eucharistic Healing Cere- mony and finishing with Benediction of the Most Blessed Sacrament.

Once again, the theme of this year’s Novena is “Throwing Open the Doors to Christ” – taken from the homily of Pope St John Paul at the Opening Mass of his Pontificate on 22 October 1978. The first Guest speaker on the opening night will be Bishop Michael Router.

All are very welcome. Please come along, bring your families and make the most of this opportunity for prayer as we approach the season of Christ’s birth . (The Holy Hour each Sunday evening in St Mary’s Oratory will cease for the moment, resuming after Christmas.

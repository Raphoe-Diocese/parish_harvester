READINGS FOR ALL SAINTS 1st Reading: Apoc 7:2-4, 9-14. The ‘Apocalypse’ ‘The Revelation to John’ tells of things hidden in the future.

Composed before 90 AD‘the book is an epic of Chris- tian hope, the victory song of the perse- cuted Church,’ an image of the souls al- ready in Heaven.

It speaks ‘of those who (will) have been through the great persecution, and have washed their robes white again in the blood of the Lamb.’ 2nd Reading : 1 Jn 3:1-3. To be able to stand before God with confidence and joy,‘to be like him and to see him as he really is’, this is what Jesus’s coming has enabled us to do.

He “lets us be called God’s chil- dren…” And “Surely everyone who enter- tains this hope must purify themselves, as he is pure.” Gospel: Mt 5:1-12. On a hillside near Capernaum Jesus paints a picture of what being a follower of his will look like.

Note: ‘the poor in spirit’ of the 1st beatitude, ‘the anawim’, are those who submit to the will of God.

God’s kingdom is the answer to the prayers of those who long for a better way than that of the world.

Having been through “the great persecution”, mentioned in the first reading, their “reward will be great in heaven.” WILDWOOD 12A | 130mins

READINGS FOR 22ND SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME
1st Reading: Jer 20: 7-9. The prophet Jeremiah lived
through the siege of Jerusalem (600 BC), the destruc-
tion of the Temple and deportations to Babylon. His
prophesying urged a shift from outward practices to a
person-to-person, relational, heartfelt religion. For this
he was ridiculed and imprisoned. ‘The prophet here
foreshadows Christ on the eve of his passion: the living
word of God had lit a similar consuming fire deep in
Jeremiah’s heart.’
2nd Reading: Rm 12:1-2. Merely external, outward
practice is not acceptable. Our behaviour has to spring
from a whole new mind, offering ourselves in all things
like Jesus, 'as a holy sacrifice pleasing to God.'
Gospel: Mt 16: 21-27. Back in 1994 at the Maynooth
launch of the Catechism of the Catholic Church
Cardinal Desmond Connell described and explained
how Jesus will proudly wear the wounds of his Passion
for all eternity. Christians should accept their cross just

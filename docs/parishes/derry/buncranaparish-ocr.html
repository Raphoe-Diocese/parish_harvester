READINGS FOR 30TH SUNDAY IN OT 1st Reading: It is c.1200 BC. In this passage the Lord is teaching his people something never before heard in the religions of the world.

As much as the Lord cares for us as his beloved creation, he vehemently requires us to care for one another, especially the weakest and most defenceless among us.

There is a strict prohibition here against usury between the rich individual and the poor person in need. 2nd Reading: This letter is the earliest writ- ing of the New Testament.

Paul compliments his converts from Thessaly on having taken to the Gospel “with the joy of the Holy Spirit”, in spite of serious opposition.

News of how they had broken with idolatry had already “spread everywhere” (even without the Internet!).

Gospel: In this Gospel reading Jesus is in Jerusalem.

Before long he will be arrested by the chief priests and elders.

He uses their carefully contrived questions to reveal ever more about God and our relationship with Him.

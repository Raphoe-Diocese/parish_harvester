Sunday 23rd August 2026 – Twenty First Sunday in Ordinary Time – Year A
In this Sunday’s Gospel, Jesus asks his disciples a question that reaches across the centuries and speaks to every
Christian: “Who do you say I am?” The disciples can easily repeat what others are saying, but Jesus invites them to
move beyond public opinio n to a personal profession of faith. Peter responds, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living
God,” not because he has learned the right answer, but because he has come to know Jesus through walking alongside
him. In a world filled with competing voices and opinions, the Lord still asks each of us the same question. Faith cannot
rely solely on what others believe or what we learned long ago. It grows through our own experience of Christ's presence
in our lives, espe cially in moments of joy, struggle, loss and hope. As we reflect on the Gospel this week, may we
recognise anew the Lord who walks with us and find in him the firm foundation on which to build our lives .

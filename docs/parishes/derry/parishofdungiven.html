DUNGIVEN LIBRARY
On Friday 21 August from 12.00 noon –
1.30 pm. – a lovely event for all the
family (including grown-ups!). And her
name was Finvola… is our week for
hearing about the story behind the song
‘The Gem of the Roe’. A fun storytelling
session first, then Aoife Mullan from
‘The little Music Studio’ will teach us the
song, accompanied by Scarlett O’Hara
on the harp. The children will also get to
draw their favourite scenes from the story.
To finish off our Big Summer Read, on
Friday 28 August at 12:00 noon, the
children can come and get crafty in our
Animal Mask Mania activity.
Each activity takes place in our library
junior section, and no booking is required.
If you’re interested in local history or
tracing your family tree, then make sure
you come to our PRONI event. On the
Tuesday 18 August, PRONI on tou r
brings the Public Records Office of
Northern Ireland and Libraries NI
together to provide archival support and
historical exhibitions in communities
across Northern Ireland. This workshop at
Dungiven Library this August is designed
to help you get started using PRONI's
online services and other resources and
it’s a perfect opportunity to ask questions
directly to PRONI staff.
Booking is required for the workshops on
the first floor of the library, but you can
drop in anytime between 10:00 am - 1:00
pm or from 2:00 pm - 4:00 pm to talk to
PRONI staff in the library's lobby area.

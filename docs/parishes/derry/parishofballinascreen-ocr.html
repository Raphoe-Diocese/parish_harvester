VIATORES CHRISTI PARISH VISIT Members of Viatores Christi will speak at Masses in this parish during the Saturday Vigil and the Sunday Masses on the Weekend of 7/8 November.

Viatores Christi is an association of Catholic lay missionary/development workers, founded in 1960 . VC brings about positive change by improving access to healthcare, education and livelihood opportunities in the marginalised communities in which we work.

We want to raise awareness about the missionary work of the Church and the role of lay missionaries, both at home and overseas.

Thank the people of the Derry Diocese for your support for our work over the years.

We would like to offer an opportunity and preparation to anyone who feels called to volunteer.

And we appeal for ongoing help both financially and above all through prayer.

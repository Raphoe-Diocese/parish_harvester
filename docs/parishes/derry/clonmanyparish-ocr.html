Triduum of Masses for Bishop Michael Router:
As the diocese and parishes prepare to celebrate the joy-
ous occasion of the Installation of Bishop Michael Router
as Bishop of Derry on Sunday September 20, a Triduum
of Masses will be celebrated in parishes throughout the
diocese, on Friday 18, Saturday 19 and Sunday
20 September. In our parish these Masses will be cele-
brated in St Michael ’s Church on Sunday September 20 at
10am and St Mary ’s Church at 11:30am. All are welcome
to attend as we come together as God ’s people to pray
for Bishop Router and the people of the diocese, that we
may walk together in faith and hope, guided by the Holy
Spirit.
Prayer:
God of holiness, we give you thanks for this diocese, the
church that is in Derry.
We recall with gratitude all who handed on the gift of
faith in past generations, in difÏcult times and in troubled
years down to the present day.
We look forward with hope as we walk together in faith
with our new Bishop, Michael, under the guidance of the
Holy Spirit, along the synodal path to which the church is
now being called.
We pray that we may become more faithful disciples of
Jesus and joyful witnesses to his Gospel for the genera-
tions of young people who follow after us in all the par-
ishes of our diocese.
We ask this through Christ our Lord. Amen CLONMANY PARISH DRAW

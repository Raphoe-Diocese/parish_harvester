clonmanyparish1@outlook.com www.clonmanyparish.com Today, the Church rejoices as we celebrate the Solemnity of All Saints.

We honour the countless men and women who now share in the glory of heaven.

The saints are living witnesses to the saving grace of our God.

Today, we are reminded that the saints inspire and urge us to seek their company in the heavenly kingdom.

Through their example, they show us that holiness is possible at every age and in all circumstances.

Therefore, we are called to strive towards the blessedness that God has promised to all of us who love Him faithfully.

This day reminds us that holiness is not reserved for a few extraordinary individuals; instead, it is a possibility for every soul called to live a life reflecting Christ’s love.

The feast invites us to meditate on the virtues of the saints and to seek their intercession, strengthening our own journey towards spiritual perfection.

The Church sees this day as an affirmation of the communion of saints, a mystical bond that unites all believers across time and space.

In celebrating this feast, we remember that our lives are intertwined with the legacy of faith, grace, and moral courage instilled by those who have gone before us.

clonleighparish@derrydiocese.org ; Website : www.clonleighparish.com Gospel Reflection (Matthew 25:1-13) In today ʼs gospel, Jesus tells the parable of ten virgins who were waiting for the bridegroom.

Five were wise because they brought extra oilfor their lamps, while the other five were foolish because they failed to prepare.

When the bridegroom finally arrived, those who were ready entered the wedding ce lebration, while the others were le behind.

Through this parable, Jesus reminds us of the import ance of being spiritually prepared because we do not know when He will return.

The lamp is a symbol of our lives, our faith, and our relations hip with God.

Just as a lamp needs oil to continue burning, our spiritual lives need to be constantly nourished through prayer, love, acts of kindness, and unwav ering trust in the Lord.

We are called to remain vigilant and faithful, keeping our he arts close to God so that our faith never grows weak or runs dry.

By living according to His will each day, we stay prepared for the return of Jesus.

Rather than fearing His coming, we should look forward to it with joyful anticipation, confide nce, and hope, knowing that a life rooted in faith and obedience allows us to welcome Him with hearts full of peace and devotion.

Loving God, thank You for always being with me.

Help me keep the lamp of my faith burning brightly each day.

May I always be ready to welcome with joy, hope, and a faithful heart.th Clonleigh (Strabane Pastoral Area) https://clonleighparish.com

REST A WHILE
In our day -to-day life’s journey, oftentimes we are overwhelmed with
our daily routines, individual tasks as well as duties and responsibilities.
It seems that the 24 hours of the day are not enough because we are too
busy with so many things. We do not have time to “stop and smell the
roses.” We do not have time to relax and to take time out of our busy
schedules to appreciate and enjoy God ’s gift of the beauty of life. We
need time to rest, to recognises our human limitations that we need essential respite to
prevent us from being exhausted and to avoid the possible danger of breakdown.
According to the mechanical engineers, even machines need cooling -off time to prevent
overheating. When we approach our “hallowed space ” like spending time praying in front
of the Blessed Sacrament or lighting candles inside the church, we are refreshed and
strengthened. We will be inspired to go on despite all the burdens, challenges, and
obstacles in life. That is why after the tiresome and successful mission of the Apostles the
Lord told them, “Come away by yourselves to a deserted place and rest a while. ”
Following Jesus ’ command, these first followers were spiritually and physically recharged
to do the next job. We also badly need to have some “refreshment ” of the Lord and to
spend time with Him in silent prayer to find meaning in every life ’s situation. In this way,
we are far from being “worn out” and preventing ourselves from “falling apart. ” And so,
let us all find time to relax and rest for a while with Him.

The Famine Bell
We have recently acquired, through the
good auspices of the Late Father Liam
Donnelly, a very old church bell. This bell
was cast during the famine years.
The bell has been placed in the old
cemetery near the old church ruin in
memory of the victims of the Great
Famine, many of whom are buried here. It is also a memorial to
all victims of conflict in the years since the buil ding of the
Church in 1803.
The bell also reminds us that the law of the land forbade Church
bells when the old church was built. This was one of the Penal
Laws.
Going further back, it also links us to the Great St. Patricks Bell,
which was for many years concealed in Mayogall for safety.
Since the bell was erected last week it has become a focal
point of great interest. It marks a site or remembr ance and
prayer.
The design and construction of the bell was the great work of
Packie Duggan, Andreus and Mark. It is an outstandi ng
addition to the parish along with the great work al ready
carried out in the old cemetery.

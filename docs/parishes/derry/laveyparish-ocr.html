Thoughts on the Gospel (Matthew 14:13 -21)
The miracle of the loaves and fishes began with compassio n. Jesus first noticed the
crowd’s pain before providing for their hunger. In our busy lives, God invites us to see
those who are lonely, discouraged, or forgotten. Even small acts of kindness can
become someone’s miracle.
The disciples focused on what they lacked, yet Jesus asked them to offer what they did
have. God rarely asks for what we do not possess. When we offer our time, talents,
and resources — however small — He multiplies them in ways we cannot imagine.
Before the food was shared, Jesus surrendered everything to the Father. We too carry
burdens that feel heavy, but surrender is not giving up; it is trusting God with our
limitations. What seems insufficient to us becomes more than enough in His hands.
The abundance left over reminds us that God’s blessings are meant to flow through
us. When we share generously, encourage others, and forgive freely, His grace
continues to multiply.
Lord, fill us with compassion, use what little we offer, and multiply it for Your glory.
Teach us to trust You and to share Your blessings generously. Amen.

028 2954 0343Mercy to every person “Lord” , the woman said, “help me. ” Human
life is too big and too complex for any of us to live it on our own. We
have all kinds of people and relationships to help us to wholeness.
The most important one of all has to be the presence of Christ with
us. The intimate relationships of marriage and family life, for
example, change so often and so deeply that we cannot manage
them on our own. Christ is with us to help us see the opportunities
for ever greater loving and to give us the strength to grow in our love.
Relationships within the faith community are also so complex that
we cannot possibly build our unity with one another except through
the power of Christ. When we depend on our own efforts, we set
very low limits and settle for mediocrity. When we call on the power
of Christ, and live by his power, we are set free to reach for the
stars.
Father Johnny Doherty, C.Ss.R.

The Famine Bell
We have recently acquired, through the good
auspices of the Late Father Liam Donnelly, a
very old church bell. This bell was cast during
the famine years.
It is hoped to place the bell in the old
cemetery near the old church ruin in memory
of the victims of the Great Famine, many of
whom are buried here. It is also a memorial to
all victims of conflict in the years since the
building of the Church in 1803.
The bell also reminds us that the law of the land forbade Church
bells when the old church was built. This was one of the Penal
Laws.
Going further back, it also links us to the Great St. Patrick’s Bell,
which was for many years concealed in Mayogall for safety.

in love, humility and a desire for restored relationship. Wh en conflict or hurt arises,
the first response should not be anger, gossip, avoidance or revenge, but a quiet
and honest conversation with the person involved. This priv ate dialogue gives
space for listening, understanding and healing. If the conf lict cannot be resolved
alone, Jesus encourages the involvement of trusted witness es, not to shame or
accuse, but to help guide the conversation with wisdom, fair ness and care. When
diiculties continue, the wider community has a role in enco uraging accountability,

LEGION OF MARY
‘Embrace the Grace’ Young Adult Catholic Conference 29 August. Embrace the
Grace is a conference on faith and culture for young adults (18-40) with talks,
prayer, Mass, confession, workshops, food and social in the historic Legion of
Mary headquarters at De Montfort House, North Brunswick St, Dublin 7. It will
be a chance to build faith in friendship with young adults from across the country.
This event takes place on Sat 30 Aug 9.30am – 6.00pm with a social afterwards.
Tickets 20 euros and can be booked on the Eventbrite website by looking up
‘Embrace the Grace’. Any queries can be directed to deusetpatria@gmail.com
ONE DAY MARIAN PILGRIMAGE TO ST MARY’S CHURCH
The Three Patrons Parish in Derry has arranged a short pilgrimage to each of the 5
Churches dedicated to Our Lady within the Derry Diocese and will include St

praying to Our Lady for peach in our home. Everyone welcome.

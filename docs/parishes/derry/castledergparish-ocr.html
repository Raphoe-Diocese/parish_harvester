As our parishes prepare to welcome Fr Chris next weekend, Fr Thomas and I take this opportunity to wish him every blessing in his ministry in Aghyaran, Ardstraw West & Castlederg and Drumqu in.

Born in 1986, Fr Chris is a native of Leckpatrick Parish.

His parents, Rosemary and Sean , live in Strabane.

Fr Chris tells us that his three brothers, two nieces and a nephew form his closest family.

Following school, Chris was employed for several years in an administrative role at the Oncology Department of Altnagelvin Hospital.

He subsequently pursued seminary studies at St Malachy’s College, Belfast, and St Patrick’s College, Maynooth.

Since ordination in June 2016, Fr Chris has ministered among the faith communities of St Eugene’s Cathedral in Derry, Drumragh (Omagh), Limavady, Kilrea & Desertoghill, Errigal (Garvagh) and Granaghan (Swatragh).

Fr Chris says that, a fter spending the last five years ‘on the missions’ in Co Derry, he now looks forward to serving the people of the West Tyrone Pastoral Area.

He and Kobe (thepet dog) will take up residence in Castlederg Parochial House at the end of the month.

Fr Chris brings many gifts to his ministry and, in giving generously of himself to serving the people entrusted to his care, I know that he will enjoy the support and encouragem ent of you all.

I pray God’s blessing on all that you will share and accomplish together.

Please keep Fr Chris in your prayers as he prepares to move to Castlede rg and begin the next chapter of his priestly ministry in the West Tyrone Pastoral Area.

May God bless you all, Fr Paul.

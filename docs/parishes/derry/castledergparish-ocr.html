THOUGHT FOR THE DAY
The season of harvest is a time of gratitude, reflection
and abundance. For farmers, it signifies the end of a sea-
son of hard work, when crops are gathered and the fruits
of labour are celebrated.
Spiritually, it reminds us of God ’s faithfulness and the
importance of sowing seeds of righteousness in our lives.
The bible speaks of harvest not just as an agricultural
event but as a symbol of spiritual growth, provision, and
God’s blessings.
‘let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper
time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up. ’

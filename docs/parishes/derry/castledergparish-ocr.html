Season of Creation 2026: 1 September to 4 October
The annual celebration of Season of Creation aims to bring Christians throughout the world together in prayer,
reflection and action for the care of our common home. This year’s theme, Living Water , invites us to give thanks
for the gift of water and renew our care for God’s creation. The theme is inspired by the vision of hope and

live… and everything will live where the river goes."

Thoughts on the Gospel
Matthew 18:15– 20
In today’s Gospel, Jesus teaches that reconciliation is a sacred responsibility , rooted
in love, humility and a desire for restored relationship.
When conflict or hurt arises, the first response should not be anger, gossip, avoidance
or revenge, but a quiet and honest conversation with the person involved. This private
dialogue gives space for listening, understanding and healing .
If the conflict cannot be resolved alone, Jesus encourages the involvement of trusted
witnesses — not to shame or accuse, but to help guide the conversation with wisdom,
fairness and care. When difficulties continue, the wider community has a role in
encouraging accountability, unity and peace.
Remember LOVE
L — Listen with humility
Hear one another deeply and seek to understand.
O — Offer reconciliation
Choose restoration and forgiveness over pride.
V — Value community
Protect the unity, peace and wellbeing of the community.
E — Embrace divine presence
Trust that God is present wherever people gather in faith, prayer, forgiveness and
harmony.
Jesus reminds us that love is more than a feeling; it is a way of living . Through
patience, courage, humility and faith, conflict can become an opportunity for healing,
forgiveness and renewed fellowship.
May the peace of Christ guide our hearts, strengthen our unity, and fill our community
with love, forgiveness and grace. Amen.

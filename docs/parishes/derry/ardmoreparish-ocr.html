ARDMORE PARISH NEWSLETTER Twenty-Sixth Sunday in Ordinary Time — Year A Sunday, 27th September 2026 ✝ Thoughts on the Gospel Matthew 21:28–32 — Faith Shown in Action Life is full of promises — to change, improve, forgive, serve, and do what is right. Yet even sincere intentions can sometimes remain only words. In today’s Gospel, Jesus tells of two sons who were asked to work in their father’s vineyard. One refused but later changed his mind and went. The other agreed togobut did not. The message is simple and challenging: our actions matter more than our words. True change turns faith into action. Today’s Gospel invites us to be honest about where we need to grow; to live out kindness and forgiveness; to refuse to be defined by past mistakes; to offer God a willing heart; to examine our conduct; and to embrace every opportunity to do good. God values sincere action over empty promises — anditis never too late to begin again. Lord, fill our hearts with Your love and guide us to live our faith each day. Sometimes life can feel like one overwhelming knot, especially when we face health challenges or burdens beyond our control. This novena reminds us that sin never entangled Our Lady and that Christ gave us His Mother as Our Lady, Untier of Knots .

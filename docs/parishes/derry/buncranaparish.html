gal. READINGS FOR 20TH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME
First Reading: Is 56:1, 6 -7. This passage from Isaiah
serves to introduce today’s much -loved gospel passage.
The time of writing was probably after 500 BC. The
Lord will welcome those Gentiles who come to
‘observe and who cling to my (Mosaic) Covenant.’
God’s house will become for all people a house of
heartfelt prayer.
Second Reading: Rom. 11:13 -15, 29 -32. It had been
the definitive rejection of Christ by the Jews that had
driven the Apostles out of Jerusalem to bring salvation
to the Gentiles, i.e., people like the Romans and the
Greeks. Yet Paul continues to long that God’s mercy
may somehow triumph in achieving the conversion of
his countrymen. Their choice by God as the Chosen
People was permanent and will never be revoked.
Gospel: Mt. 15:21 -28. Leaving the argumentative
Pharisees and scribes behind, Jesus had moved to
southern Lebanon. There he encounters a pagan woman
who addresses him with an ardent plea. His initially
harsh attitude towards her alters mid -stream. He uses
the incident to show his disciples how the Lord will
always listen to the pleas, made in humble faith, even
of pagans GARDEN FETE 2026

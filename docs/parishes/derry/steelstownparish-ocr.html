Creation is God’s work. Before humanity was,
the rest of creation came into being. The earth and
her creatures – indeed the whole cosmos – have
beauty, dignity and meaning before human
presence.
So to nurture awe at the wonder of creation is part
of our calling. Look up into the skies, walk in the
fields, stand at the ocean’s edge. Everything
belongs, everything has beauty! The garden is
such an insightful model for our care of creation.
Our call is to be good gardeners, thoughtful, alert
and diligent, working with the soil and with the
rhythm of the seasons, caring for the creatures
who find home and shelter in the garden.
Ian Adams
Sunday 6 September

CALENDAR FOR THE WEEK AHEAD
Sunday 23rd Because of Peter’s faith the promise is given to him. It is through faith that the Church at all
times can have trust in the promise of God’s protective power. So encouraged, we can meet the difficulties
and human weaknesses that are part of the Church’s life. The deepening of our faith must be the lifelong
search of every Christian.
Monday 24th St Bartholomew , Apostle. Probably to be identified with Nathanael of Cana, whom Philip
brought to Jesus. Said to have preached the Gospel in India. Patron saint of plasterers, tanners and
leatherworkers.
Tuesday 25th St Louis of France (1214-70). King of France, father of eleven children, Franciscan tertiary,
man of integrity, died in Tunisia on his second crusade.
St Joseph Calasanz (1556-1648) founded the Piarists to run free schools. Patron of Catholic schools.
Thursday 27th St Monica (332-387). Converted her pagan husband, Patricius, to the faith and by her prayers
and her tears brought her son, St Augustine, to Christianity. Patron saint of mothers.
Friday 28th St Augustine (354-430). Bishop of Hippo in North Africa. An outstanding thinker, preacher and
pastoral bishop. One of the most significant theological influences in the history of the western Church.
Patron of theologians.
Saturday 29th The Passion of St John the Baptist . John prepared the way for Christ and bore witness to him
when he came. His martyr’s death was his last and greatest act of witness.

Adoration Chapel
The prayer and presence of anyone who comes to quietly meditate before the Blessed Sacrament is an
encouragement, a witness to fidelity and a gift to the Parish. As of 31st August, our Adoration Chapel is open for
this prayer ministry, for those who faithfully come together to pray the Rosary each evening and to spend time
during exposition of the Blessed Sacrament on Mondays and Wednesdays, or for anyone seeking a sanctuary of
peace. All are welcome.
Our older parishioners will know this space was used for many years by the Sisters from the Convent of Mercy,
who were a praying presence in our parish. Hopefully the warmth and simplicity of the Adoration Chapel will
allow many of us to continue that legacy of prayer, in and on behalf of the needs of others and the needs of our
world.
The Parish is grateful to all who helped in the design and setting up of the little Chapel and for the signage which
points the way and is an invitation to all to ‘come and pray’.

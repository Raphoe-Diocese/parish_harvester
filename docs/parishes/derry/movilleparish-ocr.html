Sunday 22nd At the end of our earthly life we will be judged on how we have lived and acted.

At the end of time the kingdom of God will be established through the saving work of Christ. Then the Shepherd-King will come to judge how we have loved.

Our lives are to be characterised by a love that welcomes Christ in those most in need, and by joyful expectation of his glorious coming.

Monday 23rd St Columban (Columbanus) (543-615), a monk of the monastery of Bangor who left the monastery ‘to go on pilgrimage for Christ’. Remembered as one of the greatest of Irish missionary monks.

He preached the Gospel on mainland Europe, in parts of what is now France, Switzerland, Austria and Northern Italy, where he established several monasteries and centres of learning.

Tuesday 24th St Andrew Dung-Lac and companions. Many Christians suffered martyrdom in Vietnam in the 17-19th centuries.

Wednesday 25th St Colman , bishop. Born around 530, probably in west Cork.

A bard by profession, he became a priest, working in east Cork and establishing his main foundation at Cloyne.

St Catherine of Alexandria . Martyred in 310 in Alexandria; her body is venerated at the monastery on Mount Sinai.

Friday 27th St Fergal (Virgil ). An Irishman, who lived first in France and then in Bavaria, before becoming bishop of Salzburg around 745. He died in 784, leaving a reputation for learning and holiness.

CALENDAR FOR THE WEEK AHEAD
Sunday 13th The Christian community must be seen to be one of forgiveness and mercy. If the Lord is
compassion and love, slow to anger and rich in mercy, who are we to be otherwise? We need t o be conscious
of our own weaknesses and failures so that we may not judge others. We cannot pray the Lord’s Praye r with
unforgiving hearts.
Monday 14th The Exaltation of the Holy Cross . This feast celebrates the Cross both as the instrument of
Christ’s victory over sin in his passing to the Father’s glory and as the means of our salvation.
Tuesday 15th Our Lady of Sorrows. Mary stood by the Cross in solidarity with her Son in his passion,
uniting her sufferings with his and sharing in his offering to the Father.
Wednesday 16th St Cornelius and St Cyprian. Cornelius became Pope in 251 and died in exile as a martyr
in 253. Cyprian, bishop of Carthage, a very influential and revered figure in North Africa, died as a martyr in
258.
Thursday 17th St Robert Bellarmine (1542-1621), Jesuit, bishop, cardinal, and Doctor of the Church, who
was deeply involved in the theological controversies of his time. Patron of catechists.
Friday 18th First Anniversary Mass for Fr. Michael Keaveney at 10am.
Saturday 19th St Januarius , bishop, who was martyred with six companions in Naples in 305.

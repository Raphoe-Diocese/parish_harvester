Centring Prayer Group – Join us on Wednesday at 8:00pm in the St Columb’s Church (Parish Hall) Unlocking the Mystery of the Bible Many of us want to read the Bible but can find it difficult to understand.

The Parish will be running ‘Unlocking the Mystery of the Bible’, an eight-part course that will help us to understand the main story of the Bible and how all the different parts fit together.

It gives the ‘big picture’ and helps us see what the Bible is really about.

We will meet on Monday evenings starting on 2nd November at 8pm (after 7.30pm Mass) in the Parish Hall, St Columb’s Church.

Sessions will begin with prayer, followed by a video presentation and small group discussion.

By the end, you’ll feel more confident reading the Bible and have a better understanding of how you fit into God’s plan.

To register for the course, please leave your name and contact details at the Parish Office (028 71 342303 or email secretary@watersideparish.net ).

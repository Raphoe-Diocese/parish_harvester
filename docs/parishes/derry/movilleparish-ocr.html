CALENDAR FOR THE WEEK AHEAD
Sunday 16th There is a great mystery in the will of God that all will be saved. He knows the minds and hearts
of all and does not judge in a human way. We hold to that belief in the need of membership for salvation, but
we do so in the light of the universality of salvation. Like the Canaanite woman we must persevere with a
determined faith in seeking the truth in love, until all nations learn of God’s salvation and all peoples praise
his name.
Monday 17th Our Lady of Knock. Our Lady is believed to have appeared at the south gable of Knock Parish
Church on 21 August 1879 in the company of St Joseph and St John the Evangelist. There were fifteen
witnesses, young and old, to the apparition. Knock is an internationally recognised Marian shrine and was
visited by Pope St. John Paul II as part of his 1979 pilgrimage to Ireland, and by Pope Francis when he visited
Ireland for the World Meeting of Families in 2018.
Wednesday 19th St John Eudes (1601-1680), from Normandy, spent a fruitful twenty years with the French
Oratory. He founded a congregation to improve the standards of the clergy through seminaries. He also
founded the Sisters of Our Lady of Charity and Refuge. A powerful preacher, he was among the first to
promote devotion to the Sacred Heart.
Thursday 20th St Bernard (1090-1153), abbot and doctor of the Church. Became a Cistercian monk at
Citeaux in 1113 and became abbot of Clairvaux in 1115. A figure of great influence in his day, and even later
through his writings.
Friday 21st St Pius X (1835-1914) became Pope in 1903. He encouraged pastoral liturgy and sacramental
practice, especially of frequent communion to which he admitted young children.
Saturday 22nd The Queenship of Mary . During the Middle Ages, Mary was venerated as queen of the
angels and saints. Pope Pius XII prescribed the memorial for the universal church in 1955, at the close of the
Marian Year. Its place on this date to stress the connection of Mary’s queenship with the Assumption.

Yet even sincere intentions can remain only words. Jesus tells of two sons asked to work in their father’s vineyard. One refu sed but later went ; the other agreed but did not. The story shows that actions matter more than words. True change turns faith into action: the gospel passage invites us to be honest about where we need to grow, to live out kindness and forgiveness, to refuse to be defined by past mistakes, to offer God a willing heart, to examine our conduct, and embrace every opportunity to do good. God values sincere action over empty promises, anditis never too late to begin again. Lord, fill our hearts with Your love and guide us to live our faith each day. Amen.

CALENDAR FOR THE WEEK AHEAD
Sunday 30th As the day of Resurrection, Sunday is not only a remembrance of a past event; it is a celebra tion
of the living presence of the risen Lord in the midst of his own people. For this presence to be properly
proclaimed it is important that the disciples of Christ, saved as the people of God, come together to express
fully the very identity of the Church (Pope St John Paul II).
Monday 31st St Aidan of Lindisfarne was of Irish descent and a monk of Iona. When Oswald, the exiled
king of Northumbria who had fled for refuge to Iona, returned to his throne in 634, he invited Aidan to come
to reconvert his people. Aidan made his headquarters at Lindisfarne. With the a id of the king as interpreter he
was very successful. He died in 651.
Tuesday 1st September Today, the World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation, The Season of Creation
takes place from 1 September to 4 October, bringing Christians throughout th e world together in prayer,
reflection and action for the care of our common home. This year’s theme, Living Water , invites us to give
thanks for the gift of water and renew our care for God’s creation.
Thursday 3rd St Gregory the Great (540-604). A Roman nobleman, who was Prefect of the City before
becoming a monk. He became Pope in 590; he established a pattern for the papacy in the y ears to come and
his influence lasted long after his death. He had a strong influence on the Roman liturgy and he s ent
missionaries to Lombardy, Sardinia and England.
Friday 4th St Mac Nissi. Oengus Mac Nissi took his name from his mother Cne s or Ness. It is claimed that
Patrick baptised him and taught him the psalms. He chose the dist rict of Connor (Co. Antrim) for his hermitage
and later became bishop.
Saturday 5th St Teresa of Calcutta (1910 – 1997), founded the Missionaries of Charity to serve the hungry,
unwanted and dying.

Adoration Chapel
On Monday we open the new Adoration Chapel in St Pius X Church. From now on this is
where we will have the exposition of the Blessed Sac rament each Monday and Wednesday
at the usual times, as well as the recitation of the Rosary and the Divine Mercy Chaplet
each evening. The Adoration Chapel can be accessed a t the rear of the sacristy. Please
follow the direction signs.
I will officially bless the Chapel at the end of th e 10.00am mass on Monday and I hope it will
become a well-used prayer space in the years ahead. The main church will remain open
throughout the daytime for those who wish to come an d spend some time in private prayer,
but with the darker evenings the church will close at 6.00pm whilst the Adoration Chapel
will remain open until the usual finish times each e vening.

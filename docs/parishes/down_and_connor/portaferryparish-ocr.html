Thinking about becoming Catholic or want to deepen your understanding of the faith?

community for adults who wish to explore the teachings of Jesus Christ and the Catholic Chu rch. It
is open to those who are not baptised, those baptised in another Christian tradition, or C atholics

discussion, and time for questions. Spouses, fiancés or friends are welcome to
attend alongside participants.
All are welcome, whether you are exploring faith for the first time or returning after
some time away.

Bereavement Information Evening especially dedicated to supporting surviving siblings
this autumn. As part of our commitment to supporting everyone following the death of a child, we
are exploring opportunities to further support the entire family. This event is the first, hop efully of
many, of that commitment. This event is open to all parents whose child or childre n have died,
regardless of the age of their child, circumstances of their death, or time since bereav ement. We
are also opening this Bereavement Information Evening to bereaved adult siblings ove r 18.
This event will take place online on Tuesday 15 September at 7pm .
We are joined by an exceptional panel of local organisations and experts in sibling bereavement.
Michelle Hartnett is a senior play therapist, systemic practitioner, and bereavement specialist
currently working with LauraLynn, Ireland's Children's Hospice, with over 15 years' experience
supporting families through serious illness, grief, and loss. Michelle holds an MSc in Systemic
Therapy and an MSc in Bereavement Studies and is a Grief Consultant with Middlesex Un iversity.
She also holds a postgraduate certificate and diploma in Play Therapy from NUI Galway.
Anna May is founder of The Student Grief Network and Executive Head of Grief Programm es at
Kaplan International. She lost her brother as a child and her dad as a young adu lt and is
passionate about improving the support that people get following a bereav ement. Anna is also a
writer and artist, interested in how creativity can help us explore difficult human experiences and
build more connected ways of living.
We are also joined by Childhood Cancer Ireland, Jacinta's Smile, and Rainbows.
In the world of grief and loss, siblings, especially adult siblings, are often called ‘forgo tten
mourners.’ Siblings face not only their own loss, but often the loss of their parents and families as
they knew them, the loss of the only person who was present of their whole life, and ofte n the loss
of a close friend. We believe this is why it is essential to begin forming an all-island bereav ement
support for adult siblings facing all kinds of sibling loss. We hope you can join us in this e ffort by
sharing about this Bereavement Information Evening with your community.

PORTAFERRY AND BALLYGALGET BAPTISM MINISTRY
Our newly commissioned Parish Baptism Team is a voluntary ministry that supports
parents, families and Priests as they prepare to celebrate the Sacrament of Baptism.
Central to their role is ensuring that every family experiences their Parish as a place of genuine
welcome and encouraging faith within the home beyond the day of their Baptism. Our Baptism
Ministers will help parents understand the meaning of the Rite and what it involves by:
• Welcoming parents and children into the Parish community.
• Providing a preparation session for parents (and Godparents if they wish to attend).
• Offering guidance on the symbols, prayers and commitments involved in the Sacrament.
• Helping parents participate more fully in the celebration on the day of Baptism.
May the time of preparation for Baptism be filled with peace, gratitude, and the loving presence of
God in our families.

Join us for the inaugural Féile tours of St Peter’s Cathedral, led by Stevie Corr, as we
celebrate its 160th anniversary!
Officially opened with great ceremony on 14 October 1866, St Peter’s Cathedral, with its iconic
twin spires, has stood at the heart of West Belfast ever since.
This special guided tour offers a unique opportunity to explore areas of the Cathedral that are rich
in history and significance, including:
The Chapel of the Resurrection, where three Bishops of Down and Connor are laid to rest.
The Sanctuary, home to the Bishop’s Cathedra (official seat).
The Aumbry, which houses the Holy Oils donated in memory of Fr Hugh Mullan and Fr Noel
Fitzpatrick, who lost their lives while anointing the wounded during the Ballymurphy shootings.
Tour dates: Monday 3 August at 3pm , Tuesday 4 August at 3 pm, Wednesday 5 August at 3pm
Thursday 6 August at 3pm
Tickets: £5 per person , payable at the entrance (please bring the exact amount).
Come and discover the history, heritage, and stories of one of Belfast’s most treasured landmarks.

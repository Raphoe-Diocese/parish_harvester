PORTAFERRY AND BALLYGALGET BAPTISM MINISTRY
Our newly commissioned Parish Baptism Team is a voluntary ministry that supports
parents, families and Priests as they prepare to celebrate the Sacrament of Baptism.
Central to their role is ensuring that every family experiences their Parish as a place of genuine
welcome and encouraging faith within the home beyond the day of their Baptism. Our Baptism
Ministers will help parents understand the meaning of the Rite and what it involves by:
• Welcoming parents and children into the Parish community.
• Providing a preparation session for parents (and Godparents if they wish to attend).
• Offering guidance on the symbols, prayers and commitments involved in the Sacrament.
• Helping parents participate more fully in the celebration on the day of Baptism.
May the time of preparation for Baptism be filled with peace, gratitude, and the loving presence of
God in our families.

Bereavement Information Evening especially dedicated to supporting surviving siblings
this autumn. As part of our commitment to supporting everyone following the death of a child, we
are exploring opportunities to further support the entire family. This event is the first, hopefully of
many, of that commitment. This event is open to all parents whose ch ild or children have died,
regardless of the age of their child, circumstances of their death, or time since bereavement. We
are also opening this Bereavemen t Information Evening to bereaved adult siblings over 18.
This event will take place online on Tuesday 15 September at 7pm .
We are joined by an exceptional panel of local organisations and experts in sibling bereavement.
Michelle Hartnett is a senior play therapist, systemic practitioner, and bereavement specialist
currently working with LauraLynn, Ireland's Children's Hospice, with over 15 years' experience
supporting families through serious illness, grief, and loss. Mi chelle holds an MSc in Systemic
Therapy and an MSc in Bereavement Studies and is a Grief Consultant with Middlesex University.
She also holds a postgraduate certificate and diploma in Play Therapy from NUI Galway.
Anna May is founder of The Student Grief Network and Executive Head of Grief Programmes at
Kaplan International. She lost her brother as a child and her dad as a young adult and is
passionate about improving the support that people get following a bereavement. Anna is also a
writer and artist, interested in how creativity can help us explore difficult human experiences and
build more connected ways of living.
We are also joined by Childhood Cancer Ireland, Jacinta's Smile, and Rainbows.
In the world of grief and loss, siblings, especially adult siblings, are often called ‘forgotten
mourners.’ Siblings face not only their own loss, but often the loss of their parents and families as
they knew them, the loss of the only person who was prese nt of their whole life, and often the loss
of a close friend. We believe this is why it is essential to begin forming an all -island bereavement
support for adult siblings facing all kinds of sibling loss. We hope you can join us in this effort by
sharing a bout this Bereavement Information Evening with your community.

RELICS OF THE APOSTLES & MARTYRS The parish of Newtownards & Comber has the privilege of being the custodian of relics of the Holy Apostles, Martrys of the 2nd & 3rd centuries, in addition to other relics on temporary loan to the parish . As we will celebrate the Feast Day of the Holy Apostles Saints Simon and Jude this coming Wednesday, 28th October, the relics will be on display in the reliquary in St Patrick’s Church.

They will also be on display for the Feast of All Saints 31st Oct/1st November.

Those attending Mass are invited to venerate the relics after Mass.

We ask for the prayers of the apostles and martyrs and all the saints.

Enroute there would be stops near Ballytrustan Graveyard, at St. Cooey's Wells, n ear the Mass Rock at Ballyedock and back to The Lourdes Grotto.

At each stop the pa rticipants could pray for the Holy Souls, if they wish.

Everything would be done from the public road apart froma visit to the Wells where toilets will be available.

To avoid a large bunch of people, the walkers would setoff in small groups of 4 or 5 at five- minute intervals from St. Patrick’s.

This will not be a large procession of pilgrims but, as stated, small groups of 4 or 5 friends.

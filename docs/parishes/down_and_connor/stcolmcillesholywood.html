Wishing Joel Every Success
Next Sunday, we will be saying goodbye to Joel as he begins an exciting
new chapter in his career in Hospitality.
We would like to wish Joel every happiness and success in his new role.
While we are delighted for him and this wonderful opportunity, we are
also very sorry to see him leave. Joel has been an excellent and much -
valued member of our parish team for this past year, and his
contribution, dedication and friendly presence have been greatly
appreciated. He will be very much missed by us all, and we hope he
leaves knowing how grateful we are for all he has done.
Thank you, Joel, and every good wish for the future!
As we say goodbye to Joel, we are looking for someone within the
parish who may be able to help with some general duties, including
opening and closing the church particularly at the weekends,
along with some light work inside the church.
There is a small retainer for this help, if you are retired or have some
time to spare, we would be delighted to hear from you.
Please contact Cla ire in the Parish Office
for more information.

Sunday 16th August 2026
Almighty ever -living God, who assumed the Immaculate Virgin Mary,
the Mother of your Son, body and soul into heavenly glory,

we may merit to be sharers of her glory. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
God, for ever and ever.

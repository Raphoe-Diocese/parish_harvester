Alleluia! Gospel: Matthew 25:1-13HOW CAN I HELP? Could you play a part in the life of our parish?

Our Parish Engagement Group is launching a new campaign, “How Can I Help?”, inviting more parishioners to become involved in the life and liturgy of St Colmcille’s.

Our parish is blessed with so many people who generously give their time and talents, but there i s always room for more!

We would particularly love to welcome: New Readers, New Extraordinary Ministers of Holy Communion New Choir Members New Welcomers New Altar Servers.

This ministry is open to all young people from Confirmation age up to 18 years old.

We would especially love to see some girls getting involved, as we currently have no female altar servers.

You don't need any previous experience. All that's needed is a willingness to give a little of your time.

Guidance and support will be provided. Our parish isn't simply somewhere we come to Mass.

It's a community to which we all belong, and each of us has something to offer.

So perhaps the question to ask yourself is:“How can I help?” Please take a moment this week to fill out one of the short forms and return it either to a member of the Parish Engagement Group or place it in the box at the back of the church.

Every offer of help, no matter how small, can make a real difference to the life of our parish.

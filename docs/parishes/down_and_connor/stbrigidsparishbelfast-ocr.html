Season of Creation 2026 (1 September – 4 October). Join us …
The Season of Creation is the annual Christian celebration which invites us t o respond in
hope and commit to positive action meeting the challenge and responsib ility to care for
creation - our common home. The challenge has been underlined by Pope Francis in
Laudato Si’ (2015) Laudato Deum (2023) and Pope Leo XIV in his message for Wo rld Day
of Prayer for Care of Creation 2025 .
As we journey through the Season of Creation from 1 September through to the feast-day
of St Francis of Assisi on 4 October, let us each take one action to care for our plan et, our
common home.
The theme for this season is ‘Living Water’ symbolised by a small stream flowing from
the Temple of God - growing into a deep and powerful river. Every act of care for creation
becomes part of a global stream of hope” (Living Water Season of Creation 2026).
What action will we take in this season to show care for our planet, our common home?
Action an Eco - tip
• Endeavour to buy locally sourced food on one shopping visit

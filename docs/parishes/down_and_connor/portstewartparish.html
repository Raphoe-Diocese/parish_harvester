24 Hours of Adoration on the
Feast of Our Lady's Assumption
To honour the Feast of Our Lady's Assumption the Legion of
Mary are organising 24 hours of Eucharistic Adoration in St
Agnes Church.
Adoration will begin after the 7pm Mass on Friday 14th
August and will continue throughout the night and
throughout the day, ending 6.30 vigil Mass on 15th August.

earth". Please come along and give thanks to Our Lady and
her Son Jesus.

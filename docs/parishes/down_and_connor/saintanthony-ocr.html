Farewell by Fr Henry McCann. This Sunday 9th August will
mark the last Sunday Mass celebrated by me in the parish
of St Anthony, as its parish priest. I thank God for 12 fond
and fulfilling years I have spent working with parishioners
here. I have been privileged to help parishioners to come to
a deeper knowledge of the faith that has sustained many in
this parish and church of St Anthony, since its opening in
1938, and its subsequent formation as a parish outright in
1955. I have acknowledged the various previous parish
priests who have supported the faith community. I have seen
the pride with which parishioners have supported this parish
and church remembering its difficult years during the
Troubles. I have acknowledged the deep faith of the
remnant of those parishioners, and the Polish community
who has also found it a home, fostering their language,
customs and traditions which are witnesses to their deep
faith as well. I have witnessed the many other nationals who
have come to find it their home, from Eastern European
nationals, Philippino, Indian, Goan, African, Chinese,
Portugese, and many others besides. We have witnessed
together the 80th Anniversary of the Opening of St Anthony’s
in 1918 by Bishop Noel Treanor and the commissioning of
the then Pastoral Council. We have coped with the recent
Covid-19 Pandemic, which has led to the broadcasting of
mass on Facebook Live. I have found the support of
parishioners and visitors to the parish as something alive
and faith-filled, encouraging my ministry and vocation. And
hopefully in the guidance of parishioners and visitors to St
Anthony’s they have found in me a loyal and faithful
shepherd after the heart of Jesus the Good Shepherd. I
continue to be the Chaplain to the Ulster Hospital,
Dundonald, as I move to the parish of St Malachy, in the city
centre, on the weekend of 15th August, but I hope not to be
stranger, as we as clergy continue to work in the family of
Parishes of St Anthony’s, St Malachy’s, St Matthew’s, St
Colmcille’s (Ballyhackamore) and St Colmcille’s (Holywood).
I pray that the parish can cope with the absence of a resident
priest, and give support to Fr Peter Carlin, parish priest of St
Matthew’s as he prepares to take up the pastoral leadership
of what was its daughter parish. I am certain that you will
welcome and give Fr Carlin the same support and prayers
as you have given to me. I wish you every blessing from
God as we mark a further stage along the history of the
parish of St Anthony’s and in the life of the diocese. May the
of parishioners and visitors continue to be strengthened and
nourished into the future. God bless you all. Fr Henry.

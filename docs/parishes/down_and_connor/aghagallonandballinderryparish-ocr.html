The Annual Produce Swap is being held from 3pm to 4pm on Sunday 11 October 2026 in the Cranagh Hall. The produce swap is one of three events that have been taking place in the Cranagh Hall over the past few years. It starts with the seed swap in March where people can exchange fruit, vegetable, flower seeds etc.atthe start of the growing season. Then the seedling swap in March when people can exchange plants etc. and then finally the produce swap in October where people can swap anything that they have grown or produced such as jams etc. Everyone is welcome. MINISTRY OF WELCOME Booking Masses, Baptisms and Weddings As parish office opening hours have been reduced slightly, we are returning to our previous practice of booking Masses, baptis ms and weddings in person, in the sacristy after Masses. Masses can be booked well in advance to ensure your preferred dates ar e available. Baptisms can only be booked by the child’s parent or legal guardian. At least o ne month’s notice is required. To book a baptism parents must present the child’s long birth certificate , complete the baptism request form, meet with one of our Baptism team and attend weekly Mass during the month prior to the date of the Baptism Weddings can be booked by the couple themselves. Couples are required to give at least six months’ notice of marriage to ensure the preferred date is available and that there is adequate time to complete all Civil and Church paperwork.

Baptisms can only be booked by the child’s parent or legal guardian. At least one month’s notice is
required. To book a baptism parents must present the child’s long birth cert ificate , complete the
baptism request form, meet with one of our Baptism team and attend weekly Mass during the
month prior to the date of the Baptism
Weddings can be booked by the couple themselves. Couples are required to give at least six

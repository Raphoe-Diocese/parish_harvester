Booking Masses, Baptisms and Weddings As parish office opening hours have been reduced
slightly, we are returning to our previous practice of booking Masses, baptis ms and weddings in
person, in the sacristy after Masses.
Masses can be booked well in advance to ensure your preferred dates ar e available.
Baptisms can only be booked by the child’s parent or legal guardian. At least o ne month’s notice is
required. To book a baptism parents must present the child’s long birth certificate , complete the
baptism request form, meet with one of our Baptism team and attend weekly Mass during the
month prior to the date of the Baptism
Weddings can be booked by the couple themselves. Couples are require d to give at least six
months’ notice of marriage to ensure the preferred date is available and that there is adequate time
to complete all Civil and Church paperwork.

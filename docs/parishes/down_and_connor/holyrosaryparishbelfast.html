Parish Vision Statement :
Christ’s love, working within us, invites us to live for
him and to work to make his name known and loved.
Our Christian Community in Kilmore and Killyleagh
seeks to be a place where everyone:
• Feels welcomed
• Feels safe
• Feels listened to
• Feels respected
• Experiences a sense of belonging
• Deepens thei r understanding of our Catholic
Faith and traditions in a place of joyful and
prayerful worship
• Actively walks together as brothers and
sisters inspired by God’s love
• Reaches out to others who are not engaged
in Church life
• Supports those who may be in need including
the bereaved
• Experiences God in the wonder of creation
and seeks to care more proactively for it.

SEASON OF CREATION 2026
Each year, from 1st September to 4th October, Christians
around the world unite in this special season of prayer
and action to care for our common home. It is a time to
celebrate God as Creator and to reflect on our shared
vocation to protect and cherish all of creation.
This year's theme, "Liv ing Water," is inspired by
Ezekiel 47:9, 12 - a powerful biblical vision of hope and
ecological renewal.

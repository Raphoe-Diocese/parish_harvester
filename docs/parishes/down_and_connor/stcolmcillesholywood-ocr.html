Lay Bereavement Ministry – Volunteers Needed
Parishioners are invited to consider becoming
Lay Bereavement Ministers, a new ministry replacing
Bethany Bereavement . Ministers offer compassionate
support to those who are grieving through visits, telephone contact,
group support and parish remembrance events. We are looking for
people with good listening and communication skills, a caring and non-
judgemental approach, reliability, and an active parish faith. A minimum
team of 4 people is required, with ministry carried out in pairs. Teams can
also work across a Family of Parishes. Training: 29 September –14
November at the Diocesan Offices, 120 Cliftonville Road, Belfast.
Attendance at all six sessions is required. Application deadline: 12
noon, Friday 18 September . Places are limited. Further information is on
our website www.stcolmcillesholywood.org

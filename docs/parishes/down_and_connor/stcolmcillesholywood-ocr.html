Opening Mass for the Season
of Creation 6pm on
Sunday 6th September
St. Patrick’s Church, Donegall Street, Belfast :
Celebrant: Fr Piaras Jackson SJ, with interfaith contributions. This Mass
marks the beginning of the Season of Creation (1 Sept –4 Oct), a time
when Christians worldwide commit to prayer and action for our
common home under the theme “Living Water”. 1st September is the
World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation. In his message for the
occasion, Pope Leo said, “The prophetic call to “beat swords into
ploughshares” ( Is 2:4) is (.) not only a vision for nations, but a call to
each person. It invites us to transform harm into life. Yet this
transformation cannot be achieved through external change alone. It
requires, through cooperating with God’s grace, a deeper person al
and collective conversion — a returning to God, who will reorder our
desires, heal our wounds and help us grow in virtue.” Further
information on the Season of Creation can be found on our website.
Lay Bereavement Ministry – Volunteers Needed
Parishioners are invited to consider becoming
Lay Bereavement Ministers, a new ministry
replacing Bethany Bereavement . Ministers offer
compassionate support to those who are grieving through visits,
telephone contact, group support and parish remembrance events.
We are looking for people with good listening and communication
skills, a caring and non-judgemental approach, reliability, and an
active parish faith. A minimum team of 4 people is required, with
ministry carried out in pairs. Teams can also work across a Family of
Parishes. Training: 29 September –14 November at the Diocesan

required. Application deadline: 12 noon, Friday 18 September .

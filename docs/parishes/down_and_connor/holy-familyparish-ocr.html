Sat 15: The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin
St Attracta was born into a noble family in County Sligo in the fifth century. She desired
to consecrate her life to God, so Attracta fled home and sought out St Patrick, from
whom she received the veil as a consecrated virgin. In Killaraght, Attracta established a
monastery, an oratory, and most notably a hospice for travellers, pilgrims, the poor, and the sick.

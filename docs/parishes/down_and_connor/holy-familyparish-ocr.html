Heavenly Father,
Thank You for walking with me through every day of this summer. Thank You for the
sunshine, the peaceful mornings, the laughter of loved ones, and every blessing t hat
often went unnoticed. Even during difÏcult moments, You never left my side. Y ou
gave me strength when I was tired, comfort when I felt worried, and hope when I

As this season comes to an end, I choose to remember Your goodness, inst ead of my
disappointments. Help me keep a thankful heart in every season of life. May I ne ver

the summer months, and help me continue sharing Your love with everyone I meet.
In Jesus ’ name, Amen.
The Season of Creation is celebrated each year from September 1 (the World
Day of Prayer for Creation) to October 4 (the Feast of Saint Francis of Assisi).
This season is celebrated in all Christian Churches around the world & it invites
us all to renew our participation in our vocation as custodians of God ’s creation.
For 2026, the official theme is “Living Water ”, which is inspired by the biblical
vision found in the Book of Ezekiel (47:9, 12). The passage describes water flow-
ing from God ’s sanctuary, bringing life and restoration to ecosystems and com-
munities.

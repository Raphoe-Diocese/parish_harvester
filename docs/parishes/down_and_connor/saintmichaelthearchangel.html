St Michael the Archangel Parish Golden Jubilee
Celebrations
To celebrate the 50th Anniversary of St Michael
the Archangel Parish, we invite our parishioners
and parish family to save the following dates as
we look forward to 5 days of Jubilee events this
September .
Jubilee Masses
Saturday 26th September at 5.00pm
Sunday 27th September at 11.00am
These special Jubilee Masses will be followed by
refreshments in the Parish Hall.
Jubilee Parish Mission
Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday 28th & 30th
September at 7.00pm, with Mass each evening.
We are delighted to welcome Michelle Moran as
our Jubilee Mission speaker.
Michelle Moran is an internationally renowned
Catholic speaker, evangelist and co -founder of
the Sion Community.
Michelle will also share with our school pupils
and staff over those 3 days encouraging them in
their faith journey.
Further events will take place throughout our
Jubilee Year, including a pilgrimage to St
Patrick’s Country in Downpatrick next year. Details of these and other celebrations will be
announced in due course.
Please keep these dates free and join us as we
give thanks for 50 years of parish life and look
forward with hope to the future !

MATT TALBOT – URBAN MYSTIC
The Knights of St Columbanus, CK12
Downpatrick , invite you to a special screening of
Matt Talbot – Urban Mystic at Newcastle
Community Cinema, St Mary's Hall, Main Street,
Newcastle , on Friday 4th September at 7.00pm .
Matt Talbot's remarkable true story of faith,
struggle, addiction and redemption continues to
inspire people throughout the world and offers a
powerful message of hope to those affected by
addiction and their families.
The evening will also feature guest speaker
Michael Murphy .
Admission is free , with a suggested donation of
£3 towards costs . Tea and coffee will be served
following the screening.
Everyone is very welcome – please bring family
and friends.

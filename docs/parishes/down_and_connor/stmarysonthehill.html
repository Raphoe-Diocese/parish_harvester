IMPORTANT NOTICE
THERE WILL BE NO ACCESS TO ST BERNARD’S SITE
3rd-14th AUGUST
St Bernard’s site , both upper and lower carpark,
WILL BE CLOSED
for re -tarmacking and re -marking from
Monday 3rd – Friday 14th August 2026.
There will be NO ACCESS FOR CARS OR PEDESTRIANS .
There will be NO PARKING AVAILABLE ON SITE .
There will be NO ACCESS TO THE CHURCH,
THE PASTORAL CENTRE OR THE SCHOOL.
Daily masses (Monday -Saturday mornings 9.45am)
and funerals will be celebrated in St Marys on the Hill .
However, because St Mary ’s is too small
to accommodate our Sunday congregations
the Saturday Vigil 6pm and Sunday morning masses
9am and 11am on 8th & 9th August will be online .
Parishioners , who wish to, are welcome to attend masses
in the neighbour ing parishes
St Gerard ’s Vigil 6.30pm Morning 10.30am ,
Whitehouse vigil 5pm
St Nicholas’ Carrickfergus 11.30am
- note these could be quite full.
Adoration will be temporarily suspended during this period.
Apologies , in advance , for the inconvenience.

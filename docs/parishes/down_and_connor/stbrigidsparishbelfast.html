Women’s Prayer Group
Are you looking to find a closer relationship with God?
If so, St Brigid’s Women’s Prayer Group may be for you.
We are a group of women of all ages who come together once a month. It’s a very gentle
hour in which we study a passage of scripture and pray. It’s a community that continues
to grow, developing relationship with not only God, but with one another too.
We meet the third Wednesday of each month, for about an hour, in the Parish Offi ce at
7.30pm. We start with a cup of tea and a chat. There is always wisdom, suppor t and good
humour to be found amongst us. If you decide to join us you will receive the warmest of
welcomes .

December.

Season of Creation 2026 (1 September – 4 October). Join us …
The Season of Creation is the annual Christian celebration which invites us t o respond in
hope and commit to positive action meeting the challenge and responsib ility to care for
creation - our common home. As we journey through the Season of Creation from 1
September through to the feast-day of St Francis of Assisi on 4 Octo ber, let us each take
one action to care for our planet, our common home.
What action will we take in this season to show care for our planet, our common home?
Action an Eco - tip
• Endeavour to buy locally sourced food on one shopping visit
• Make one journey on foot or use public transport
• Select one day and turn off all non- essential lights
Start the conversation with family and friends – what can we do to care for our
common home? Involve young people in our conversations – their voice matters - they
will inherit the planet that we create.
Read and consider what others are saying
Keep informed and join wider discussions by visiting info@jointhedotstogether.org
Read My Appeal (paragraphs 13 - 16) Laudato Si’ - On Care for Our Common Home - visit
Contribute your ideas and talents
Join our parish ‘Care for Common Home’ group to share ideas on eco -tips and suggestions
for future talks/events — contact through the Parish Office.
Let’s rise to the challenge
Pope Frances had every confidence that we can make change happen.
‘All of us can cooperate as instruments of God for the care of creation, each according to
his or her own culture, experience, involvements and talents’ (LS14).

participants. Further details are on our website.
Lay Bereavement Ministry – Volunteers Needed
Parishioners are invited to consider becoming
Lay Bereavement Ministers, a new ministry replacing
Bethany Bereavement . Ministers offer compassionate
support to those who are grieving through visits, telephone contact,
group support and parish remembrance events. We are looking for
people with good listening and communication skills, a caring and non-
judgemental approach, reliability, and an active parish faith. A minimum
team of 4 people is required, with ministry carried out in pairs. Teams can
also work across a Family of Parishes. Training: 29 September –14
November at the Diocesan Offices, 120 Cliftonville Road, Belfast.
Attendance at all six sessions is required. Application deadline: 12
noon, Friday 18 September . Places are limited. Further information is on
our website www.stcolmcillesholywood.org

Confirmation Preparation Classes 2026/2027
Enrolment for St Brigid’s Parish Confirmation Preparation Classes 2026/27 is
now open to Year 7 pupils, who are not attending St Bride’s Primary School,
and Year 8 pupils. Each class lasts one hour, 5.45pm -6.45pm, on Tuesdays in
St. Brigid’s Parish Hall. The first class will be 5.45pm -6.45pm on Tuesday 8th
September.
Each pupil intending to enroll in the preparation classes must have received
their First Holy Communion and be able to attend all the one hour sessions.
The limited places available will be awarded on the first come first served

St Vincent de Paul – Christmas Appeal 2026 The St Comgall’s and St Joseph’s SVP Conferences will launch their annual Christmas Appeal at all Masses this weekend, 7th & 8th November.

At this time of year, we are once again asking for the support of our pari shioners to help children, families and individuals within our local community who are experiencing hardship.

Donations of gift vouchers or money are greatly appreciated.

Gift vouchers are particularly valuable as they allow families to choose what they need most and pu rchase their own Christmas gifts, while maintaining choice, dignity and respect.

How to Donate Vouchers: Vouchers can be placed in an envelope marked “St Comgall’s SVP” or “St Joseph’s SVP” and left at either parochial house or in the baskets at the back of the church after Mass.

We kindly ask that all vouchers are received by 22nd November , giving families time to purchase what they need before Christmas.

How to Make a Cash Donation: We understand that not everyone carries cash, so there are several ways to contribute: SVP Collection Boxes – Available at the end of each Mass.

Debit or Credit Card Payments – Scan the barcode displayed on the collection boxes ( St Comgall’s Conference only ).

Bank Transfer – Use the details below: St Comgall’s Conference Account Name: Society of St Vincent de Paul St Comgall’s Antrim Sort Code: 95-02-02 Account No: 71005294 St Joseph’s Conference Account Name: Society of St Vincent de Paul St Joseph’s Antrim Sort Code: 98-01-80 Account No: 02344005 By Phone – Call 028 9075 0161 , available 24/7. Please nominate St Comgall’s Antrim Conference or St Joseph’s Antrim Conference . Online – Visit the SVP donations page, enter your amount, select North Region , and specify St Comgall’s Antrim or St Joseph’s Antrim in the drop-down menu.

SVP Christmas Cards are also available at the back of St Comgall’s Church.

The cards are free, although donations are very welcome.

Finally, we would like to say a sincere thank you to the parishioners of Antrim Parish for your continued kindness and generosity throughout the year.

Your support makes a real difference to people and families in our own community, particularly at Christmas.

Thank you for helping us to help others this Christmas.

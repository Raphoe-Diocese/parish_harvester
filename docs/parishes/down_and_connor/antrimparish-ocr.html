WELCOME TO OUR NEW PARISH PRIEST
Rev Patsy Mulholland begins his ministry in our Parish this weekend and we extend a
very warm welcome to him. Please keep him in your prayers that God will continue to
bless him in his priesthood especially while he serves in the Parish of Antrim.
The Medical Missionaries of Mary have been invited to visit us in Down and Connor
this year and will be with us here at Antrim Parish on the weekend of 5th and
6th September 2026. They will speak of their work, mainly in Africa and South America,
and their mission to bring Christ’s healing love to those most in need. Our parish and our
diocese have supported MMM generously in the past, both in finance and in prayer , and
MMM wishes to express gratitude for that support and to emphasise that we here at home
are the body of mission, and those who are sent to far away places are the hands and the
feet. When they speak to us and share their story they serve as a connecti on between us
and those whom they serve.

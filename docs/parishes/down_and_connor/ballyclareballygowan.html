May God bless them and all the priests taking up new appointments in our diocese this week.Parish of Ballymoney& DerrykeighanCAUSEWAY FAMILY OF PARISHES16th August 2026 Twentieth Sunday of Ordinary TimeGREAT FAITH, NO BOUNDARIES Today's Gospel ﬁnds Jesus seemingly reluctant to help a Canaanite woman pleading for her daughter, telling her it wouldn't be right to give "the children's bread" to "the dogs." It's very strange - until we see what the Lord is really doing. He's teaching his apostles, who had brushed the woman aside as a foreign outsider. Through their back-and-forth, Jesus reveals her deep faith and heals her daughter, showing the Twelve that God's love has no borders of race or nation.It's a lesson for us too. As Isaiah foretold, God's house is "a house of prayer for all peoples." We're called to carry that welcome beyond our church doors — meeting everyone with respect, and speaking up against the prejudice and division still so present in our world.

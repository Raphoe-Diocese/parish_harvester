WORLD MISSIONS SUNDAY-18th October This weekend we celebrate the 100th Anniversary of World Missions Sunday, an opportunity to grow in awareness of the crucial importance of the Church’s work on the mission fields.

Many missionaries work in difficult and dangerous situations. 17 Catholic missionaries died tragically in 2025, 10 of them in Africa.

The missionaries who were killed included 6 priests and 2 catechists.

Missionaries carry out their important work in obedience to the commission given by Our Lord: “ Go and baptize all the nations …. know that I am with you, yes, until the end of time 28) In his message for Mission Sunday this year, Pope Leo XIV states: On World Mission Day 2026, which marks the centenary of a celebration established by Pius XI that is very dear to the Church, I have chosen the theme “One in Christ, united in mission.” Following the Jubilee Year, I wish to encourage the whole Church to continue its missionary journey with joy and zeal in the Holy Spirit.

This requires hearts united in Christ, reconciled communities and, in everyone, a willingness to cooperate with generosity a nd trust. As we reflect on being one in Christ and united in mission, let us allow ourselves to be guided and inspired by divine grace, “to renew in ourselv es the fire of our missionary vocation” and advance together in the commitment to evangelization, in this “new missionary age” in the history of the Church ( Homily, Jubilee of the Missionary World and of Migrants , 5 October 2025).

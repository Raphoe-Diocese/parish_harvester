Bulletin Reflection 24th Sunday in Ordinary Time – 12th & 13th Sept. 2026 All politics is local, it is said. The Church is local in the community of Holy Cross parish. The message of Jesus come down to us per sonally answering His Word today before weaskhow other people or places aredoing. The Word of Godis struggling to be heard amid thenoiseofwarand destruction in so many places. There is more of‘aneyeforaneyeanda tooth for a tooth’ asinthe Old Testament. Instead listen to Jesus today speaking tousandtoall people: “If a person nurses anger against another, can they then demand compassion from the Lord?” [1st Reading at Mass today] The answer must be NO. “The life and death of each ofushasits influence on others.” [2nd Reading at Mass today] We are Influencers whether we know it ornot. Itis Godwho wants us to influence for good in a World ofevil. “Lord, how often must I forgive another if they wrong me?” [Gospel at Mass today] Peter resorted to mathematics inhis question to Jesus with his ‘how often’. He needed to be reminded of the astonishing promise of Jesus , “Whatever you loose on earth shall be considered loosed in Heaven,” The Sacrament of Reconciliation [Confession] puts this within reach of each of us. Our Eternal Destiny is decided onhow I : 1) Forgive others. 2) Work for deep reconciliation with others and 3) Render humble a nd living service to others. We have alottodo, letusget started and take once more to heart the Word of God. This is the only hope for our World anditisourown personal Key to Heaven. Aidan Troy, C. P . S. V. D.

Opening Mass for the Season of Creation
6.00 pm on Sunday, 6th September in St. Patrick ’s Church,
Donegall Street, Belfast
Celebrant: Fr Piaras Jackson SJ.
This Mass marks the beginning of the Season of Creation (1
Sept– 4 Oct), a time when Christians worldwide commit to
prayer and action for our common home under the theme
“Living Water ”. 1st September was the World Day of Prayer
for the Care of Creation. In his message for the occasion, Pope
Leo said, “The prophetic call to “beat swords into
ploughshares ” (Is 2:4) is (.) not only a vision for nations, but a call
to each person. It invites us to transform harm into life. Yet this
transformation cannot be achieved through external change alone. It
requires, through cooperating with God ’s grace, a deeper personal
and collective conversion — a returning to God, who will reorder
our desires, heal our wounds and help us grow in virtue. ”

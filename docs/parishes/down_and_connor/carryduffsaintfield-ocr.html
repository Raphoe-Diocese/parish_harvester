Season of Creation 2026 1 September–4 October
As we prepare to celebrate the first Sunday during the Season of Creation 2026, let us take
action—individually and collectively as parish communities—to care for ‘our common
home’.
The Season of Creation is the annual Christian celebration which invites us to respond in
hope and commit to positive action in meeting the challenge and responsibility of caring for
creation—‘our common home’. This challenge has been underlined by Pope Franci s in
Laudato Si’ (2015) and Laudate Deum (2023), and by Pope Leo XIV in his message for the
World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation 2026.
As we journey through the Season of Creation, which commenced on 1 September and
continues to the feast day of St Francis of Assisi on 4 October—also marking the 800th
anniversary of Saint Francis’s death, his Transitus—let us each take one action to show c are
for our planet, ‘our common home’.
Living Water
The theme for the 2026 season is ‘Living Water’, symbolised by a small stream flowing from
the Temple of God and growing into a deep and powerful river. ‘Every act of care for creation
becomes part of a global stream of hope.’ Christians worldwide are called t o address water
scarcity, pollution, freshwater damage and climate justice.
What action will we take this season to show care for our planet, our common home?
Put an eco-tip into action
· Endeavour to buy locally sourced food on one shopping visit.
· Make one journey on foot or use public transport.
· Select one day and turn off all non-essential lights.
· Join the ‘Waterwise’ webinar on 10 September, from 12.00 to 13.00, for simple and
practical ways to save water at home.
Let’s rise to the challenge
Pope Francis had every confidence that we can make change happen.
‘All of us can cooperate as instruments of God for the care of creation, each according to his
or her own culture, experience, involvements and talents’ (LS 14).

ST. PATRICK'S INDOOR BOWLING CLUB
Our Bowling Club will commence bowling on Sunday 13th
September at 7.00 pm in St. Patrick's Hall, Portrush. This
year the League has been restructured into 3 Sections
meaning there will be fewer Inter-club matches and more
nights for social bowling. Anyone wishing to join the Club
come along on Sunday 13th or any Sunday that suits. If
you are new to the sport, we have Bowls available, and
members will make you welcome.
BLESSING OF ANIMALS
Once again, our Family of Parishes will be marking the
Church's Season of Creation with a blessing of pets and
animals. This year the blessing takes place in the grounds
of Our Lady & St Patrick's in Ballymoney on Sunday
27th September at 2pm. All welcome to attend.
We are blessed to be able to offer two opportunities to
our parish community to honour Our Blessed Mother
Mary. Parishioners are warmly invited to join either
pilgrimage, depending on what suits your schedule and
spiritual needs. Both are beautiful opportunities to enjoy
time away from the noise and busyness of our lives and
immerse ourselves into a day of prayer and reflection.

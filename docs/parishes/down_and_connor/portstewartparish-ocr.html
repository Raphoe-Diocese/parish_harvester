LITTLE STARS’ BABY AND TODDLER GROUP
Our Parish Little Stars’ baby and toddler group will
be back on Tuesday 1 September 10.00-11.30am
and every Tuesday in term time in St. Mary’s, Star of the
Sea Parish Centre, Portstewart. Parents, grandparents,
carers and little ones are all invited for playtime, chat, a
friendly cuppa and the best scones in Portstewart!
We will have a warm welcome for all, lots of fun toys and
activities to occupy the little ones and refreshments for
both kids and adults are provided. There is a voluntary
donation of £2 per family to cover costs. We have a great
team of volunteers who help create a lovely environment
for everyone and it’s a fun way to give of your time. If
you’d like to join our team , contact Emer on 07709043921
or emer.m.okeeffe@gmail.com
FLAME CLUB IS NEARLY BACK!
We are excited that our Parish Youth Club
Flame will return on 4 September and each
Friday night in term time in St. Mary’s, Star of the Sea
Parish Centre, Portstewart from 6-8pm, £2 per child
which includes break. The club is open to children from 6
years old and above and we provide a range of fun
activities; games, arts and crafts, gardening, cooking,
singing and prayer time. All are welcome as we support
the wonderful children of our Parish to grow in friendship,
fun and faith! Registration forms are available at the back
of the Church or at the Parish Centre on the 4 Sept. For
more information text Emer on 07709043921 /email
flameclubportstewart@gmail.com
Flame Club is led by a brilliant team of young leaders
supported by adult volunteers. We are always happy to
welcome new youth volunteers; training and support will
be provided. Volunteering with Flame is a great way to
give of your time, gain experience in working with
children, build your skills, creativity and leadership, all
whilst having fun. It also is a great addition to your CV
and works in conjunction with John Paul II, Duke of
Edinburgh, Oscar Romero and school-based awards.
We also welcome new adult volunteers who give one
Friday a month to help provide support and ensure our
safeguarding protocols, all whilst seeing the kids have a
ball! If you are interested in giving your time or would like
to find out more, please contact Emer as above.

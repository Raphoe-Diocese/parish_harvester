INDULGENCES FOR THE HOLY SOULS During the month of November the Church urges us to pray ferve ntly for the Holy Souls in Purgatory.

The motivation behind our praye r is to free the deceased of the effects of their sins so that the Lord will welcome them to the happiness of heaven.

Through our prayer, we can also gain Plenary Indulgences for the Holy Souls, that is the remission of temporal punishment due to sin, for sins already forgiven. 1st-8th November-by those who visit a cemetery, praying for the Holy Souls and praying for the intentions of the Holy Father, including the Our Father and the Apostle’s Creed.

In order to obtain the plenary indulgence for the Holy Souls, we need to be in the state of grace, that is, we must have attended the Sacrament of Confession, and received Holy Communion recently.

A PRAYER FOR THE HOLY SOULS O God, the Creator and Redeemer of all the faithful, grant to the souls of Your servants departed the remission of their sins, that, through our pious supplications, they may obtain the pardon which they have always desired.

Through Christ Our Lord. Amen.

PAUSE FOR HOPE -

SUNDAY 26TH OCTOBER AT 3.30PM IN CLONARD CHURCH. Bringing together in prayer those who have and have had cancer; their loved ones; those who care for them — doctors & nurses: those with special skills involved in the investigation and treatment of cancer; those in authority who have responsibility for providing resources; our scientists trying to find a cure.

Following the service light refreshments will be provided, along with the opportunity to share stories and experience with others in a similar situation.

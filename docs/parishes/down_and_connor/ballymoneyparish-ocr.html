In this Sunday's Gospel, Jesus asks his disciples, "Who do you say I am?" Peter answers for them all: "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." Jesus is delighted, and in turn reveals who Peter is: "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my Church." This conversation changes history. Jesus founds his Church and gives Peter, the ﬁrst Pope, the mission to guard it — a mission carried on today by Pope Francis, the 266th successor of St Peter.Jesus asks each of us that same question personally. We come to know the answer the way Peter did: by spending time with Jesus in prayer, in the Word of God, and in the life of the Church. And once we know him, we begin to discover who we truly are.

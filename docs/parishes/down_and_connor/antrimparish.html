For the Church: that our faith communities may always be a ‘ house of prayer ’ and a
welcoming home for all people
Let us pray to the Lord
For our parish community: that nourished by the Eucharist, we may recognise the gifts
God has given us and joyfully reach out to support those in need around us.
Let us pray to the Lord.
For all seeking refuge from war, conflict, exploitation, poverty, famine or trafficking.
May God protect them in their exile, keep them safe from harm, and lead them to a
place of peace.
Let us pray to the Lord
Give courage and strength to the young as they prepare for college, employment or
apprenticeships. Help them to choose their work and make the right decisions for their
way of life.
Let us pray to the Lord
Give patient tolerance to those who are no longer young. Open the hearts of the young
to accept from them understanding and love.
Let us pray to the Lord
We pray for all those who are suffering. May they, like the woman in the gospel,
experience Christ’s power to heal them and their loved ones.

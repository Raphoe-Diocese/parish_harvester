The Medical Missionaries of Mary have been invited to visit us in Down and
Connor this year and will be with us here at Ballymena Parish on the
weekend of 5th and 6th September . They will speak of their work, mainly in
Africa and South America, and their mission to bring Christ’s healing love to
those most in need.
Our parish and our diocese have supported MMM generously in the past,
both in finance and in prayer, and MMM wishes to express gratitude for that
support and to emphasise that we here at home are the body of mission, and
those who are sent to far away plac es are the hands and the feet. When they
speak to us and share their story they serve as a connection between us and
those whom they serve.

THURSDAY 13th AUGUST
The groundwork at St Bernard’s site is largely on schedule

Solemnity of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary .
The weekend masses will resume in St Bernard’s Church
Saturday 15th August vigil 6pm, Sunday 16th August 9am

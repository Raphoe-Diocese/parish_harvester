Fountain Foodbank Annual Christmas Appeal Once again this Christmas, Fountain Foodbank is asking for your support and generosity for those in need in our community.

On each of the coming four weekends of Advent you are asked to please consider bringing an item or items of NON-PERISHABLE food for use in food parcels to be distributed before Christmas to those in need in our parish and wider local community.

Items of non-perishable food that are sought include tins of beans, soup, tuna, fruit, spaghetti, ham, corned beef, meals/pies, peas, carrots, sweetcorn and cooking sauces.

In addition, boxes of cereal, teabags, coffee as well as packets of biscuits, pasta and rice are very welcome.

Toiletries and cleaning products are also sought by the Foodbank.

All donations, no matter how small, make a difference to those in food poverty.

Throughout Advent a large, marked donation box will be available in Strangford and Kilclief churches.

Donations from Saul & Ballee can be left in the Church or the Parish office (during office hours) . Thank you in advance for your support and generosity!

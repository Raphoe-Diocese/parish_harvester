IT COULD BE YOU!
I am deeply indebted to all the volunteers we have in our 2 Parishes. In fact, we could
not function without each of you.
One of the biggest fears that prevent people from volunteering is that they could still
be there in 15 years time!
I don’t want that for anyone which is why I am making this appeal.
We are always looking for new volunteers –
- For practical things such as cleaning our churches, helping with the flowers,
collectors and counters.
- For the roles of Eucharistic Minister or readers, especially from the younger
members of our congregations .
- For the various committees in the Parishes, I can assure you we try to keep the
meetings brief and not too often.
If you would be interested, I’d love to hear from you – even to explain what the
various roles entail. I am eager to revamp our Parish Pastoral Councils. To look at
what can be done to make our Parishes and our celebrations more vibrant and
meaningful.
Thank you all for your continued support.
Fr Aidan

Thinking about becoming Catholic or want to deepen your understanding of the faith?
The Diocesan RCIA (Rite of Christian Initiation of Adults) is a journey of faith, formation,
and community for adults who wish to explore the teachings of Jesus Christ and the Catholic
Church. It is open to those who are not baptised, those baptised in another Christian tradition, or
Catholics who wish to reconnect with their faith.
The 2025–2026 programme begins on Wednesday 1st October and runs weekly from 6.30–8.00 pm
at St Mary’s Hall, Chapel Lane, Belfast. Sessions include prayer, teaching, discussion, and time for
questions. Spouses, fiancés or friends are welcome to attend alongside participants.
All are welcome, whether you are exploring faith for the first time or returning after
some time away.
To register or find out more, please email rcia@downandconnor.org or scan the
QR code.
Opening Mass for the Season of Creation - 6.00 pm on Sunday 6th September in St.
Patrick’s Church, Donegall Street, Belfast . Celebrant: Fr Piaras Jackson SJ, with interfaith
contributions. This Mass marks the beginning of the Season of Creation (1 Sept–4 Oct), a time
when Christians worldwide commit to prayer and action for our common home under the theme
“Living Water”. 1st September is the World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation. In his message
for the occasion, Pope Leo said, “The prophetic call to “beat swords into ploughshares” ( Is 2:4) is
(.) not only a vision for nations, but a call to each person. It invites us to transform harm into life.
Yet this transformation cannot be achieved through external change alone. It requires, through
cooperating with God’s grace, a deeper personal and collective conversion — a returning to God,
who will reorder our desires, heal our wounds and help us grow in virtue.”

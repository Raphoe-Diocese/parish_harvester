PIPS Charity is a regional Northern Ireland charity dedicated to suicide prevention, supporting mental health and emotional wellbeing.

With offices in Belfast, Derry and Enniskillen, we p rovide a range of free support services, such as crisis intervention, counselling, and befriending, to individuals and families affected by suicidal thoughts, emotional distress, mental health difficulties, and bereavement.

We would particularly like to bring your attention to our Weekend Crisis Hopeline, a regional overnight telephone service available Friday and Sunday nights, to anyone across Northern Ireland experiencing emotional distress, suicidal thoughts, or a mental health crisis.

The operational hours for the Weekend Crisis Hopeline are as follows.

Friday: 9pm - 10am Saturday Sunday: 9pm - 9am Monday 028 9080 5850 | Freephone 0800 088 6042

We know that crisis doesn't wait for working hours.

The Weekend Crisis Hopeline provides a confidential, non-judgemental space to talk with a trained crisis practitioner when someone is in distress, needs support or simply wants someone to talk to during a difficult time outside of working hours.

There is no referral process for the Hopeline, and individuals can contact us directly whenever they feel in need of support.

For more information, please feel free to refer to our website where the information on the Weekend Crisis Hopeline is posted: Weekend Crisis Hopeline - PIPS Suicide Prevention Ireland

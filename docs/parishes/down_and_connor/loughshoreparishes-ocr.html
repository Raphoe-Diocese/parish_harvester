24 HOURS OF ADORATION ON THE FEAST OF
OUR LADY'S ASSUMPTION
St Agnes’s Church
To honour the Feast of Our Lady's Assumption the
Legion of Mary are organising 24 hours of Eucharistic
Adoration in St Agnes Church. Adoration will begin
after the 7pm Mass on Friday 14th August and will
continue throughout the night and throughout the day,
ending before 6.30 vigil Mass on 15th August. St
Theresa of Calcutta described time spent with Jesus in
the Blessed Sacrament as the "best time you will spend
on earth". Please come along and give thanks to Our
Lady and her Son Jesus.

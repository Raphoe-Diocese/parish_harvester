SEASON OF CREATION 2026
1 September –4 October
As we prepare to celebrate the first Sunday
during the Season of Creation 2026, let us
take action, individually and collectively
as parish communities, to care for ‘our common home’.
The Season of Creation is the annual Christian
celebration which invites us to respond in hope and
commit to positive action in meeting the challenge and
responsibility of caring for creation —‘our common
home’. This challenge has been underlined by Pope
Francis in Laudato Si’ (2015) and Laudate Deum
(2023), and by Pope Leo XIV in his message for the
World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation 2026.
As we journey through the Season of Creation, let us
each take one action to show care for our planet, ‘our
common home’.
Please visit the parish website to read the document
from the Down & Connor Laudato Si ’ group.

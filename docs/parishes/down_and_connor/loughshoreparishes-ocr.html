SEASON OF CREATION 2026
Each year, from 1st September to 4th October, Christians
around the world unite in this special season of prayer
and action to care for our common home. It is a time to
celebrate God as Creator and to reflect on our shared
vocation to protect and cherish all of creation.
This year's theme, "Liv ing Water," is inspired by
Ezekiel 47:9, 12 - a powerful biblical vision of hope and
ecological renewal.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A HOLIDAY AND A PILGRIMAGE?
A holiday is for yourself and a pilgrimage is for others. A group of your :
fellow parishioners from the Colin and Derriaghy Parish Area will be going a
on pilgrimage in early September, walking the Camino Celta, and in the
spirit of pilgrimage want to carry you with them!

prayer box in the foyer of our Lady Oueen of Peace, Kilwee, for you to place
your written intentions. Our pilgrims will physically carry these in their day
packs, lifting you up in prayer as they walk, as they reflect together and at
the tomb of St. James in Santiago.

SEASON OF CREATION 2026
Each year, from 1st September to 4th October, Christians
around the world unite in this special season of prayer
and action to care for our common home. It is a time to
celebrate God as Creator and to reflect on our shared
vocation to protect and cherish all of creation.
This year's theme, "Liv ing Water," is inspired by
Ezekiel 47:9, 12 - a powerful biblical vision of hope and
ecological renewal.

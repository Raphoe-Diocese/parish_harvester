Centering Prayer Group: We are pleased to announce the Centering Prayer has resumed.

Centering Prayer Group offers a quiet and peaceful opportunity to step away from the business of everyday life and spend some time simply being still in God’s presence.

Centering Prayer is a simple form of silent prayer that helps us to deepen our relationship with God and grow in openness, trust and peace.

No previous experience is necessary — just come as you are.

Whether you are new to Centering Prayer or already familiar with it, you will be very welcome.

It is a chance to pause, pray, reflect and share this quiet time with others.

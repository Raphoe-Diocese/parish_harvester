JACKPOT £1,000 IN 51 N UMBERS Nothing but Jesus
We all need some statements like Peter's that give a
rock and a meaning to life, it gives us a clue of where
we belong at deeper levels. We all need statements
that express our faith in God. For Peter it was his faith
in Jesus, the son of God. This would keep him going all
of his life, even at times of unfaithfulness and danger.
He could never forget that he had said, 'you are the
Christ, the son of the living God'.
We might wonder who or what is a living God for us?
Where might we put our basic trust? What is it that, if
taken away, we would be lost without? We can give our
lives to family, country, a political party, money. Some of these are
worth our trust and some not so much.
We need the rock that Peter found in Jesus and Jesus in Peter. He was
called rock only because of his relationship with Jesus Christ, the one
who calls forth our faith and love and gives our lives a huge meaning.
It is God who reveals Jesus to us.
We want to give this to our younger people - a rock they can stand on
in life, which nothing can demolish. This is Jesus, and in him all is
created.
Recall strong convictions in your life.
Ask that they may he strengthened.
Lord, be my rock, my strong hold, my safety.

Liturgical ‘Sign of Peace ’ The Sign of Peace remains an integral part of
the celebration of the Eucharist and has been reintroduced as a normal
practice across the Diocese. The customary expression of the Sign of Peace
in Ireland is a handshake. However, other non -contact forms of the sign of
peace such as a nod, a bow or a smile are also legitimate expressions of the
Sign of Peace. No offence should be taken if someone prefers to express a
non-contact Sign of Peace. The Diocese encourages all to continue to
exercise good hygienic practices, and if you exhibit flu -like symptoms, you
are encouraged to remain at home.

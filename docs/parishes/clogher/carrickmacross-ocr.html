St Vincent De Paul:

Contact - Free Phone 1800677777 . Weekly Reflection. ‘What to do next!

I was given a book recently about former Taoiseach Brian Cowen who was in office when the world economy crashed in the 2008/09 period.

He and his Minister for finance, the late Brian Lenihan have been condemned for a lack of wisdom, for proclaiming that the Irish economy was on a sound footing, that the fundamentals were secure, just as the entire banking system collapsed and the Troika were coming in.

The two men were condemned for not knowing what to do when all the strategies of the world economy were about to be turned upside down.

They were condemned for a lack of wisdom.

The Bridesmaids in the Gospel today are presented as either foolish or wise.

The foolish were safe only if everything went to plan but were lost completely if anything unexpected turned up.

The wise were safe regardless of what circumstance prevailed, be they unexpected or not, because they were prepared.

In life we face many challenges where we often find ourselves challenged by the unexpected.

Wisdom consists in doing the ordinary things with sensitivity, doing the next thing you have to do sensibly, and putting your whole heart into enjoying what you do.

Faith is about knowing what to do next. Fr Shane.

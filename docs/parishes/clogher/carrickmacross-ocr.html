Weekly Reflection.
‘If only it was so easy!’
Occasionally the Gospel presents us with challenges that can appear outlandish. Today’s passage
from Matthew’s Gospel is one such example.
Jesus suggests that each one of us has an obligation to point out faults to another, in the hope of
achieving a reform of the alleged failing. This can be a dicey enterprise. We are not good at
receiving reprimands. The garda telling us to slow down when he has pulled us for speeding is
acceptable and indeed such a warning could indeed be preferable to points on the licence. But for
others to give me a reprimand it could appear as an unwarranted act of self-righteousness on their
behalf.
However, it can often be a tremendous act of true friendship when one’s errors are pointed out.
Indeed, it can be a kindness and a blessing and when well-received can be constructive. A priest
friend occasionally asks me for constructive criticism. It requires great maturity to ask, and even
greater sensitivity to respond to the request.
Let us pray today for such sensitive wisdom in all our relationships. Fr Shane.

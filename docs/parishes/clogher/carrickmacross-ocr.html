During a recent audience with Missio Ireland, Pope Leo highlighted the fact that this year (2026) marks the 100th Anniversary of the establishment of World Mission Sunday, celebrated this year on

Sunday 18th October, which is today. For 100 years, this day has been set apart for prayer, reflection as well as contributing to the Church's mission of evangelisation, especially in areas where the proclamation of the Gospel is only just beginning and where the Church is still young.

Fr Michael Kelly, the National Director of Missio Ireland, thanks parishioners across the parishes of Ireland for their continued support of the Missions.

In 2025 Missio Ireland received over €2m from collections in Irish parishes.

The annual collection on Mission Sunday supports 1,130 dioceses in mission territory that cannot sustain themselves financially.

Yet the day is fundamentally spiritual before it is financial.

Recent popes have repeatedly emphasised that mission begins with prayer, witness and communion.

The opening verse of this Sunday's psalm captures the heart and essence of Mission Sunday: Osing a new song to the Lord, sing to the Lord all the earth.

Tell among the nations his glory and his wonders among all the peoples.

Saint Thérèse of the Child Jesus and Saint Francis Xavier, Patrons of missions, pray for us.

Mary, Queen of missions, pray for us. Fr. Owen Joe.

Mission Sunday Sharing God’s Love This Mission Sunday invites each of us to reflect on what it means to be a missionary in our own lives.

We may not travel to distant lands, but we can share the love of Jesus through a kind word, a helping hand, a listening ear, or a visit to someone who feels lonely.

We remember in our prayers all those who dedicate their lives to bringing hope and faith to others throughout the world.

May we be open to God’s call in the ordinary moments of each day, and may our words and actions reflect His love.

Let us also support the work of missionaries through our prayers and generosity, remembering that each of us has a part to play in spreading the Good News.

MISSION SUNDAY PRAYER We pray to the Lord.

Our Father, ignite our hearts with the fire of Your love.

As we strive to answer our call to mission, guide our steps and empower us to be true witnesses of Your love in every corner of the world.

Through Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior.

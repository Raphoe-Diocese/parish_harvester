May God rebuke him, we humbly pray; and do you O Prince of the heavenly host, By the power of God cast into hell Satan and all the evil spirits who wander through the world seeking the ruination of souls.

Amen Thursdays: Prayer for the sick and Elderly Father of goodness and love, hear our prayers for the sick, elderly, housebound members of our community and for all who are in need.

Amid mental and physical suffering may they find consolation in your healing presence and let us be a source of comfort for them.

Show your mercy to them as you close wounds, cure illness, make broken bodies whole and free downcast spirits.

May these special people find lasting health and deliverance and so join us in thanking you for all your gifts.

We ask this through Jesus Christ, your Son, our Lord, who healed those who believed.

Amen. Fridays: “A prayer for Vocations ” Oh Jesus, send labourers into your fields, which are awaiting Holy Apostles, Saintly priests, heroic missionaries and dedicated sisters and brothers.

Enkindle in the hearts of men and women the spark of a vocation.

Grant that Christian families may desire to give your Church, helpers in the work of tomorrow.

Amen Our next Pope John Paul meeting will take place on

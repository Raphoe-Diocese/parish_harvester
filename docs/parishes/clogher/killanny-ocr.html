These fossil fuels are causing global warming, while the cost of our reliance on fossil fuels is becoming increasingly prohibitive.

Bishops ask of the delegation to advocate for a fossil fuel phase out; fossil fuel profit tax; clean energy investment and sufficiency; and, climate finance and social justice.

Norms and Indulgences at the Commemoration of All Souls (2°* November) From 12.00 noon on 1st November until midnight on 2nd November all who have confessed, received Holy Communion and prayed for the Pope's Intentions (one Our Father and Hail Mary or any other prayer of one’s choice) can gain one plenary indulgence by visiting a church or oratory and reciting one Our Father and the Apostle’s Creed.

The indulgence is applicable only to the souls of the departed.

Confession may be made at any time within the week preceding or the week following 2° November.

Holy Communion may be received on any day from 1* of November to the 8"November.

The faithful who visit a cemetery and pray for the dead may gain a plenary indulgence applicable to the Holy Souls on the usual conditions once per day from 1“ to the 8°November.

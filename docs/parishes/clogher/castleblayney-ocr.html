Reflection
In today ’s Gospel, Jesus asks his disciples a question that is as important for us as it was
for them: “Who do you say I am? ” Peter answers with faith and courage: “You are the
Christ, the Son of the living God. ”
It is easy to speak about Jesus, to know stories about him, or to come to Mass out of hab-
it. But Jesus invites us to something deeper: a personal relationship with him. Who is Jesus for me?
Where do I recognise his presence in my life? And am I willing, like Peter, to place my trust in him?
Peter ’s faith was not perfect, and neither is ours. Yet Jesus chose him and entrusted him with a mission.
This reminds us that God does not wait for us to be perfect before calling us. He works through ordinary
people who are willing to say “yes”.
May we take time this week to hear Christ ’s question anew, and may our lives become our answer:
“Lord, you are the Christ, the Son of the living God. ”
Diocesan Changes
During the past week, Bishop Larry Duffy has announced Diocesan Changes to take effect from 1 September.
These are available on the diocesan website www.clogherdiocese.ie .
This year ’s changes involve our group parishes as Fr Stephen, after eight years with us in Muckno and five
years in Clontibret, is being transferred on promotion as Parish Priest of Bundoran & Ballyshannon in Co
Donegal. In addition, Fr Owen Gorman has been appointed Parish Priest of Aughnamullen East. Fr Owen will
continue to support ministry in our parishes. Bishop Duffy has also appointed Fr Jeremiah Munyoki wa
Kalekye to our parishes. Fr Jeremiah is from the Diocese of Kitui in Kenya – a diocese that Clogher has links
with for the past 40 years. He has been ministering in Bundoran and Ballyshannon for the past three years
and served in Magheracloone and Inniskeen for periods before that.
Masses of Thanksgiving to mark Fr Stephen ’s departure will be celebrated on Sunday next, 30 August at
10am in Annyalla and 11.30am in Castleblayney . These will be opportunities - in the narrow timeframe avail-
able for us - to prayerfully give thanks to God for Fr Stephen ’s pastoral care for us over these years.
For now we congratulate Fr Stephen and Fr Owen on their promotions and assure both of them of our
prayers in their new and future ministries. Castleblayney (Muckno, includes Clontibret)

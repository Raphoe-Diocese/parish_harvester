Reflection In today ’s Gospel, Jesus tells the story of two sons. One says “no” to his father but later changes his mind and goes to work; the other says “yes” but does not follow through. The message is simple but challenging: God looks not only at what wesay, butathowwe live. We can sometimes make promises, say the right things, or appear faithful, yet fail to put our faith into action. At other times, we may have made mistakes or wandered from God, but then have the humility to turn back and do what is right. God does notaskfor perfection. He asks for a willing heart that is ready to change. Every day gives us another opportunity tosay“yes”to God—not merely with our words, but through kindness, forgiveness, service and love. Mayour“yes”to Godbe seen inthewaywe live.

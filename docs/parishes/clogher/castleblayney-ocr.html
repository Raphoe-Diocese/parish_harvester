Reflection on todays Gospel
In today ’s Gospel, Jesus reminds us that our faith is lived in relationship
with others. When disagreements arise, He encourages us not to ignore
them or speak about one another behind their backs, but to approach
each other with honesty, patience and love.
Jesus also gives us the beautiful promise: “Where two or three are gath-
ered in my name, I am there among them. ” Our parish community may
be small or imperfect, but whenever we gather with faith, prayer and a
sincere desire to follow Christ, He is truly present.
May we seek to build communities where forgiveness is stronger than
resentment, reconciliation stronger than division, and Christ ’s love is at
the heart of all we do.

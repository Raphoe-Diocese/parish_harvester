Farewell to Fr Stephen Duffy
As Fr Stephen prepares to leave the parishes of Muckno and Clontibret after eight years of dedicated and faithful
service, we take this opportunity to express our sincere gratitude and appreciation for all that he has given to our
parish communities.
Over these eight years, Father Stephen has shared in the joys and sorrows of our people, celebrating our Masses
and sacraments, supporting families in times of need, and being a caring and faithful presence among us. His
commitment, kindness and dedication have touched the lives of so many.
While we are sorry to see him leave, we are delighted for him as he begins a new chapter in his priestly ministry as
Parish Priest in Bundoran, Co Donegal. We wish him every blessing and every happiness in his new appointment.
Fr Stephen, thank you for your eight years of service, friendship and pastoral care. You will be greatly missed by
the people of Muckno and Clontibret.
May God bless you and guide you in your new parish, and may your ministry in Bundoran be fil led with many bless-
ings and much joy.
Go raibh míle maith agat, Fr Stephen, and every good wish for the future. Castleblayney (Muckno, includes Clontibret) http://www.mucknoparish.ie/

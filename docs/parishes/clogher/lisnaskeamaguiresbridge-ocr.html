MISSION APPEAL FOR THE COLUMBAN FATHERS We welcome Fr. Oliver McCrossan of the Columbans this weekend and he will be doing a Mission Appeal at all our Masses.

He will thank you for your previous support for the Columbans, and encourage new subscribers to the Far East magazine.

We can do this by writing our details on the envelopes provided and enclosing a suggested donation of €20 (a one year subscription).

Or, if you do not have the money at hand, you can bring home the envelope, fill in your details (with donation) and bring it back to Mass next week.

Alternatively, if you wish your loved ones to be remembered at Columban Masses, you might consider subscribing to the “Mission League ” by taking the card and envelope provided, writing down your own name and those of your dearly deceased.

You are welcome to enclose a donation and also bring these cards back to the parish next week.

Finally, you might simple wish to make a donation in the boxes at the back of the Church, or online at www.columbans.ie /donation We thank you in ad- vance for your generosity.

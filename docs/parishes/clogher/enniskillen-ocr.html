Planning a Baptism
When presenting a child for Baptism, parents are encouraged to contact the Par ish Office in good time so that the preparation process can be ex-
plained and any questions answered. The Church asks that a child have eith er one sponsor (Godparent) or, where two sponsors are chosen, one
male and one female sponsor. Sponsors should normally be practising Catholics, at least 16 years of age, who have received the Sacraments of
Baptism, Confirmation and the Eucharist, and who are willing to support the ch ild in the practice of the faith. Where this is not possible, we will

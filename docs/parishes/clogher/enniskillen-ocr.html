When presenting a child for Baptism, parents are encouraged to contact the Parish Office in good time so that the preparation process can be explained
and any questions answered. The Church asks that a child have either one sponsor (Godparent) or, where two sponsors are chosen, one male and one
female sponsor. Sponsors should normally be practising Catholics, at least 16 year s of age, who have received the Sacraments of Baptism, Confirmation
and the Eucharist, and who are willing to support the child in the practice of the faith. Where this is not possible, we will be happy to explain the role of a

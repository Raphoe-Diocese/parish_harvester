CATHEDRAL HEATING UPDATE We had hoped to begin upgrading the cathedral’s heating system last month.

Unfortun ately, we have encountered a difficulty, and the work will now be delayed.

We sincerely ap ologise for the disappointment and frustration this will cause.

We recognise that the upgrading of the heating system is long overdue, and are very conscious of the discomfort many of you have experienced in the cathedral during the colder months.

The current heating arrangement is only temporary and does not always provide sufficient warmth.

In the meantime, please dress warmly f or Mass and, where possible, sit near the temporary heaters.

We are grateful for your patience and continued support.

Having considered the proposed system carefully, we have concerns about its long-term running costs.

Given the size of the cathedral, we want to be sure that any system we install will be effective, af fordable to operate and sustainable into the future.

With no comparable large building in Ireland, we have identified two large churches i n England which have installed similar systems and we are arranging visits to learn from their experience, particularly regar ding effectiveness and running costs.

Our objective remains to find a heating system that is effective, effic ient, environmentally friendly and sustainable for generations to come.

We thank you sincerely for your patience, understanding and continued sup port.

We will keep you informed following these visits and update you as soon as a clear way forward has been established.

St Patrick’s Church – 200th Anniversary Next year, 2027, marks the 200th anniversary of St Patrick’s Church.

We would like to celebrate this important milestone in the life of our parish and are inviting interested parishioners to come together to help plan for the occasion.

An initial meeting will take place on Tuesday, 17th Nov. at 7pm in the church.

Everyone welcome. Whether you have ideas, or simply a willingness to help, your contribution would be greatly appreciated.

We hope to make this a fitting celebration of our church, our faith and the generations who have worshipped here over the past 200 years.

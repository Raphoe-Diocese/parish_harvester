The Seas on of Creation Sept 1 st - Oct 14th As we prepare to enter the Season of Creation, Pope Leo X1V offers
us a powerful Message for the World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation. This year, as we follow the theme Living
Water , his words feel especially meaningful. Scripture reminds us to guard our hearts, because from them flows
everything we do (Prov 4:23). What is flowing from my heart into the world? Is it care or indifference? Hope or
discouragement? A desire for justice, peace and communion? Pope Leo reminds us that ecological conversion begins
within us - but it is meant to flow outward, transforming how we live, how we relate to others and how we care for our
common home. Before Living Water becomes a river that renews the world, it first renews us.

Planning a Baptism
When presenting a child for Baptism, parents are encouraged to contact the Parish Office in good time so that the
preparation process can be explained and any questions answered. The Church asks that a child have either one sponsor
(Godparent) or, where two sponsors are chosen, one male and one female sponsor. Sponsors should normally be
practising Catholics, at least 16 years of age, who have received the Sacraments of Baptism, Confirmation and the
Eucharist, and who are willing to support the child in the practice of the faith. Where this is not possible, we will be
happy to explain the role of a Christian witness. Please speak to the Parish Office, who will be happy to advise.

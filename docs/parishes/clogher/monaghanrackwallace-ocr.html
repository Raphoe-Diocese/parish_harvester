CATHEDRAL HEATING UPDATE: We had hoped to begin upgrading the cathedral’s heating system last month, but unf ortunately the work has been delayed due to some concerns about the proposed system’s long-term running costs.

We sincerely apologise for the disappointment and frustration this will cause.

We recognise that the upgrade is long overdue and are very conscious of the discomfort many have experienced during the colder months.

The current heating arrangement is temporary and may not always provide sufficient warmth.

In the meantime, we ask you to dress warmly for Mass and, where possible, sit near the temporary heaters.

Given the size of the cathedral, it is important that we find a system that is not only effective but also affordable to run and sustainable for the future.

We are therefore arranging visits to two large chur ches in England which have installed similar systems, so that we can learn from their experience, particularly regarding effectiv eness and running costs.

Our aim remains to provide a heating system that is efficient, environmentally friendly and sustainable for generations to come.

We sincerely thank you for your patience, understanding and continued support.

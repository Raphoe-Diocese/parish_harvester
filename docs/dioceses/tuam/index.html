Pope Leo’s Message on the Rosary “I would like to make an appeal that is particularly close to my heart. I invite all the faithful & all Christian communities to pray the holy rosary throughout the coming month of October, with the particular intention that wars may cease & that peace may be established among peoples in conflict. Let us accompany this prayer with concrete acts of penance & mercy, committing ourselves always to fostering dialogue & reconciliation. May our most holy Mother intercede forus!”(In Lourdes recently). When back in Rome Pope Leo was joined by approx. 700 children in the Vatican Gardens. Rest in Peace

Twenty-Eighth Sunday in Ordinary Time The landowner had prepared for a large crowd. When he sent his servants out to personally remind the guests to come, some of the servants were mistreated, beaten and even killed by his prospe ctive guests! Quite naturally, the king was outraged. What does this Sunday’s Gospel say to us? I doubt that we have had the experience this story speaks of. However, we all have been disappointed and upset at various times in our lives. We may have been deeply hurt by someone or betrayed by another. Our natural human response istobe angry, hurt and disappointed – and rightly so. Hopefully, we haven ’t decided to kill anyone because of that! In these situations, we need to take time to calm down and deal withthe hurt, anger and disappointment. We need to acknowledge and feel the emotions. If we repress them, most likely the emotions will come back with a vengeance . But we can’t stop there. We also need to honestly acknowledge what we are experiencing but then with time to gradually ‘let these feelings go’ and forgive. Most often this is the last thing that we want todo!It will ta ke time butitisan important step to take. If we nurse our anger and hurt, wearenot hurting the individuals who disappointed us, butweare hurting ourselves and those we love! Ifwearenot able to forgive, we simply need to ask God to help us. Maywe have the grace toask Godto heal the anger and hurt – and to free us!

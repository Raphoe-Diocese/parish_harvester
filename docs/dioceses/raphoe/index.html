PIUS X ADORATION CHAPEL Eucharistic Adoration is a devotion whereby members of a parish unite in making continuous adoration before the exposed Blessed Sacrament, for as many hours as may be possible, based on the number of people who pledge to do a weekly holy hour. Eucharistic Adoration is silent and contemplative in nature. We in this parish are very blessed that St. Pius X Adoration Chapel (located in the grounds of Loreto Secondary School) is open from Sunday 1 pm – Friday 8 pm and that we have enough people willing to commit an hour each week. Thursday adoration includes the Divine Mercy @ 5: 30 pm and concludes with Rosary @ 8: 30 pm All Masses are available live on: www.naomhfionan.com & Parish Radio @ 106.5 FM Reflection It’s never easy to keep faith, but to hold on to it in stormy times, as many people are going through at present is particularly difficult. Today ’s Scripture readings encourage us not to lose heart, or give in to despondency, but to trust in the Lord who is with us even when we are unaware of his presence. ‘Courage! It is I! Do not be afraid ’. These are the same words Jesus speaks to us today as we sail on the uncertain sea of life. He does not promise us a storm free voyage, but he does promise to be always there for us. The example of Peter teaches us that, to stay afloat on the sea of life, we need to fix our gaze on Jesus, not on the surging waves around us, or on the fears and doubts that well up in our hearts. Today ’s gospel invites us to deepen our faith and to maintain our focus on Jesus. In stormy times, there is always a tendency for us to retreat to a cave like Elijah, or to seek out some safe harbour, far fr om the stormy seas. But clearly that is not what Jesus wants. In today ’s gospel, Matthew tells us that Jesus ‘made his disciples get into the boat and go on ahead of him to the other side ’ (Mt 14:22). On another occasion, he tells Peter not to hesitate but to ‘launch out into the deep ’ (Lk 5:4). Jesus was calling his disciples, as he is calling us today, to join him in the greatest adventure known to human history: the realisation of God ’s dream of a world transformed by the power of love. And, irrespective of what storms we encounter, we shall not fail, because Jesus is always with us. Cuimhneacháin Báis: Bridget Donnelly whose funeral was in Ramelton on Monday.

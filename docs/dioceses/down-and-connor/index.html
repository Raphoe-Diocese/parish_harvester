A king forgives a servant an unpayable debt (worth billions in today's money) only for that servant to refuse mercy to a colleague who owes him a much smaller sum. The king's anger reveals the lesson: having received such extraordinary mercy, the servant should have shown it in turn. Wetooare debtors before God, and Christ has already paid what we could never repay. But receiving that gift means extending it to others Ñ even when forgiveness feels impossible. If weÕre Christians we have to be serious about seeking forgiveness and forgiving others.

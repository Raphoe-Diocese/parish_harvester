KILDARE AND LEIGHLIN DIOCESEAND AND Parish Safeguarding Audits Annual safeguarding audit forms were circulated to all parishes on 8th January 2026. To be completed and returned to Bishop’s House by 12th March 2026 . The Final Report for 2024’s Audit is available on the diocesan website: www.kandle.ie/safeguarding Following the 2024 launch of the Church’s Child Safeguarding Policy, Ailish Higgins, our Diocesan Director of Safeguarding met with young people from the diocese to ask them “What does a Safe & Welcoming Church mean to you?” Thanks to all the children and young people who took part in these sessions and were open and honest about their experience of parish life.

Thanks too to their parents, the group leaders and parish priests who supported the facilitation of these groups.

National Board for Safeguarding Children in the Catholic Church in Ireland – Biannual conference The held their biannual conference from 3rd – 5th September 2025 titled: Towards a Restorative and Transitional Justice model of engaging with victims and survivors in the Catholic Church in Ireland.

The event aimed to enhance the understanding we all have of how we must engage in a just and restorative way with those who have suffered harm in the Catholic Church in Ireland.

Our diocesan director of safeguarding and DLP attended the event and shared valuable feedback with our diocesan safeguarding committee.

The learning from the conference will help guide our practice going forward.

Special thanks to the National Board for organising and hosting the event.

CALENDAR 27th October: St Otteran, a descendant of Conall Gulban, is usually identified with Odhran who preceded Colum Cille in Iona.

His death is recorded in 548 and his grave was greatly revered in Iona.

He was chosen by the Vikings as patron of the City of Waterford in 1096 and later patron of the diocese. 28th October: Saints Simon and Jude.

The teaching of the apostles is the living Gospel for all to hear.

Their authority comes from their fidelity to Christ and his message.

We pray today that they continue to watch over the Church and give protection against error.

St Jude is Patron of hopeless causes. 29th October: St Colman hailed from Kilmacduagh, Co. Galway, in the 7th century after studying in Aran, where he founded two churches on Inis Mhór, he returned to make a foundation at Kilmacduagh. 31st October Blessed Dominic Collins was born around 1566 in Youghall Co. Cork.

In 1598, after a military career, he entered the Society of Jesus as a Brother.

He returned to Ireland in 1601, but on 17 June 1601 he was captured by the English who tried in vain to make him renounce his faith.

He was hanged in his native city on 31 October 1602.

Hay & Oats Collection (Annual collection for Priest of the Parish) We have come to that time of year again for our Hay and Oats collection.

First of all, I would like to thank you sincerely for your continued and generous support of our parish.

As you may know we have two accounts in our Parish: 1. Parish account; 2. Clergy Account.

Thanks to your continued support our Parish account is in relatively good standing.

Our Clergy account has understandably bottomed out and we have had to borrow money from our Parish account to keep the Clergy account solvent.

All the contributions that come in from the Hay and Oats will be lodged into the Clergy Account.

Representatives in your area will drop in an envelope into your house over the next week or two.

It would be greatly appreciated if you could place your donation into the envelope and drop it back to your representative or, into the Parish office, or into mass at the weekend.

We would be grateful if you could put your correct address and Eircode on the front of the envelope.

Once again, a sincere "Thank You" for your continued support.

We also appreciate the hard work of our Parish Representatives who voluntarily give of their time to distribute the envelopes and collect them.

Stay safe everyone. God bless and thank you.

Fr. John O’Brien.

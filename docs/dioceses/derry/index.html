He worked in parts of what is now Holla nd, Northwest Germany and Luxembourg.

He is remembered as the first of the renowned Anglo-Saxon missionaries who went to continental Europe.

A short reflection on today’s Gospel Matthew 5: 1-12 On the Solemnity of All Saints, we hear Jesus proclaim the Beatitudes, showing us the path to true happiness and holiness.

In a world that often values success, wealth and recognition, Jesus reminds us that God blesses the humble, the merciful, the peacemakers and those who hunger for justice.

Today, we celebrate not only the great saints whose names we know, but also the countless ordinary people whose quiet lives of faith, love and service reflect the goodness of God.

They remind us that holiness is not reserved for a few; it is a calling for every one of us.

The Beatitudes invite us to live our faith in everyday life: to forgive, to care for those in need, to bring peace where there is division, and to remain faithful through difficult times.

As we celebrate All Saints, may their example inspire us to follow Christ more closely, trusting that even our smallest acts of love can lead us along the path to holiness.

Lord Jesus, through the prayers of all the saints, help us to live the Beatitudes each day and become signs of your love in our families, our parish and our community.

LUMEN CHRISTI YOUNG ADULT PRAYER & SCRIPTURE GROUP Mondays 7 – 8.30pm. Ages 18 – 35. Luke Wadding House, Lady Lane, . info@waterford-cathedral.com SOME FEEDBACK FROM STUDENTS WHO TOOK PART IN THE POPE JOHN AWARD 2026 I realised that faith is not only about praying or going to Mass.

It is also about how you treat people and whether you are willing to help others when they need support.

It helped me connect faith with actions instead of just saying I will.

If this experience taught me anything, it’s that there are people who do believe and are really close to their religion and I have developed my respect for them.

I think the one obstacle I had to overcome was finding going to Mass an enjoyable place as when I was younger I used to find mass very long and I was not really able to understand what was being said, but since going to mass more since taking part in JP2 I have been able to understand the gospel, readings and hymns etc.

I think my faith has become stronger from taking part in JP2. Over time my confidence grew and I even decided to join the choir that our JP2 team founded.

I would go to practise any Friday it was on and try my best to learn the songs and just do my best even though I am not the best singer.

But I was doing it for the Church so it didn’t feel as uncomfortable or out of the ordinary when it usually would if I sang outside the Church.

Through these activities I have learned more about my religion and what it actually means.

Mass before was routine, but being involved made it more meaningful.

I have started paying more attention to the readings and think about how they apply to real life.

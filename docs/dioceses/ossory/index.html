World Mission Sunday - 18th October 2026 World Mission Sunday 2026, a day when the whole Church unites in praying for Missionaries and offering practical support where possible.

Today we mark the 100th anniversary of its establishment by Pope Pius XI in 1926. theme for this year’s celebration of Mission Sunday is:“One in Christ, united in mission ”. Pope Leo reminds us that love is at the heart of mission.

Sa int Francis of Assisi said, “Love is not loved.” Saint Thérèse also desired “to love Jesus and to make him loved.” On this World Mission Day, may we renew our missionary spirit through our prayer, kindness, generosity and care for those in need, may we make Jesus known and loved.

May Christ make us one in him, unite us in mission, and send us out to share God’s love with everyone.

Through Isaiah, he promises a banquet for peoples, where death is destroyed and every tear wiped away.

In the Gospel, the king sends his serva nts to the crossroads: “Invite everyone you can find.” The feast is ready— but we must come clothed in the grace he gives, not in our own self- sufficiency.

St Paul, even in prison, learned that Christ is enough: “There is nothing I cannot master with the help of the One who gives me strength.” At Mass, we taste that heavenly banquet.

Let us accept the invitation, put on Christ, and bring others to the feast. Come —everything is ready.

Major Restoration of the Church Tower In recent times it has become apparent that there some significant structural problems in the tower of Holy Trinity Church Durrow.

The wooden Louvres (Slanted beams) on all four sides of the tower are rotting, and each is framed in steel.

These present a significant health and safety risk.

When the tower was examined earlier in the summer other major difoculties with the pointing of stonework were also identified.

The upshot of all this is that some major restoration work is necessary and as a matter of urgency.

The work is scheduled to begin on Monday next, 19th of October and will take approximately four to six weeks.

The doors across the front of the church will remain locked during the work and entrance to the church will be through the side aisle doors only.

The front of the church and the tower is a building site and therefore a health and safety risk.

I realise this presents an inconvenience, but I ask for your consideration and cooperation.

I am grateful to the Parish Finance Committee and the diocesan authorities who have worked with me to address this problem.

Together with other necessary works to repair the Lightning conductors on the church carried out earlier in the year, this major repair job will cost in the region of €90,000.

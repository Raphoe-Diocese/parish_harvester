Matthew fit z Griffin, seneschal of Munster, and, most probably, son of Griffin fitz William, who was brother of Raymund le Gros, was lord of Knocktopher in the time of Hugh de Rous, Bishop of Ossory from 1202 to 1218. He was a great benefactor of the Priory of Kells, to which he appropriated the churches of Knocktopher, Castlecolumb, Kilmoganny, Kilcreddy and Kilbeacon, in Ossory.

He was succeeded by his heir, Raymund fitz Griffin, proprietor of Knocktopher and the new town of Jerpoint in 1247. In 1293, Knocktopher was the property of Edmund fitz Milo le Brit.

On the 12th October, 1312, Knocktopher passed from Mathew fitz Philip Maunsel to Edmund le Botiller, father of James, 1st Earl of Ormond. Mass of the Sick and Anointing of the Sick The Sacrament of Anointing of the Sick brings spiritual grace, peace, strength, comfort and healing.

It can restore spiritual, emotional and physical healt h, if it is God’s will.

Any Catholic or Christian in serious illness, advancing age or preparing for surgery can avail of it.

It can be repeated monthly or if an illness worsens.

The priest lays his hands on the person and anoints their forehead and the palms of their hands with blessed oil offering a special prayer of healing and grace.

In relation to this importan t Sacrament, starting in November, the last Thursday of the month in Ferrybank at 10.00am and the last Friday of the month at 10.00am in Slieverue, a special Mass of the Sick including anointing will be celebrated.

Amen. November The Month Of Holy Souls “It is a holy and wholesome thought to pray for the dead that they may be loosed form their sins.” Each November, the Church engages in a great crusade of prayer for our dead.

We pray for our dead because we believe there is a communion - an unbreakable link - between the living and the dead.

That link ensures us that we can help them and they can help us.

One saint said that the Holy Souls may win astounding favours for us and deliver us from evils, sickness and dangers of every kind.

Our prayer, especially the holy sacrifice of the Mass, is the greatest expression of our gratitude and love for those who have died.

In fact, the November Novena of Masses is the most powerful way to honour with great respect the memory of our dead.

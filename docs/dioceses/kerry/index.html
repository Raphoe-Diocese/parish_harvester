Week beginning 4th October 2026 27th Sunday in Ordinary Time A

In this Sunday's Gospel Jesus compares the kingdom of God to a lavish wedding banquet where the king sends his servants out to call the invited guests, but they were not interested. Furious, the king sent his servants to the crossroads of the town to gather everyone they could find, good and bad alike and the wedding hall was filled with guests. What does this all mean forus?The rejection by the initially invited guests can be seen as a mirror to those in Isaiah’s time and even our own, who often overlook or dismiss Goddueto distractions or worldly concerns. The feast in Isaiah represents the promise of salvation and eternal joy. However, many miss out on this divine opportunity. Similarly, the king's invitation in Matthew’s Gospel, representing God's call, is ignored or treated with disdain, highlighting human tendencies to neglect spiritual wealth for fleeting worldly gains. But God's invitation remains constant. The challenge tousisto recognise, accept and prepare for these divine moments. May we hear Christ call usandmaywe accept His invitation with grateful hearts.

Do you know the purpose of Holy Mass?

The primary purpose of the Holy Mass is to worship God through participation inthere- presentation of Jesus’ passion, death and Resurrection. The Secondary purpose is to receive Jesus in Holy Communion and thirdly to unite as God’s Church sharing Faith and Thanksgiving.

Rota: Min Eoc: Máire Uí Ghealbháin. Léitheoir: Cathy Ní Ghrifín. Thought fortheday: Inthe parables of Jesus, wedding feasts are mentioned from time to time. The original parable may have ended with the words “invite everyone you can find to the wedding.” The detail of the troops, highly unlikely as an actual part of a wedding invitation, realistically portrays the later destruction of Jerusalem, which Christians did indeed look back upon as punishment precisely for that rejection. The final verse is difficult for us today on a spiritual level. However, it is meant to provoke (re)conversion in a possibly complacent church.

11TH OCTOBER 2026 ~ 28TH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME WORLD MISSION

SUNDAY 18 OCTOBER 2026 World Mission Sunday, which is celebrated on the 2nd last Sunday of October each year, is the one Sunday of the year when the entire global Church comes together in support of mission.

It is the Holy Father’s annual appeal for spiritual and financial support so that the life giving work of overseas mission and missionaries can continue.

Mission Sunday collections will take place in every single parish where the Church is present.

It is a moment of universal solidarity when each member of the Church family, regardless of location or background, plays their part in supporting each other.

In Ireland, Missio Sunday is co-ordinated by Missio Ireland (Pontifical Mission Soc ieties).

They are the Holy Father’s official charity for overseas mission, his chosen instrument for sharing the Gospel and building the Church throughout the world.

Through the generosity of parishioners and donors, they can reach out to offer aid to struggling communities, helping them to grow and thrive by bringing hope to places where there is often turmoil, poverty and uncertainty.

They can provide mission dioceses with the essential support they need to become self sufficient.

Working thr ough the local bishops, churches and missionary congregations, they ensure resources are distrib uted equitably and justly, based on need.

We thank people for their great generosity and kindness.

Mitchel stown Paris h & Com munity Newsletter Volume : 16 Issue : 29 Date: 10th & 11th October 2026 Twenty Eighth Sunday In Ordinary Time Thought for the Day The landowner had prepared for a large crowd.

When he sent his se rvants out to personally remind the guests to come, some of the servants were mistreated, beaten and even killed by his prospective guests!

Quite naturally, the king was enraged. What does this Sunday’s Gospel say to us?

I doubt that we have had the exp erience this story speaks of.

However, we all have been disappointed and upset at various times in our lives.

We may have been deeply hurt by someone or betrayed by another.

Our nat ural human response is to be angry, hurt and disappointed – and rightly so.

Hopefully, we haven’t decided to kill anyone because of that!

In these situations, we need to take time to calm down and deal with the hurt, anger and disappointment.

We need to acknowledge and feel the emotions.

If we repress them, most likely the emotions will come back with a vengeance.

But we can’t stop there. We also need to honestly acknowledge what we are experiencing, but then with time to gradually ‘let those feelings go’ and forgive.

Most often this is the last thing that we want to do!

It will take time, but it is an important step to take.

If we nurse our anger and hurt, we are not hurting individuals who disappointed us, we are hurting ourselves and those we love!

If we are not able to fo rgive, we simply need to ask God to help us.

May we have the grace to ask God to heal the anger and hurt – and to free us!Sr Kristine Anne Harpenau OSB Masses during the week in the Parish Church – 12th to 16th October 2026 Monday to Friday: Rosary: 9:30am Mass: 10am Friday: Divine Mercy: 3pm. As the 250th Anniversary year of the foundation of the Presentation Sisters draws to a close , we celebrate the light that Nano Nagle brought to the people o f Cork—a light of hope, compassion and possibility.

Through her care for people living in poverty and her dedicatio n to education, Nano brought comfort and opportunity to those too often overlooked.

Her lante rn became a symbol of love in action, illuminating a path towards a brighter future.

For 250 years, the Presentation Sisters have carried that light through out the world.

Through education, service and a steadfast commitment to justice, they have touched countless lives, bringing hope and upholding the dignity of each person.

As this anniversary year concludes, we give thanks for Nano’s courage and for the generations of Sisters who have continued her work.

May her light inspire us all to reach out with kindness, stand alongside those in need and carry hope into the lives of others.

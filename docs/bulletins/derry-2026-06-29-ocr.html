DAY FOR LIFE – Jesus sanctified the beginning of life as an unborn child, hidden in the womb of Our Lady .  Mary

knew she was carrying the Son of God – God and man – as soon as He was conceived.  John the Baptist leapt in

his mother Elizabeth’ s womb when Mary greeted her . Our Lady treasured Our Lord in her heart and womb until His

birth. T o read the full bishops’ message visit www .councilforlife.ie

FATHERS’ DAY – If there’ s no self-sacrifice, it’ s not fatherhood! Fathers are givers.

FATHERS’ DAY PRA YER – God our Father , we give You thanks and praise for fathers, young and old.   May they

find courage and perseverance in their vocation.   May their lives follow the example of Saint Joseph, model in the

protection and care of the family .  We remember fathers, grandfathers and great-grandfathers who are no longer with

us, but who guided us in the Faith, nourished us with their love and live forever in our memory .  Through the prayers

of St Joseph, may our fathers receive abundant blessings today .

NOVENA TO ST OLIVER PLUNKETT – 22nd - 30th June – Glorious Martyr , Saint Oliver , who willingly gave your life

for your faith, help us to be strong in faith.   May we be loyal like you to the See of Peter .  By your intercession and

example, may all hatred and bitterness be banished from the hearts of Irish men and women.   May the peace of

Christ reign in our hearts, as it did in your heart, even at the moment of your death.   Pause for your own intentions.

St Oliver Plunkett, pray for us and for Ireland.

appointed Archbishop of Armagh in 1669. He was forced to conduct a covert ministry during the suppression

convicted in Ireland he was moved to Newgate Prison in London and found guilty of high treason ‘for

promoting the Catholic Faith’ and was condemned to death. He was martyred at Tyburn on 1 July 1681.

faith that led him to know Christ in his wounds sent him to the farthest places to preach Christ.

persevered in prayer and good works and as a widow lived in poverty as a Franciscan tertiary.

A short reflection on today’s Gospel Matthew 10: 37-42

‘Anyone who finds his life will lose it; anyone who loses his life for my sake will find it’.

These words spoken by Jesus in today’s Gospel, spoken with such force, can initially seem

enigmatic, paradoxical, even. How can we find ourselves by losing oursel ves? And then it

dawns on us. If the life we find ourselves living and cherishing is a life wedded exclusively

to the material world, a life which embraces the power -hunger, greed and corrupt practices

of that world, then that life, when it ends, is lost . But anyone who lives life with Jesus as its

centre, a life which embraces Jesus fully – his teaching, his guidance, the cross he carried

out of a love so profound it made the cold world reel in shocked incredulity, that person has

found eternal life.

To be a true and faithful disciple of Jesus is not easy, and Jesus knows this. He knows it is

not easy for us to carry the cross of our suffering with the same courage, fortitude and

humility with which he carried his own heavy cross to Calvary. He knows our predilection

to attach ourselves to the wrong things, the wrong people, the shiny and superficial as

opposed to the deep and worthwhile. He assures us over and over that he will never desert

us: he will never abandon us to our fears. He assures us t hat true faith overcomes all fears:

if we put his spirit before all else, our reward will be great indeed, in this life as well as in

eternal life. We can experience in the here and now the peace and joy which is the reward

of casting aside the ego -centre d life and embracing a life which is gloriously Jesus -centred.

Jesus, may we never forget that faith can overcome all fear. Jesus, may we always

remember to put you before all else if we are to gain a life of peace and joy for all eternity.

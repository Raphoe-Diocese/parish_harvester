Mission Sunday: 18th October 2026 : This weekend parishes worldwide celebrate World Mission Sunday.

Our prayers and the generosity shown will help overseas missionaries to continue to share the love of God with some of the world’s most marginalised and vulnerable people.

World Mission Sun day is the Holy Father’s annual appeal for spiritual and financial support so that the li fe-giving work of overseas mission and missionaries can continue.

It always falls on the second last Sunday in October.

World Mission Sunday collections take place in every single parish where the Church is present.

This includes not only Ireland and Europe, but also in poorer parishes in developing countries.

It is a moment of universal solidarity when each member of the Church family, regardless of location or background, play their part in supporting each other.

This is what makes it such a special celebration.

What is a Parish Pastoral Council? A Pastoral Council is a representative body of faithful people from all areas of the parish, working in close partnership with the priests to further the mission of Christ and his church.

It is not primarily involved with the maintenance of property or the raising of finances The Pastoral Council is concerned with people – concerned with faith, with mission, with building up a living and caring community.

To have a good and effective Parish Pastoral Council is very important.

Therefore members of the Council have to be prepared to give the Commitment for the four year term.

The Pastoral Council would meet around 5/6 times each year (roughly every 2 months) – not a huge commitment.

It is vital that true listening and genuine dialogue takes place at these meetings and that everyone in the parish feels represented and th at they have a voice.

It is therefore important to give this matter careful thought and elect those people you think would best suit this role.

A new Pastoral Council needs to elected by the end of October.

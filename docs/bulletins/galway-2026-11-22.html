Give your best anyway. Amen. A Little Bit About Us St. Brigid’s Parish is located in Ballybane 4.8 kilometres east of Galway City.

The area of Ballybane takes its name from a Ring Fort, which dates back to a period between 600BC and 16 AD. The Parish is named after St. Brigid of Kildare and the emblem of the parish is the St Brigid’s Cross.

St. Brigid’s is a relatively new parish (1995) and now boasts a small native population, a much larger population coming originally from Galway City and county, a sizeable number of Travellers and a large and diverse representation of people from Africa and Eastern Europe.

Ninety Years of Faith, Hope and Love Christ the King | Sunday, 22 November 2026 A grandmother was walking past the church with her grandson when he noticed the preparations for the parish's 90th birthday celebrations. “Granny, was the church here when you were little?” “It certainly was,”she smiled. “And long before that.” “Imagine all the people who have been here!” he replied.

Granny paused. “Yes, and every one of them has helped make this parish what it is today.” This Sunday, as we celebrate the Feast of Christ the King and the 90th birthday of our parish, we remember with gratitude the generations who have worshipped, served, celebrated and supported one another here.

In today's Gospel, Jesus reminds us that his kingdom is not built on power or possessions, but on love expressed through kindness, compassion and service.

Whenever we welcome a stranger, comfort someone who is lonely or help a neighbour in need, we are serving Christ himself . Our parish has always been more than bricks and mortar.

It is a living community, a Family of Families , where faith is expressed through the goodness of ordinary people.

WE INHERIT A PARISH BUILT BY THE LOVE OF OTHERS. WE BUILD ITS FUTURE THROUGH THE LOVE WE SHARE TODAY. Something to ponder: What small act of kindness can I offer to help our parish family continue to flourish?

Prayer: Lord, we thank you for ninety years of faith, friendship and service.

Bless all who have gone before us, those who serve today and the generations yet to come.

May our parish always be a home where your love is welcomed and shared.

As the 250th Anniversary year of the foundation of the Presentation Sisters draws to a close , we celebrate the light that Nano Nagle brought to the people o f Cork—a light of hope, compassion and possibility. Through her care for people living in poverty and her dedicatio n to education, Nano brought comfort and opportunity to those too often overlooked. Her lante rn became a symbol of love in action, illuminating a path towards a brighter future. For 250 years, the Presentation Sisters have carried that light through out the world. Through education, service and a steadfast commitment to justice, they ha ve touched countless lives, bringing hope and upholding the dignity of each person. As this anniversary year concludes, we give thanks for Nano’s courage andforthe generations of Sisters who have continued her work.

The just encounter God in death. He calls them to himself so as to share eternal life with them.

No one however can be received into God’s friendship and intimacy without having been purified of the consequences of personal sin.

The Church gives the name Purgatory to this purification of the elect, which is entirely different from the punishment of the damned.

This is the landlord’s Heir: Come, let us kill Him. William Shakespeare had a remarkable ability to cap ture eternal truths in just a few words. One line From his play, Julius Cæsar, captures almost exactly what Jesus is saying to the Pharisees in the parable of today’s gospel. In the play Brutus says of Cæsar: “The abuse of greatness is when it disjoins remorse from power.” In ordinary language it means, ‘when those with power and authority stop feeling empathy and pity, and become blind to the effects of their Laws on people, then their greatness is abused and lost ‘. This is exactly the accusation Jesus is levelling at the Pharisees in today’s parable.

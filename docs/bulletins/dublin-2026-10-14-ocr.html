The Mass is therefore the greatest prayer we have. Because of this, the Church has always encouraged the faithful to ask that Mass be offered for particular intentions; for example, for the repose of the soul of a deceased loved one, for someone ’s recovery from illness, for a wedding anniversary, or for some other person or particular need. So, when you request a Mass for your deceased loved one, youare essentially saying: “I want the Church to offer the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass for them. ” When you request a Mass for someone who is sick, you are saying: “I want the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass to be offered for this person ’s healing and needs. ” When you request a Mass in thanksgiving, you are saying: “I want to join my thanksgiving to Christ ’s perfect offering of Himself to the Father. ” The Mass intention does not add some extra power to the Mass. The Mass is already the infinite sacrifice of Christ. Rather, the Church allows us to direct the fruits and prayers of a particular celebration of the Mass toward a particular intention. Sending a Mass card is a statement that we have arranged to have Mass offered for that person ’s intentions. Itisa beautiful expression of our love and concern for the person concerned.

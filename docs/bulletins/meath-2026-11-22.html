Honouring Christ, as King of the Universe On the Solemnity of Christ the King, Jesus describes the final judgment as a judgment of love.

The nati ons are separated according to concrete acts of mercy: feeding the hu ngry, welcoming the stranger, clothing the naked, visi ting the sick and imprisoned.

The astonishing truth is that Christ id entifies Himself with the least. What is done to them is done to Him.

Love is not proven mainly by words or feelings, but by deeds.

In the evening of life, we will be judged on love charity reveals whether we recognized the King hidden in the poor.

This Gospel asks us to meet Christ where He has chosen to be found.

Today, choose one work of mercy, however small.

The King of the universe comes disguised in need.

To serve the least is not optional kindness; it is reverence toward Christ Himself.

Le t this reflection become concrete today.

Choose one small act of faith, conversion, or charity that corresponds to the Gospe l.

The Lord does not ask for vague admiration, but f or a heart that receives His word and lets it take flesh in ordinary decisio ns.

We can begin simply: a prayer made with attenti on, a truth accepted without excuse, a person treated with mercy, or a duty carried out with love.

Grace becomes fruitful when it is welcomed in the present moment.

If the step seems small, it is still precious when offered to God, because daily fidelity is where the Gospel becomes a real path of holiness.

The important thing is to re spond today, with humility and trust, while grace is being offered.

Above all, cultivate your faith together.

Following the example of Christ, the Word made Ňesh, we can observe everyday reality with the wisdom that comes from the Gospel, opening up a future of righteousness for all.

This mission begins in prayer and is nourished by the sacraments.

The work of the whole Church is carried out in families and parishes, in the streets and schools, in hospitals and prisons.

Seek to multiply the Lord’s gifts everywhere, promoting communion with all men and women of good will.

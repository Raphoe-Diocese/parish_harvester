Reflection: Belonging Sometimes we discover what a community is when something happens that asks something of us. Over these past days, following the death of Father Halton, many people quietly did what needed to be done. People prayed. People prepared the church. People welcomed and organised. People provided music and hospitality. People looked after practical things that others hardly noticed. No one person could have carried everything. And perhaps that is worth noticing. A parish is not simply a place from which we receive things: Mass, the sacraments, schools, funerals, pastoral care. A parish is something to which we belong. And belonging asks something ofus. It asks us to notice what is needed, to recognise what we can contribute, and sometimes simply to be there for one another. These past days have been sad days for our parish. But within the sadness, something good has also been revealed: when something needed to be carried, there were people willing to carry it. That is something for which to be grateful. Anditis something upon which we can build.

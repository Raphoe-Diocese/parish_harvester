Reflection
Forgiveness does not come easy to us because we are not like our compassionate and loving
Father, ‘slow to anger and rich in mercy ’. Even in small things we find it difficult to forgive. We
can bear grudges for years against those we consider to have wronged us, to have let us
down, or to have treated us unfairly. This can apply to family members, neigh bours, friends and
colleagues at work or in school. We often nurture bad feelings towards them. We avoid people
with whom we have clashed or argued. We forget – or overlook the fact – that God continues to
forgive us over and over again.
Why does Jesus insist so much on forgiveness? It is because without forgiveness love cannot

Bhíodh ceithre fhéile mhóra ag na Ceiltigh.

Ceiliúradh a bhí i ngach ceann acu bainteach le hobair fheirmeoireachta.

Tráth na Samhna bhíodh na barraí uile bainte ag na Ceiltigh agus dóthain bia bailithe acudon gheimhreadh fada, fuar.

Bhíodh féasta acu le ceiliúradh a dhéanamh taréis na hoibre ar fad.

I ndiaidh teacht na g Críostaíochta go hÉirinn, rinne na Críostaithe a bhféilte féin a cheiliúradh ag an am céanna le féilte na g Ceilteach.

Sin an fáth a dtiteann Lá na Naomh Uile ar an 1 Samhain agus Lá na Marbh ar an 2 Samhain.

Bhí siad araon ag gabháil buíochais as fómhar torthúil.

De réir a chéile ghlac na Ceiltigh leis an g Críostaíocht Ceiliúradh buaatá i bh Féile na Naomh Uile . Déanaimid gairdeas leis na milliúin atá imithe romhainn ar neamh, roinnt mhaith díobh aitheanta ach na milliúin eile nach bhfuil.

Bíonn an Eaglais de shíor ag guí ar son na marbh.

Tá an mhí áirithe seo ceaptha aici mar chuimhneacháin na bh Fíréan uile atá marbh.

Suaimhneas síoraí tabhair dóibh a Thiarna agus go lonra solas suthain orthu.

Go bhfaighe anamacha na bhfíréan go léir trócaire ó Dhia agus cónaí faoi shuaimhneas.

Áiméan. The Month of November is here and we welcome it.

The Celts had four major feasts. They were farmers and each feast related to farming.

At Samhain the Celts had harvested the crops and they had sufficient food for the long, cold Winter.

They held a great feast to celebrate after their hard work.

With the spread of Christianity in Ireland the Christians celebrated their feasts the same time as the Celts.

This is the reason why the feast of All Saints was celebrated on the November 1 and All Souls on November 2. Each understood the importance of giving thanks for a rich harvest. Gradually the Celts embraced Christianity.

The Feast of All Saints is a celebration.

We rejoice with the millions who have gone before us to heaven, some recognised saints and many others who are not.

The Church always prays for the Dead. This month is devoted to the faithful departed.

Eternal rest grant to them O Lord and let perpetual light shine upon them.

May they

rest in peace

Survey for Columbarium Wall The parish pastoral council, along with the

parish finance

and the graveyard committees are exploring whether there is sufficient interest in installing columbarium walls in Burrane Graveyard and Knockerra Graveyard.

A columbarium wall is a permanent, freestanding structure.

Each wall contains recessed compartments called niches (instead of a plot), designed to respectfully hold 2+ cremation urns.

Many parishes install them to provide families with a dignified, long-term resting place for loved ones who choose cremation and as an alternative to burial.

A Litany of Remembrance In the rising of the sun and in its going down.

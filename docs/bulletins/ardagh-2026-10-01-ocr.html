He had just been to the temple in Jerusalem where, in righteous anger, he had expelled the money changers and those who were selling animals. His prophetic action was, implicitly, a condemnation of the temple authorities, whose way of acting failed to give due deference to God. That incident was followed by the symbolic cursing of a fig-tree. Itwastobe understood as a symbol of the Jewish people. They bore the outward leaves of religious observance, while failing to bear the expected fruit of repentance and obedience to the word and will of God. Asa result, they were cursed like the fig-tree to wither anddie. The curse came to fulfilment with the destruction of Jerusalem and its temple in AD 70.

PORTAFERRY AND BALLYGALGET BAPTISM MINISTRY Our newly commissioned Parish Baptism Team is a voluntary ministry that supports parents, families and Priests as they prepare to celebrate the Sacrament of Baptism. Central to their role is ensuring that every family experiences their Parish as a place of genuine welcome and encouraging faith within the home beyond the day of their Baptism. Our Baptism Ministers will help parents understand the meaning of the Rite and what it involves by: • Welcoming parents and children into the Parish community. • Providing a preparation session for parents (and Godparents if they wish to attend). • Offering guidance on the symbols, prayers and commitments involved in the Sacrament. • Helping parents participate more fully in the celebration on the day of Baptism. Join us for the inaugural Féile tours of St Peter’s Cathedral, led by Stevie Corr, as we celebrate its 160th anniversary! PRAYER FOR DIVINE MERCY SUNDAY O God of infinite mercy, on this Divine Mercy Sunday, we come before You with humble hearts, trusting in Your boundless love. Through the passion, death, and resurrection of Your Son, Jesus Christ, You have opened the floodgates of mercy for all humanity. We pray with the words You gave to St. Faustina: "JESUS, I TRUST IN YOU." Grant us the grace to accept Your mercy, to share it with others, and to live as instruments of Your peace.

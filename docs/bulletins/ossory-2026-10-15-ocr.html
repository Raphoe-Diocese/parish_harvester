He p rovides comfort, strength, and hope. We must accept His invitation with ready h earts. Challenge: Trust God in both plenty and need. Res pond to His call with faith. Live in a way that shows you are ready. Action Step: Welcome someone who feels left outthis week. Verse to Remember: “Invite to the feast whomever you find.” (Matthew 2 2:9) Sunday's Scriptures The Feast Is Ready God does not wait for us to be worthy before inviting us to his table. Through Isaiah, he promises a banquet forall peoples, where death is destroyed and every tear wiped away. In the Gospel, the king sends his serva nts to the crossroads: “Invite everyone you can find.” The feast is ready— but we must come clothed in the grace he gives, not in our own self- sufficiency. St Paul, even in prison, learned that Christ is enough: “There is nothing I cannot master with the help of the One who gives me strength.” At Mass, we taste that heavenly banquet. Let us accept the invitation, put on Christ, and bring others to the feast. Come —everything is ready. Major Restoration of the Church Tower In recent times it has become apparent that there some significant structural problems in the tower of Holy Trinity Church Durrow. The wooden Louvres (Slanted beams) on all four sides of the tower are rotting, and each is framed in steel. These present a significant health and safety risk. When the tower was examined earlier in the summer other major difoculties with the pointing of stonework were also identified. The upshot of all this is that some major restoration work is necessary and as a matter of urgency. The work is scheduled to begin on Monday 19th of October . The work will take approximately four to six weeks . From after mass this weekend the doors across the front of the church will remain locked and entrance to the church will be through the side aisle doors only. The front of the church and the tower will become a building site and therefore a health and safety risk. I realise this will present an inconvenience, but I ask for your consideration and cooperation. I am grateful to the Parish Finance Committee and the diocesan authorities who have worked with me to address this problem. Together with other necessary works to repair the Lightning conductors on the church carried out earlier in the year this major repair job will cost in the region of €90,000.

Saints in Disguise. Fr John Cullen, a regular contributor to The Sacred Heart Messenger , writing in this month's issue under the heading Saints in Disguise , relates the following encounter he had with a homeless man.

I met a homeless man in a soup kitchen a As we got talking, he showed me a crumpled bit of paper with a prayer written on it, which he recites every night: 'Keep watch, dear Lord, with those who watch or work or weep this night, and give your angels charge over those who sleep.

This prayer is attributed to St. Augustine (354-450).

I was surprised by the homeless man, as he told me that he offers the prayer, not just for himself, but for all who are homeless.

His prayer was not an expression of a privatised religion or an individualised spirituality.

It was an encounter with the core value of the dignity of every human being.

As we celebrate the feast day of All Saints this weekend, let us remain open to the surprising encounter of grace that may come our way in the most unlikely of places and people.

That is, those who reflect God's Beatitude as a blessing to the world.

Blessed are those who are unseen, unrecognised and unnamed - who are known by God.

Saints in Disguise! All holy men and women, pray for us.

November: A Month of Prayer for the Faithful Departed.

During the month of November, We are invited to remember and pray for our faithful departed.

It is a time to reflect with love and gratitude on the lives of those who have gone before us: our parents, grandparents, spouses, children, relatives, friends, and all those who have touched our lives.

May our prayers throughout this month bring comfort to our hearts, strengthen our hope, and bring peace to the souls of our beloved departed.

Remember me To the living, I am gone; To the sorrowful, I will never return, To the angry, I was cheated; But to the happy, I am at peace.

And to the faithful, I have never left. I cannot speak, but I can listen, I cannot be seen, but I can be heard.

So, as you stand upon the shore, gazing at the beautiful Remember Me As you look in awe at a mighty forest and its grand majesty.

Remember Me As you kneel in front of the Altar of God.

Remember Me in your Prayers, in your hearts, in your thoughts, and in the memories of times we loved, the times we cried, the battles we fought and times we laughed.

For if you always think of I will have never gone.

May the souls of the faithful departed through the mercy of God, rest in peace, Amen.

There are hopes we have not fulfilled, mistakes we cannot undo, and experiences that have changed us in ways we never expected.

Whatever our past, the invitation remains.

The saints remind us that there is still more to become.

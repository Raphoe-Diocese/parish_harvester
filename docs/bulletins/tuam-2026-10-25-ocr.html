SUNDAY 18th OCTOBER 2026 One in Christ, United in Mission This weekend, we celebrate Mission Sunday, a special day when Catholics throughout the world come together in prayer & solidarity to support the Church’s missionary work.

This year marks the 100th anniversary of World Mission Sunday, established by Pope Pius XI in 1926. To mark this centenary, Pope Leo XIV has chosen the theme “One in Christ, United in Mission.” In his message for this special anniversary, Pope Leo reminds us that the mission of the Church belongs to all of us.

Through Baptism, every Christian is called to share God's love & bring the message of the Gospel to others.

World Mission Sunday is an opportunity to remember the many missionaries who dedicate their lives to serving communities throughout the world, often in places facing great poverty & hardship.

Through our prayers, generosity & practical support, we can all play a part in their work.

Mission begins in our own everyday lives, through simple acts of kindness, compassion & care for others.

Together, we are one in Christ & united in mission.

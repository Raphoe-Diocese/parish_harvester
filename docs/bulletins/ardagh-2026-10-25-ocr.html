We are citizens of our various countries and yet at the same time citizen of God’s kingdom.

This comes with privileges and obligations for each one and therein lies the challenge.

There should be no conflict between being faithful Christian and being a good citizen of our countries.

When however, there is a sinful or unjust policy in any society, Christians are called to take up the posture of conscientious objection.

We should at that moment, uphold the dignity of our conscience.

Jesus’s answer to them, admonishes his adversaries and us to worry less about what is due to Ceasar, to pay attention to God and worship that is duehim.

The tax we must pay to God, if we are to belong to him Kingdom, is to offer our heart, our spirit, soul, family and the society God, in a purposeful, meaningful, God-fearing manner.

Again, the gospel text today, teaches us that, all exercise of authority and power must be evaluated in the light of God’s will and plan.

If Ceasar is subservient to God, then his laws and policies will be in accord with God’s will.

AMEN. Pope Leo’s Message for Mission Sunday 2026 If unity is the condition of mission, love is itse ssence.

The Good News that we are sent to proclaim to the world is not an abstract ideal; it is the Gospel of God's faithful love, which became flesh in the face and life of Jesus Christ. The mission of the disciples and the Church as a whole is to continue the mission of Christ in the Holy Spirit: a mission born of love, lived in love, and leading to love.

In fact, the Lord himself, in his great prayer to the Father before his Passion, after invoking unity among his disciples, concludes: "so that the love with which you have loved me may be in them, and I in them"(Jn 17:26).

Impelled by the love of Christ, the Apostles then went out to evangelize for Christ (cf . 2 Cor 5:14).

In the same way, throughout the centuries, multitudes of Christians - martyrs, conf essors and missionaries - have given their lives to make this divine love known to the world.

Thus, guided by the Holy Spirit, the Spirit of love, the Churc h's evangelizing mission will continue until the end of time.

THE MISSION OF THE CHURCH, OUR COLLECTIVE RESPONSIBILITY Today, we celebrate World Mission Sunday.

We set aside this day every year to think, talk and reflect about the mission which Jesus entrusted to his disciples and to us in Mathew’s Gospel.

Pope Francis once reflected that "today, every land is a ‘mission land’ and every dimension of human life is mission territory". This marks a major shift in how the Church views its role in the modern world.

Historically, "the missions" meant sending missionaries from stablished Christian nations to far-off countries.

Today, the Pope emphasises that the global landscape has completely transformed.

Several factors must have caused this; the collapse of Christendom, geographical and human boundaries.

On the other hand, Pope Leo chose the official theme for the centenary celebration of World Mission Sunday: “One in Christ, United in Mission”. Pope Leo emphasized that through baptism; every single person becomes a member of the Body of Christ and holds a unique responsibility to cooperate as one family to spread the Gospel.

He noted that evangelisation is fundamentally "always a work of love" There are two ways to be a missionary: we can give to mission by going and we can go to mission by giving.

Dear brothers and sisters let us be actively involved in mission.

Your Mission Sunday donation envelopes, for the promotion of mission in the church, should be returned as soon as possible.

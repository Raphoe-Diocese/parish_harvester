Limerick Diocese - Biodiversity It is November and getting cold and frosty.

Time to feed the birds - foods you Nyger seed (finches), general birdseed, fat balls, unsalted peanuts, meal worms or shopped fruit and some chopped veg.

Never use bread or any food containing salt, sugar or household condiments.

Use hanging feeders rather than flat bird tables, less risk of disease.

Do scatter some little food on a hard ground surface for ground feeders like Robins, Thrushes and Blackbirds.

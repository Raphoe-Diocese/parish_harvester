His change of mind reminds us that first reactions aren’t always the best. Second thoughts are worthwhile. It’s never too late todothe right thing. The world survives on the basis of people keeping their word. The destruction of our planet, the growth of division between peoples, the loss of confidence in institutions, the deepening uncertainty with regard to the future can all be traced back to broken promises. For every promise there is a price topay. The ideal for the Christian is to make promise and practice rhyme. When deeds and words are in accord, the world is transformed.

At some oftheold churches the pattern was observed on the feast of the patron. The Dies Dedi cationis of Derrynahinch may have been the Sunday after St. Martin’s dayanditwas accustomed then to connect the patter with the feast dayof St. Martin, to the exclusion of the little known but true patron, St. Mocheallog. A wooden statue of St. Martin (St. Mocheallog) was formerly borne in procession through Ballyhale graveyard on pattern day. After the patterns were discontinued, itlayfor several years in the ancient tower at the west end of Ballyhale chapel. Around 1874 it was transferred to the Museum, St. Kieran’s College where it still remains.

Maitiú -- 22:1-14 Sanamsin, thosaigh Íosa ag labhairt i bparabail leis na príomhshagairt agus le seanóirí an phobail; “Tá ríocht na bhflaitheas,” ar seisean, “cosúil lería rinne bainis dá mhac. Chuir sé a shearbhóntaí amach ag glaoch orthu siúd a fuair cuireadh bainise, ach níor mhian leo a thíocht. Ansin chuir sé searbhóntaí eile amach agus dúirt séleoaráleo siúd a fuair cuireadh: “Breathnaigh, tá an féasta ullamh, mo chuid mart agus mo chuid beithíoch beaite maraithe, agus chuile rud réitithe: tagaigí chun na baini se.”Achba chuma leo ach imeacht, duine acu ag obair ar an bhfeilm, duine eile ag ceannacht rudaí, agus an chuid eile acu, rugadar ar a chuid searbhóntaí, thugadar spídiúlacht dóibh agus mharaíodar iad. Tháinig fearg aranrí, chuir sé a chuid saighdiúirí amach gur chuireadar na scriostóirí chun báis agus gur loisceadar a gcathair. Ansin dúirt sé leis na searbhóntaí: “Tá an bhainis réidh ach an dream a fuair an cuireadh, níorbh bhfiú iadé. Marsin téigíse amach ar na sráideanna agus tugaigí cuireadh chun na bainse do chuile dhuine a chasfar oraibh.” Chuaigh na searbhóntaí amach ar na bóithre agus bhailigh siad ar casadh orthu, olc agus maith, go raibh láthair na bainise lán le cuideachta. Chuaigh an rí isteach ag breathnú ar an gcuideachta, agus chonaic sé fear ann nach raibh aon éadach bainise air. Agus dúirt sé leis: “Céard a thug isteach anseo thú agus gan aon éadach bainise ort?” Ach níor fhreagair sé. Agus dúirt an rí leis an lucht freastail: “Ceanglaítear a chosa agus a lámha agus caitear amach sa dor chadas é: is ansin a bheidh caoineadh agus gíoscán fiacal.” Mar glaoitear ar go leor ach ní thoghtar ach beagán.” Soiscéal an Tiarna. When he sent his servants out to personally remind the guests to come, some of the servants were mistreated, beaten and even killed by his prospe ctive guests! Quite naturally, the king was outraged. What does this Sunday’s Gospel say to us? I doubt that we have had the experience this story speaks of. However, we all have been disappointed and upset at various times in our lives. We may have been deeply hurt by someone or betrayed by another. Our natural human response istobe angry, hurt and disappointed – and rightly so. Hopefully, we haven ’t decided to kill anyone because of that! In these situations, we need to take time to calm down and deal withthe hurt, anger and disappointment. We need to acknowledge and feel the emotions. If we repress them, most likely the emotions will come back with a vengeance . But we can’t stop there. We also need to honestly acknowledge what we are experiencing but then with time to gradually ‘let these feelings go’ and forgive. If we nurse our anger and hurt, wearenot hurting the individuals who disappointed us, butweare hurting ourselves and those we love! Ifwearenot able to forgive, we simply need to ask God to help us. Maywe have the grace toask Godto heal the anger and hurt – and to free us!

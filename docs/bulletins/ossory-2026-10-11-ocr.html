Major Restoration of the Church Tower In recent times it has become apparent that there some significant structural problems in the tower of Holy Trinity Church Durrow. The wooden Louvres (Slanted beams) on all four sides of the tower are rotting, and each is framed in steel. These present a significant health and safety risk. When the tower was examined earlier in the summer other major difoculties with the pointing of stonework were also identified. The upshot of all this is that some major restora- tion work is necessary andasa matter of urgency. The work is scheduled to begin on Monday 19th of October . The work will take approximately four to six weeks . From after mass this weekend the doors across the front of the church will remain locked and entrance to the church will be through the side aisle doors only. The front of the church and the tower will become a building site and therefore a health and safety risk. I realise this will present an inconvenience, but Iaskfor your consideration and cooperation. I am grateful to the Parish Finance Committee and the diocesan authorities who have worked with me to address this problem. Together with other necessary works to repair the Lightning conductors on the church carried out earlier in the year this major repair job will cost in the region of €90,000.

The customary expression of the Sign of Peace in Ireland is a handshake. Text PORTAFERRY AND BALLYGALGET BAPTISM MINISTRY Our newly commissioned Parish Baptism Team is a voluntary ministry that supports parents, families and Priests as they prepare to celebrate the Sacrament of Baptism. Central to their role is ensuring that every family experiences their Parish as a place of genuine welcome and encouraging faith within the home beyond the day of their Baptism. Our Baptism Ministers will help parents understand the meaning of the Rite and what it involves by: • Welcoming parents and children into the Parish community. • Providing a preparation session for parents (and Godparents if they wish to attend). • Offering guidance on the symbols, prayers and commitments involved in the Sacrament. • Helping parents participate more fully in the celebration on the day of Baptism. May the time of preparation for Baptism be filled with peace, gratitude, and the loving presence of God in our families.

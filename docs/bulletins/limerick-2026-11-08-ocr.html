We have loved them in life, let us not forget them in death . St Ambrose 1st November Today we take time to remember those whom we’vemet on life’s journey and who have now passed on to eternal life.

We may be remembering someone very dear to us – a mother, father, brother or sister, a grandparent, a daughter or son, a dear friend or relation.and we may also call to mind people who walked alongside us in life’s journey for only a brief time, but who touched our lives deeply.we remember those who have died during the past year, and all our loved ones who have died. 1 November – All Saints Day All Saints’ Day, also known as the Feast or Solemnity of All Saints, is celebrated every year on 1 November.

On this day, which is a Holy Day of Obligation, we honour all of the Saints and ask them to pray for us.

Mass on All Saints Day 2 November – All Souls’ Day or more formally ‘the Commemoration of the Faithful Departed’ is a solemn celebration commemorating all of those who have died.

An indulgence, applicable only to the Souls in Purgatory, is granted to the faithful, who visit a cemetery and pray, even if only mentally, for the departed.

The indulgence is plenary each day from November 1-8; The plenary indulgence is earned under the usual conditions: Going to Confession, receiving Holy Communion and praying for the Pope's intentions (usually one Our Father and the Creed or Hail Mary).

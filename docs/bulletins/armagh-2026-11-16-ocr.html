Sign up for Gift Aid and use your Parish Envelopes Gift Aid allows Lower Creggan Parish to claim an extra 25p from HMRC for every £1 you donate.

That means your giving can be worth more to the Parish, at no extra cost to you.

The additional money comes from tax you have already paid or declared to HMRC, not from your bank account.

To be eligible, you must pay enough UK Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in the tax year to cover the Gift Aid claimed on your donations.

Using your Parish Envelopes helps us record your giving and supports the Parish community.

Prayer for World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims

15 November 2026 Lord, on this World Day of Remembrance for the Victims of Road Traffic Accidents we remember all those who have lost their earthly lives on our roads and all those who have been injured in body and in mind.

May those whose earthly lives have been lost as a result of road accidents be brought into the light of your loving presence.

We pray for those who have suffered injury as a result of a road crash; those who have suffered permanent injury, and those who care for them.

May the Lord strengthen us to support and comfort them; that they may find the hope and peace that comes from God.

Lord hear us.

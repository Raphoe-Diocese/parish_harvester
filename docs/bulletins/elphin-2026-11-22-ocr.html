Dd Christ the King For many people in our current secular world the supreme authority is the government.

That is the highest power they acknowledge although they may not admit that openly.

Some would say that Christianity is coming to an end.

The venerable Archbishop Fulton Sheen famous said that it was not Christianity that was coming to an end but Christendom.

Our current society, politics and culture is very much shaped by Christian principles and that may indeed be coming to an end.

But that does not mean an end to the Catholic Church or Christianity.

As Jesus himself told us (Matthew 28:20): “And know that I am with you always, until the end of the age.

For Christians the highest power is the triune God and God Jesus told us “all authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me ” by his Father (Matthew 28:18).

The Church has designated the last Sunday in ordinary time as the Solemnity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe . In the past kings were instruments through which God’s promises to King David were to be fulfilled.

This idea of the divine right of kings continued up until the Enlightenment and the French Revolution in 1789. Following that many monarchies were overthrown and governments, following Enlightenment principles, placed their trust in human reason.

Monarchies were imperfect but since that time we have seen the failings of liberal democracies.

The only source of perfection is Christ the King.

In today’s world as we seek to build a society based on justice, peace and fraternity, we have to look to the witness of Christ and his teachings.

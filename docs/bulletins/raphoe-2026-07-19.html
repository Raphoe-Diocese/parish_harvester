Why Sunday Matters
Our invitation to reflect on Sunday Mass is presented through the lens of Synodality - Communion, Participation,
and Mission - reminding us that the Eucharist is not iust a personal encounter with Christ but also a communal
experience that unites us all as the People of God. Mass is not just a personal act of devotion; it is a communal
act of worship. We go to God together. The Sunday Mass is where the Church is most visibly alive, 'and where
every member of the community plays an essential role. Each per son's participation is needed for the Church
to truly live.
'We are deeply thankful to priests, ministers and all those who gather to celebrate Mass. Special gratitude goes
to those involved in liturgical ministries, such as Ministers of the Word, of Holy Communion, Music Ministers,
Sacristans, and Altar Servers. E ncouraging the return of young people as Altar Servers is especially important
and should be actively supported by their families and parish.
(Irish Catholic Bishops' Conference, Why Sunday Matters, 2025)

PLENARY INDULGENCE
QUASQUICENTENNIAL [125TH ANNIVERSARY ], CATHEDRAL OF SAINTS EUNAN & COLUMBA , LETTERKENNY
To mark the 125th Anniversary of the consecration of the Cathedral of Saints Eunan and Columba, Letterkenny,
the Holy See has granted a Plenary Indulgence under the usual conditions. It may be obtained as follows:
The Plenary Indulgence may be obtained under the usual conditions by any of the faithful who, rightly disposed,
participate in the liturgical life of the Cathedral with the intention of gaining the Indulgence, beginning with the
Mass of Chrism on Holy Thur sday of this year and concluding with the feast of the Epiphany, 6th January 2027.
The same Indulgence may be obtained by the sick and others who, unable to leave their homes, participate
spiritually in the same liturgical services by electronic means.
The Indulgence may be gained under the usual conditions by all who piously come on pilgrimage to the
Cathedral in the aforementioned period with that purpose in mind.
The same Indulgence is extended to those who wish to obtain it by spending at least one hour in Eucharistic
Adoration at the St. Pius X Chapel of Perpetual Adoration in the grounds of the Cathedral.
To obtain an indulgence , the usual conditions of detachment from all sin, Sacramental Confession, Holy
Communion, and prayer for the intentions of the Pope must be met within 7 days.

The Meaning and Symbolism of the Brown Scapular
The Scapular is a symbol of Mary ’s protection. It is a reduced form of the Carmelite
habit. It involves ‘putting on Christ ’ in our lives and entrusting ourselves to Mary ’s
protection. It includes the idea of consecration to Mary. Consecration is properly an act
done by God, so that when we say we consecrate ourselves to God and to Mary, we are
principally stating that we freely want God ’s will to be done in our lives. We want the
Lord Jesus to rule all we say and do. The wearing of the Scapular is a sign that we
want the values lived out by Mary to be evident in our actions and dispositions.
